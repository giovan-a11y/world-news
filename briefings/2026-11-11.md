# World Top 10 - Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Hotter-than-expected US inflation knocked Wall Street and sharpened bets on a December Fed hike, as Nvidia's CEO scandal escalated, Russia and Ukraine returned to the table in Abu Dhabi and Trump signalled he would pause Iran strikes.

## 1. Hot US inflation hits stocks and lifts rate-hike bets [Economy]

**What happened:** US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October against 0.3% expected, with core prices up 0.3% against 0.2% expected. The S&P 500 closed at 7,520.93 (-0.38%), the Dow at 52,231.89 (-0.30%) and the Nasdaq at 25,065.87 (-0.74%), reversing Monday's rally; the 2-year Treasury yield jumped to 4.73%. A weak 10-year Treasury auction added to the pressure.
**Why it matters:** Sticky inflation makes a December Fed rate hike more likely, raising borrowing costs worldwide and pressuring tech valuations.
**Sources:** [Honolulu Star-Advertiser / AP](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/11/10/breaking-news/wall-street-falls-as-hot-inflation-fuels-fed-rate-hike-fears/), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3027683/stock-market-news-for-nov-11-2026), [Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/news/live-news/article-2-2246094-20261110)

## 2. Russia and Ukraine resume US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Trilateral talks opened on 10 November, the first since February, with US envoys Witkoff and Kushner, Ukraine's Rustem Umerov and Russia's Medinsky. The first session lasted about four hours; the main disputes are territory and security guarantees, and Russia objects to European participation for now.
**Why it matters:** Direct talks restarting after nine months is the first diplomatic opening in the war in some time, though no deal is near.
**Sources:** [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/us-attempts-new-peace-push-first-russia-ukraine-talks-nine-months), [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/world/europe/us-mediated-russia-ukraine-dialogue-to-end-war-kicks-off-in-uae/amp), [Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/russia-ukraine-peace-talks-abu-dhabi-putin-zelesnky-kushner-trump-20261110.html)

## 3. Trump says he will hold off Iran strikes but keep blockade [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** After talks with Qatar's prime minister, Trump said he would pause new strikes for now and keep the naval blockade, adding that Iran's military is effectively finished; Iranian officials dismissed that and its security chief called reports of talks fake news. Iran says no deal is possible unless the blockade ends, while Qatar has put forward a 48-hour plan pairing nuclear inspections with reopening the Strait of Hormuz.
**Why it matters:** Hormuz shipping and oil prices hinge on whether this diplomacy produces a deal or a return to strikes.
**Sources:** [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/gulf/trump-uae-qatar-saudi-arabia-iran-conflict-hormuz-blockade), [Dropsite News](https://dropsitenews.com/p/trump-says-iran-war-militarily-is), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/10)

## 4. Nvidia falls about 5.5% as CEO scandal grows [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia shares dropped roughly 5.5% as the board's independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang continued, a class action was filed and Senator Kevin Cramer called on him to resign. House committee chairs asked Huang to attend a closed-door briefing on 18 November; Nvidia reports earnings on 17 November.
**Why it matters:** The AI-chip leader's governance crisis weighs on the sector that has driven much of the market's gains.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/9/nvidia-shares-slump-amid-investigation-into-ceo-jensen-huang), [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/nvidia-faces-class-action-suit-over-huang-allegations-senator-seeks-probe-analyst-puts-ceo-exit-odds-at-15/cNGuT97R97g), [Motley Fool](https://fool.com/investing/2026/11/10/why-nvidia-stock-fell-again-on-tuesday)

## 6. COP31 climate talks under way in Antalya, finance in dispute [Climate]

**What happened:** The UN climate summit opened on 9 November, with the leaders' segment due 11-12 November and talks running to 20 November. UN chief Guterres urged leaders to "get serious", disagreements over climate finance surfaced early, and the US sent no official delegation.
**Why it matters:** Financing for vulnerable countries decides whether commitments become action, in a year forecast to be among the hottest on record.
