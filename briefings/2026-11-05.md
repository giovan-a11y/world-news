# World Top 10 - Thursday, 05 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Democrats swept Congress in the US midterms, sending the Dow to its best day of the year and oil sharply lower on hopes the Iran war will wind down.

## 1. Democrats capture both chambers of Congress [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats won control of the House and Senate in Tuesday's midterms, with NBC projecting 54 Senate seats and the party's largest House majority in nearly two decades. Notable flips include Texas, where James Talarico beat Ken Paxton, and Ohio, where Sherrod Brown returned. Over 124 million ballots were cast, a midterm record, and Hakeem Jeffries is set to become Speaker; Alaska and Mississippi were still too close to call.
**Why it matters:** Trump now faces a hostile Congress able to block funding, set investigations and shape the Iran war.
**Sources:** [WSOC/Cox Media](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/trending/election-2026-democrats-capture-both-houses-congress/5ILOWTVABVDPZFYP7VBBCX4EWE/), [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/4791815/takeaways-2026-midterm-elections/), [NBC San Diego](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/elections/election-day-after-2026-midterms-congress-results-live-updates/4096585/)

## 2. Vance blames Iran war for Republican losses; Trump defends it [Politics]

**What happened:** Vice President JD Vance said on 4 November that Republicans lost because of the Iran war and high fuel prices, while Trump said "I did the right thing" and promised vetoes. Jeffries said Trump should end the war before the new Congress convenes, and a $67 billion White House war-funding request is pending.
**Why it matters:** The result turns the Iran conflict into a domestic political liability and raises pressure for a deal.
**Sources:** [Argus Media](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2901877-trump-faces-new-limits-after-midterms-blowout), [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/international/us-midterm-elections-2026-donald-trump-iran-war-democrats-brent-crude-oil-prices), [CS Monitor](https://www.csmonitor.com/Daily/2026/20261105)

## 3. Dow posts best day of the year as oil sinks [Markets]

**What happened:** The Dow gained about 3% (one source says 3.03%, to 53,356.12) while the S&P 500 rose about 1.7% and the Nasdaq about 1.4%. December WTI fell 8.3% to $88.12, its first close below $90 since mid-September, and Brent fell more than 8% to around $94; energy shares were the weakest sector. Reported figures differ slightly by source.
**Why it matters:** Markets read the result as lowering war risk and favouring divided government.
**Sources:** [Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/dow-surges-after-democratic-midterm-takeover-600930), [Finviz](https://finviz.com/news/416102/midterms-helped-the-dow-score-its-best-day-of-the-year), [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-americas-market-news-wrap-oil-crushed-as-republicans-blame-war-for-loss/)

## 4. Hang Seng gains 1.67% as Asia reacts to US result [Asia]

**What happened:** Hong Kong's Hang Seng rose 1.67% to 23,919.50 after opening lower, while other Asian markets were mixed and the US dollar fell. Seoul and Tokyo markets had been volatile earlier in the week.
**Why it matters:** A weaker dollar and lower oil eased pressure on Hong Kong, which pegs its currency to the US dollar.
**Sources:** [Qatar Tribune](https://www.qatar-tribune.com/article/262667/business/asian-shares-mixed-as-democrats-retake-us-congress), [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/52982/business/stocks-and-dollar-drop-as-democrats-enjoy-big-early-wins/)

## 5. Iran: Vance says progress made as strikes continue near Hormuz [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Vance said Washington has made "progress" with Iran and Trump feels "pretty good" about a settlement, while Trump confirmed limited US strikes on Qeshm Island but played down a return to full war. Iran's foreign ministry said past experience ruled out optimism.
**Why it matters:** The mix of diplomacy and force leaves oil and shipping through Hormuz highly uncertain.
**Sources:** [Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/world/news.php?id=2629268), [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/middle_east/2026-11-03/us-strikes-iran-qeshm-island-hormuz-23348317.html), [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/commodities/more-on-us-strikes-on-qeshm-officials-confirm-attack-as-trump-plays-down-war-risk/)

## 6. Kremlin confirms Russia-Ukraine-US talks in Abu Dhabi [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Russia said it will take part in three-way talks with Ukraine and the US in Abu Dhabi on 10-11 November. Trump also claimed Ukraine agreed not to hit Russian energy targets (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It would be the first such round in months and the main diplomatic track on the war.
**Sources:** [FDD](https://www.fdd.org/overnight-brief/november-4-2026/), [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/us-attempts-new-peace-push-first-russia-ukraine-talks-nine-months)

## 7. Qualcomm and AI stocks wobble as chip worries build [Technology]

**What happened:** Qualcomm fell sharply after a weak forecast for smartphone chip sales, and AI data-center names were sold even as the broad market rallied. The weakness foreshadowed a much bigger tech sell-off the next day.
**Why it matters:** Concerns about AI valuations and regulation are spreading beyond a few stocks.
**Sources:** [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/nasdaq-100-falls-chip-stocks-slide/), [Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824439)

## 8. Congo's Ebola outbreak remains the second-largest on record [Health]

**What happened:** UK monitoring data to 1 November show 10,532 confirmed cases and 5,126 deaths from the Bundibugyo strain, which has spread to neighbouring countries including Kenya. WHO declared the outbreak in May.
**Why it matters:** It strains health systems in eastern Congo and keeps regional spread risk high.
**Sources:** [UK Health Security Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/outbreaks-under-monitoring-in-2026/outbreaks-under-monitoring-week-44-week-ending-1-november-2026) (single source, unconfirmed), [WHO](https://who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON618)

## 9. Gold, Bitcoin: gold near $4,000, Bitcoin in the high $70,000s [Markets]

**What happened:** Gold broke below $3,900 at the start of the week and Bitcoin struggled below $77,000, with a bounce above $81,000 after the election. Both swing with rate and war expectations.
**Why it matters:** Havens and crypto are moving with risk sentiment, not as clean hedges.
**Sources:** [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/gold-breaks-below-3-900-bitcoin-struggles-below-77k-as-markets-brace-for-a-deeper-downside-202611021017), [Panda Forecast](https://pandaforecast.com/crypto-news/bitcoin-midterm-bounce-chip-rout-liquidations-etf-outflows/)
