# World Top 10 - Monday, 9 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Nvidia's board opened a probe into CEO Jensen Huang and crypto sold off over the weekend, extending a week in which Democratic midterm gains hit AI stocks, while US-Iran strikes and Qatari mediation kept Gulf tensions high.

## 1. Nvidia board opens independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang [Technology]

**What happened:** On 8 November Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation after reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort, the same day he admitted an extramarital affair in an email to staff. Outside counsel led by a former Manhattan acting US attorney will run the review; the board says it has confidence in Huang, who stays CEO, and has "no reason to believe" secrets were compromised. Reports of a link to an alleged China-linked executive are unconfirmed.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the centre of the AI trade and reports quarterly results on 17 November, so leadership risk could move the whole market.
**Sources:** [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-board-jensen-huang-inquiry-directors-succession), [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/nvidia-board-opens-investigation-into-reported-foreign-intel-c5b93236), [Four Week MBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-nvidia-board-opens-independent-probe-9-days-before-results/)

## 2. US strikes on Iran's Qeshm island deepen Gulf tensions [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Trump confirmed new US strikes on Iran's Qeshm island in the Strait of Hormuz, calling them minor strikes on air defences, after Iranian state media reported missile hits. Reporting this week also described strikes and counter-strikes in the Gulf, with Kuwait among the places affected (details unconfirmed). The US naval blockade of Iran remains in place.
**Why it matters:** Fighting around Hormuz threatens a large share of global oil flows and the wider ceasefire effort.
