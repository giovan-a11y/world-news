# World Top 10 - Wednesday, 25 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Nvidia's surprise CEO reversal whipsawed global markets while Hormuz tanker attacks, new US tariffs and a failed COP31 kept geopolitics and trade on edge.

## 1. Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang and global markets lurch [Technology / Markets]

**What happened:** Nvidia's board voted to bring back Jensen Huang as CEO, about two weeks after he resigned over a personal scandal. Nvidia shares jumped about 31% on Monday and the Nasdaq had its best day since April 2025, before the rally cooled on Tuesday with Nvidia down more than 5%.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the keystone of the AI trade, and Asian chip markets (Taiwan, Korea) swung by 6-9% in a day. Internal and regulatory investigations are not over, so volatility is likely to continue.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-board-jensen-huang-as-ceo), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang-return.html), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-rcna610864)

## 2. Iran says tankers hit mines as Hormuz traffic falls [Conflict]

**What happened:** Iran's navy says four tankers have hit mines in and near the Strait of Hormuz, the latest two catching fire east of the strait on 24 November. Hormuz oil traffic is reported down about 40% and the US naval blockade of Iran remains in place after an earlier deal collapsed.
**Why it matters:** The strait is the world's key oil chokepoint, and Iran's claims are not independently verified. Indirect US-Iran talks via mediators continue but nothing has visibly moved.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/hormuz-oil-traffic-is-down-40-percent-are-iran-s-tanker-attacks-working), [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/commodities/iran-says-three-tankers-hit-mines-yet-oil-sits-near-a-three-month-low-as-supply-wins)

## 3. Oil sits near a three-month low despite Hormuz risk [Energy / Markets]

**What happened:** Brent is trading around US$88-91 a barrel and WTI near US$80, close to three-month lows, even as Hormuz attacks continue. Analysts point to oversupply and restarts such as Libya's Sharara field.
**Why it matters:** Cheaper crude eases inflation pressure, but analysts warn prices could jump again if talks fail or shipping is hit harder.
**Sources:** [Bloomberg](https://bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-24/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-nov-25), [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/24/oil-price-today-brent-below-89-supply-glut-hormuz/)

## 4. US readies new Section 301 tariffs on 16 economies [Trade]

**What happened:** The Trump administration is set to unveil tariffs after a probe into excess industrial capacity covering China, the EU, Japan, South Korea, India, Mexico and others. Rates and exemptions were not final, and a US-China truce runs to 10 January.
**Why it matters:** The duties replace tariffs the Supreme Court struck down and could strain relations with allies as well as China.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/24/trump-tariffs-china-affordability), [US News/Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-11-24/trump-administration-set-to-unveil-new-tariffs-after-probe-on-excess-factory-capacity-sources-say)

## 5. Anthropic shares fall below US$83 IPO price [Business / AI]

**What happened:** Anthropic's stock dropped below its US$83 offer price within days of its Nasdaq debut, trading under US$76 at one point. Reports cite a broad tech pullback, insider selling and worries about data-centre spending.
**Why it matters:** It is a test of investor appetite for AI valuations after a sharp sell-off in the sector.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-options-trading-ipo-price.html), [Investing.com/Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/anthropic-breaks-below-83-ipo-price-as-debut-rally-unravels-5047654)

## 6. COP31 ends with no climate pact [Climate]

**What happened:** The UN climate conference in Antalya closed after overtime without an agreement because of a standoff over fossil fuels. The EU rejected a draft that did not call for a transition away from them, and the US was absent.
**Why it matters:** The failure leaves countries without a shared roadmap and was described as a setback by the UN chief.
**Sources:** [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168855), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/22/nx-s1-6046195/cop31-climate-talks-close-disappointment), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/un-cop31-climate-conference-ends-no-agreement-fossil-fuel-debate-standstill/)

## 7. Israel kills senior Hamas security official; strikes continue in Lebanon [Conflict]

**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/israeli-air-strike-kills-senior-hamas-military-official-in-gaza), [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/middle-east/israel-pounds-southern-lebanon-amid-withdrawal-disarmament-standoff-3013865)
