# World Top 10 - Thursday, 12 November 2026

**Today in one line:** AI stocks rebounded on Wall Street while Nvidia's CEO probe deepened, as Russia and Ukraine held their first direct talks in nine months and Trump held off new Iran strikes but kept the blockade.

## 1. Russia and Ukraine hold first trilateral talks in nine months [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** US-mediated talks opened in Abu Dhabi on 10 November, with envoys Witkoff and Kushner present, Ukraine's team led by Rustem Umerov and Russia's by Medinsky. The first session lasted about four hours and the two-day round ended 11 November; Rubio said the talks looked "very productive" but urged patience. Territory and security guarantees remain the main disputes.
**Why it matters:** It is the first direct diplomatic opening on the war since February, though no ceasefire or deal has been announced.
**Sources:** [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/us-attempts-new-peace-push-first-russia-ukraine-talks-nine-months), [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/world/europe/russia-ukraine-resume-us-mediated-peace-talks-after-9-months/amp), [Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/russia-ukraine-peace-talks-abu-dhabi-putin-zelesnky-kushner-trump-20261110.html)

## 2. Trump holds off new Iran strikes but keeps the naval blockade [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** After meeting Qatar's prime minister, Trump said he would hold off on new strikes for now and keep the Hormuz-area naval blockade in place, claiming Gulf states prefer the status quo. He also said Iran's military is effectively finished, which Iranian officials rejected; Tehran says no deal is possible unless the blockade is lifted. Qatar has proposed a 48-hour, two-stage plan combining nuclear inspections with reopening the Strait of Hormuz.
**Why it matters:** The standoff keeps a major share of the world's oil trade at risk, with talks stalled between sharply different terms.
**Sources:** [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/gulf/trump-uae-qatar-saudi-arabia-iran-conflict-hormuz-blockade), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/9/iran-war-live-trump-says-war-effectively-over-iran-demands-surrender), [Dropsite News](https://dropsitenews.com/p/trump-says-iran-war-militarily-is)

## 3. Rebounding AI stocks lift Wall Street [Markets]

**What happened:** AP reported the S&P 500 up 1.6% and the Nasdaq up 2.2% on 11 November as AI-linked shares recovered from the previous sell-off; CoreWeave jumped about 26%. Breadth was weak, with more S&P stocks falling than rising. Other recaps give smaller gains, so exact levels are unconfirmed. The bond market was closed for Veterans Day.
**Why it matters:** The rebound followed the worst day for US stocks since 2025 and shows how sensitive markets are to AI sentiment.
**Sources:** [BNN Bloomberg / AP](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/2026/11/11/rebounding-ai-stocks-lift-wall-street/), [Alain Guillot recap](https://www.alainguillot.com/stock-market-recap-november-11-2026/), [TheStreet](https://pro.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/2026-11-11)

## 4. Nvidia CEO probe deepens, with lawsuit and congressional pressure [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia's board is running an independent investigation of Jensen Huang over reports he was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort, and the stock has fallen for several sessions. A class action has been filed, Senator Kevin Cramer called on Huang to resign, and House committee chairs asked him to confirm by 11 November a closed-door briefing on 18 November. Nvidia reports quarterly results on 17 November.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the AI-chip bellwether, so governance doubt around its founder-CEO weighs on the whole sector.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/9/nvidia-shares-slump-amid-investigation-into-ceo-jensen-huang), [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/nvidia-faces-class-action-suit-over-huang-allegations-senator-seeks-probe-analyst-puts-ceo-exit-odds-at-15/cNGuT97R97g), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3373825/nvidia-faces-defining-test-jensen-huang-probe-adds-chip-stock-turbulence)

**Why it matters:** Higher US rates raise borrowing costs globally and pressure risk assets, emerging markets and Hong Kong's dollar-pegged economy.

## 7. COP31 leaders' summit opens in Antalya amid finance dispute [Climate]

**Why it matters:** Funding for poorer countries is the central test of the talks, in a year forecast among the hottest on record.
