# World Top 10 - Thursday, 26 November 2026

**Today in one line:** An AI and chip-stock rout rattled markets from Seoul to New York, while Gulf shipping and Middle East conflict reports kept energy risk elevated.

## 1. AI and chip stocks drive a global sell-off [Markets]

**What happened:** Nvidia fell about 5.9% in US trade, and Asian markets sold off on 25 November, with South Korea's Kospi down roughly 6%. On Wall Street the S&P 500 and Nasdaq fell as much as 3.2% and 4.6% intraday but recovered most of the losses; the Dow closed up 0.33%.
**Why it matters:** Concentrated AI-chip valuations are now the main source of global equity volatility, and a second day of heavy selling shows how fragile sentiment is.
**Sources:** [Stock Titan](https://stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-25-2026), [MarketScreener](https://marketscreener.com/news/hong-kong-stocks-fall-on-wednesday-as-global-tech-sell-off-deepens-alibaba-slips-1-ce7851dbd18ef624)

## 2. Hot US inflation data keeps a Fed rate hike in play [Economy]

**What happened:** Reports on 25 November said third-quarter US GDP growth was revised up to 4.3% and core PCE inflation came in at 3.3%, above expectations (figures from a single summary; unconfirmed). Bond yields nonetheless fell, with the 10-year near 4.8%.
**Why it matters:** Strong growth plus sticky inflation complicates the Fed's path, and markets are debating hikes rather than cuts.
**Sources:** [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/september-2026-pce-inflation.amp), [Closing Look](https://cappnotes.substack.com/p/closing-look-wednesday-november-25)

## 3. Iran says two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz (unconfirmed) [Conflict]

**What happened:** Iran's Revolutionary Guard said two oil tankers struck mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz. In earlier, similar incidents the US military denied the IRGC's claims, and the US has said it cleared mines from the strait.
**Why it matters:** About a fifth of global oil passes through the strait, so any confirmed attack moves oil prices and insurance costs.
**Sources:** [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/25/headlines), [Euronews](https://euronews.com/2026/11/11/oil-markets-rise-as-investors-monitor-shipping-disruption-in-the-strait-of-hormuz)

## 4. Israel says it killed a senior Hamas security official in Gaza (unconfirmed) [Conflict]

**What happened:** Gaza's Hamas-run interior ministry and medics said Israel's military killed Mohammad Abuelouf, head of Hamas's internal security service, along with his wife and two children.
**Why it matters:** The strike tests the fragile state of the Gaza ceasefire arrangements and risks further escalation.
**Sources:** [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/25/headlines)

## 5. Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia continue (unconfirmed for 25 November) [Conflict]

**What happened:** Headlines on 25 November reported Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia's largest refinery and a Riyadh skyscraper. Saudi authorities did not confirm refinery damage in comparable October attacks.
**Why it matters:** Repeated attacks on Saudi energy sites threaten global supply and have already disrupted flights and events in the kingdom.
**Sources:** [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/25/headlines), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/world/article/346693/Yemens-Houthis-say-they-hit-Saudi-Ras-Tanura-oil-refinery-with-missile)

## 6. Libya vows retaliation after Ukrainian drone strike on Russian warship (unconfirmed) [Conflict]

**What happened:** The Libyan National Army vowed unspecified retaliation against Ukraine after confirming that a Ukrainian drone attack on a Russian warship docked at an eastern Libyan port killed two Libyan naval officers.
**Why it matters:** It extends the Russia-Ukraine war into the Mediterranean and drags a third party into the conflict.
**Sources:** [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/25/headlines)

## 7. US prepares broad new tariffs after Section 301 probe (unconfirmed) [Trade]

**What happened:** The Trump administration is reportedly preparing sweeping tariffs on China, the EU and other major trading partners after a Section 301 inquiry into industrial overcapacity.
**Why it matters:** New duties would hit supply chains and add to the market jitters over technology and trade.
**Sources:** [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/25/headlines)

## 8. China's Shenzhou-24 mission with first Pakistani astronaut (unconfirmed timing) [Space]

**What happened:** China is sending Pakistan's first astronaut to the Tiangong space station as a payload specialist on Shenzhou-24, the first foreign visitor to the station. Some headlines treated the launch as happening on 25 November, but other reports said no date had been confirmed.
**Why it matters:** It marks a step in China's effort to build an international human-spaceflight partnership separate from the ISS.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/pakistani-astronaut-to-join-chinas-next-space-station-mission), [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/human-spaceflight/pakistani-astronaut-set-to-become-1st-international-visitor-to-chinas-tiangong-space-station-this-month)
