# Asia Top 10 - Monday, 23 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Fallout from the Shenzhen APEC summit and a global chip rout set up a nervous Monday for Asian markets.

## 1. Asian markets brace for a chip-led rout [Markets]

**What happened:** Wall Street's Friday selloff, led by Nvidia (-14%) and chip stocks (-10%), lands on Asian chip-heavy markets such as Korea, Taiwan and Japan on Monday. Korea's Kospi had closed at 5,137.89 on 19 November.
**Why it matters:** Samsung, SK Hynix, TSMC and their suppliers are tied closely to the AI trade.
**Sources:** [StockInvest](https://stockinvest.us/digest/digest-us-market-close-2026-11-20), [InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=682734)

## 2. Takaichi gets a cold reception at APEC in Shenzhen [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** Japan's Prime Minister Takaichi, on her first China trip, was met by a retired envoy and had only brief contact with Xi. South Korea's President Lee received a warmer welcome. The freeze dates from her Taiwan remarks a year ago.
**Why it matters:** The China-Japan rift affects tourism, trade and regional security.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374944/xi-greets-takaichi-smile-no-words-china-japan-freeze-lingers), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/china/article/348928/Japan-PM-Takaichi-gets-muted-reception-as-Beijing-sends-retired-envoy)

## 3. Xi hosts Trump and Putin; nuclear-risk dialogue agreed [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** The three leaders agreed to open talks on nuclear risks, with no timetable, after the Shenzhen meeting. China and Russia also cited points on petroleum supply.
**Why it matters:** It raises China's profile as host and broker, with little concrete change yet.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374977/world-leaders-gather-talk-trade-and-investment-apec-chinas-tech-hub-shenzhen), [IBTimes](https://mx.ibtimes.com/trump-putin-xi-nuclear-dialogue-4574)

## 4. Indonesian factory fire: seven dead, 50 missing [Disaster]

**What happened:** Reuters reported seven bodies recovered and about 50 missing. (unconfirmed)
**Why it matters:** The toll may rise.
**Sources:** [US News / Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/world)
