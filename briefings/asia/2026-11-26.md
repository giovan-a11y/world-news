# Asia Top 10 - Thursday, 26 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Chip and AI stocks sold off sharply across Asia on 25 November, led by a roughly 6% drop in South Korea's Kospi.

*Editor's note: web access was limited today. Only a few Asia stories could be sourced responsibly, and market figures differ between sources; items are marked accordingly. I have not padded the list to ten.*

## 1. Kospi plunges about 6% on chip rout [Markets]

**What happened:** After Nvidia's 5.9% drop in US trade, Kospi fell as much as 5.3% early and about 6% later, to roughly 4,700, triggering a program-trading sidecar curb. It had risen about 6% the day before.
**Why it matters:** Samsung Electronics and SK hynix dominate the index, making Korea a high-beta proxy for the AI trade.
**Sources:** [Univest](https://univest.in/blogs/asian-markets-today-kospi-nikkei-crash-ai-chip-selloff), [Close Print](https://www.closeprint.com/markets/asia-pacific-indices)

## 2. Nikkei falls on the same chip sell-off [Markets]

**What happened:** Japan's Nikkei 225 reportedly fell about 2.6% to around 57,142, after gaining 3.9% on 24 November (estimates vary by source).
**Why it matters:** The volatility follows a more-than-5% one-day drop on 20 November, showing how violently Japanese tech names are repricing.
**Sources:** [Market Speaker](https://marketspeaker.com/asia-stocks-nikkei-kospi-softbank-selloff-november-20-2026/), [Close Print](https://www.closeprint.com/markets/asia-pacific-indices)

## 3. Shanghai drops 2%, ChiNext plunges 4% [Markets]

**What happened:** The Shanghai Composite fell about 2.03% to 3,476.47, losing 3,500, while the tech-heavy ChiNext dropped 4.19%. A second source put Shanghai near -1.7%.
**Why it matters:** The sell-off reaches China's tech and chip supply chain, with trade-policy worries adding pressure.
**Sources:** [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/9a41ae49-d3b1-466a-a4cc-47bfedc97df7), [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/global-economy/sse-composite-today-shanghai-edges-up-0-27-to-3-548-37-as-early-ai-rally-fades)

## 4. China's Shenzhou-24 to carry Pakistan's first astronaut (unconfirmed timing) [Space]

**What happened:** China said a Pakistani payload specialist will fly to the Tiangong station, the first foreign visitor. Headlines on 25 November suggested the launch was under way; other reports said no date had been confirmed.
**Why it matters:** It showcases China's space diplomacy with a close partner.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/pakistani-astronaut-to-join-chinas-next-space-station-mission), [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/human-spaceflight/pakistani-astronaut-set-to-become-1st-international-visitor-to-chinas-tiangong-space-station-this-month)

## 5. Follow-up: ICJ ruling on Myanmar and the Rohingya [Law, background]

**What happened:** On 19 November the ICJ ruled 9-6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the court's first such finding against a state. Reparations will be decided later; Myanmar had not responded.
**Why it matters:** The ruling is final and may shape other genocide cases. This is older than 36 hours and included as context.
**Sources:** [HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/11/19/world-court-finds-myanmar-committed-rohingya-genocide), [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/19/top-u-n-court-rules-myanmar-committed-genocide-against-rohingya/)
