# Asia Top 10 - Friday, 20 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Shenzhen hosted a historic US-China-Russia summit while Japan's weak growth and a global tech sell-off shape the region's markets.

*Editor's note: search coverage of Asia was thin today, so this edition carries only the stories we could verify.*

## 1. Shenzhen APEC trilateral ends with nuclear-risk pledge [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Xi, Trump and Putin met for about 55 minutes on the APEC sidelines in China and issued a joint statement saying nuclear war cannot be won, promising a dialogue on nuclear risk and non-proliferation, and urging UN reform.
**Why it matters:** China positions itself as host and broker of great-power talks, with implications for Taiwan, the Korean Peninsula and trade.
**Sources:** [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/trump-xi-putin-apec-meeting-9.7427718), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/trump-meets-putin-and-xi-ahead-of-us-russia-china-trilateral-talks), [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/politics/international-relations/apec/apec-live-banquet-brings-trump-and-putin-xi-and-takaichi-together)

## 2. Japan's economy contracts in the third quarter [Economy] (unconfirmed)

**What happened:** Dow Jones reported Japan's economy shrank 0.1% annualised in July-September, with energy-driven inflation and yen weakness cited. The Bank of Japan, which raised its rate to 1.25% in September, is widely expected to hike again in December.
**Why it matters:** Weak growth against rising prices complicates Prime Minister Takaichi's pro-growth agenda and BOJ policy.
**Sources:** [Morningstar / Dow Jones](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202611151480/japans-economy-contracts-amid-inflation-squeeze-update)

## 3. Asian equities track the US tech wobble [Markets]

**What happened:** Recent readings put the Nikkei 225 near 59,444, the Kospi near 5,138 and the Shanghai Composite near 3,578, after the Kospi fell ahead of Nvidia's results. Thursday's 2% Wall Street drop points to a weak open.
**Why it matters:** Korea, Taiwan and Japan are heavily exposed to AI-chip demand.
**Sources:** [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/asian-stocks-stay-mixed-as-kospi-drops-ahead-of-looming-nvidia-results-202611160634), [Business Journal](https://thebusinessjournal.com/dow-plunges-1101-points-ai-stocks-wall-street/)
