# Asia Top 10 - Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asian chip stocks bounce hard after last week's rout, China's export boom pushes its surplus past $1 trillion, and Indonesia's vice president signals he will step down.

## 1. Nikkei and Kospi rebound sharply on AI and chip stocks [Economy]

**What happened:** On 10 November the Nikkei 225 rose about 2.7% to near 60,400 and the Kospi jumped more than 4% to around 5,240, led by Samsung Electronics (about +5%) and SK Hynix (about +6%). The moves recouped most of Friday's near-6% drop. The Shanghai Composite eased about 0.3% to 3,654 in morning trade.
**Why it matters:** Analysts read it as a positioning reset after fears that Democratic gains in the US midterms could slow AI investment.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/nikkei-kospi-extend-rebound-as-ai-and-chip-stocks-surge-recouping-friday-s-rout/), [investingLive Asia-Pacific wrap](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-kosdaq-jumps-nearly-5-as-ai-rebounds-oil-firms/)

## 2. Indonesia's Vice President Gibran says he intends to resign [Politics]

**What happened:** The state secretary said on 10 November that Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka intends to resign, in response to developments around Constitutional Court and Supreme Court decisions on his education records. He asked to prove his documents in the State Administrative Court.
**Why it matters:** A vacancy would trigger a vote in the MPR within 60 days on a successor nominated by President Prabowo.
**Sources:** [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/11/10/breaking-vp-gibran-intends-to-resign-says-state-secretary), [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4004396-indonesia-vp-expresses-intent-to-step-down-presidential-official-says)

## 3. China's trade surplus tops $1 trillion for a second year [Economy]

**What happened:** Customs data showed October exports up 35.2% year on year and imports up 41.6%, helped by semiconductors (exports +137%) and a recovery in Gulf crude shipments. The October surplus narrowed to about $105 billion, and the January-October surplus reached $1.04 trillion.
**Why it matters:** The surplus risks fresh friction with the EU and US ahead of the Xi-Trump meeting at APEC.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/china-october-exports-beat-forecasts-imports-surge-41-6-as-trade-surplus-narrows/), [ITV News (AP)](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-11-10/chinas-trade-surplus-exceeds-1-trillion-for-a-2nd-straight-year)

## 4. Japan and Vietnam agree to deepen economic security and energy ties [Economy / Security]

**What happened:** The Star's ASEAN roundup for 10 November reported that Japan and Vietnam agreed to boost economic security and energy cooperation, and that Vietnam was among 13 countries that kept energy stability (single roundup: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Energy security is a shared priority for Asian importers amid the Iran-war oil squeeze.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/10/asean-news-headlines-as-at-10pm-on-tuesday-nov-10)

## 5. Manila prepares to host the ASEAN summit [Politics]

**What happened:** Philippine officials were making final preparations for the 49th ASEAN Summit in Pasay City, with Marcos due to inspect the venues.
**Why it matters:** The summit opens this weekend and is expected to be dominated by energy and the South China Sea.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/10/asean-news-headlines-as-at-10pm-on-tuesday-nov-10), [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/world/news.php?id=2631572)

## 6. Nvidia CEO Huang admits relationship with suspected Chinese-linked executive [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** In a message to staff, Jensen Huang admitted a romantic relationship with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive US officials reportedly believe was recruited by Chinese intelligence. Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation and said Huang remained CEO at that point. Nvidia shares tumbled and SoftBank, an AI investor, jumped 15% in Tokyo ahead of earnings on the same day.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia's governance risk hits its Taiwanese, Korean and Japanese suppliers and the US-China chip export debate before the Shenzhen summit.
**Sources:** [Morningstar / Dow Jones](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/20261109444/asian-equities-mostly-rebound-though-sentiment-remains-fragile-update), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/347978/Nvidias-shares-slide-as-chip-giant-probes-CEO-over-spy-claims)

## 7. Najib Razak returns home to begin house arrest [Politics]

**What happened:** Former Malaysian prime minister Najib Razak arrived at his Kuala Lumpur residence on 10 November to serve the rest of his graft sentence under house arrest, after a new High Court order replaced one the attorney general had sought to defer. He had been granted conditional clemency by the King in September, and the house arrest runs to August 2028.
**Why it matters:** The decision has already forced a resignation from a senior coalition figure and tests Anwar's unity government.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/10/malaysian-court-clears-former-pm-najib-razak-for-house-arrest-transfer), [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/?p=313850), [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/malaysia/2026/11/10/najib-arrives-at-taman-duta-residence-to-begin-his-house-arrest/242755)

## 8. Sonia Gandhi's memoir "Belonging" goes on sale in India [Society / Politics]

**What happened:** HarperCollins India released Sonia Gandhi's 544-page memoir on 10 November after Penguin Random House India pulled out in August over proposed editorial changes. The book covers family tragedies and Congress politics; a friend of the author said the earlier publisher backed away over criticism of the BJP and RSS.
**Why it matters:** The release revives a political fight over free expression and gives Congress a platform as it tries to regain ground.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/10/sonia-gandhi-tells-of-love-and-loss-as-india039s-congress-fights-back), [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle/books/sonia-gandhi-memoir-belonging-harpercollins-india-penguin-release-date)

## 9. Iranian delegation touts "indirect trade" with China at Shanghai expo [Economy / Diplomacy]

**What happened:** At the China International Import Expo in Shanghai, an Iranian delegation that included oil company representatives said indirect trade with China had grown since the war began, SCMP reported. SCMP also said Chinese shipyards have overtaken South Korea's in the surging LNG-carrier market (single-source: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** The Iran war is reshaping Asian energy flows and sanctions risk ahead of Trump's meeting with Xi.
**Sources:** [South China Morning Post China section](https://www.scmp.com/news/china), [Al Jazeera China](https://www.aljazeera.com/where/china/)

## 10. Taiwan's exports jump nearly 60% on AI demand; Taiex rallies on intervention pledge [Economy]

**What happened:** Taiwan's October exports rose nearly 60% year on year on AI demand, and the Taiex posted its second-largest daily point gain after an official pledge to intervene in markets, Focus Taiwan reported on 9 November. Local election campaigning is intensifying ahead of the vote.
**Why it matters:** Taiwan is the most concentrated beneficiary of the AI hardware boom, and swings in the Taiex now move regional sentiment.
**Sources:** [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/), [Taiwan News](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/)
