# Asia Top 10 - Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asian chip stocks bounce hard after last week's rout, China's export boom pushes its surplus past $1 trillion, and Indonesia's vice president signals he will step down.

## 1. Nikkei and Kospi rebound sharply on AI and chip stocks [Economy]

**What happened:** On 10 November the Nikkei 225 rose about 2.7% to near 60,400 and the Kospi jumped more than 4% to around 5,240, led by Samsung Electronics (about +5%) and SK Hynix (about +6%). The moves recouped most of Friday's near-6% drop. The Shanghai Composite eased about 0.3% to 3,654 in morning trade.
**Why it matters:** Analysts read it as a positioning reset after fears that Democratic gains in the US midterms could slow AI investment.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/nikkei-kospi-extend-rebound-as-ai-and-chip-stocks-surge-recouping-friday-s-rout/), [investingLive Asia-Pacific wrap](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-kosdaq-jumps-nearly-5-as-ai-rebounds-oil-firms/)

## 2. Indonesia's Vice President Gibran says he intends to resign [Politics]

**What happened:** The state secretary said on 10 November that Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka intends to resign, in response to developments around Constitutional Court and Supreme Court decisions on his education records. He asked to prove his documents in the State Administrative Court.
**Why it matters:** A vacancy would trigger a vote in the MPR within 60 days on a successor nominated by President Prabowo.
**Sources:** [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/11/10/breaking-vp-gibran-intends-to-resign-says-state-secretary), [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4004396-indonesia-vp-expresses-intent-to-step-down-presidential-official-says)

## 3. China's trade surplus tops $1 trillion for a second year [Economy]

**What happened:** Customs data showed October exports up 35.2% year on year and imports up 41.6%, helped by semiconductors (exports +137%) and a recovery in Gulf crude shipments. The October surplus narrowed to about $105 billion, and the January-October surplus reached $1.04 trillion.
**Why it matters:** The surplus risks fresh friction with the EU and US ahead of the Xi-Trump meeting at APEC.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/china-october-exports-beat-forecasts-imports-surge-41-6-as-trade-surplus-narrows/), [ITV News (AP)](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-11-10/chinas-trade-surplus-exceeds-1-trillion-for-a-2nd-straight-year)

## 4. Japan and Vietnam agree to deepen economic security and energy ties [Economy / Security]

**What happened:** The Star's ASEAN roundup for 10 November reported that Japan and Vietnam agreed to boost economic security and energy cooperation, and that Vietnam was among 13 countries that kept energy stability (single roundup: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Energy security is a shared priority for Asian importers amid the Iran-war oil squeeze.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/10/asean-news-headlines-as-at-10pm-on-tuesday-nov-10)

## 5. Manila prepares to host the ASEAN summit [Politics]

**What happened:** Philippine officials were making final preparations for the 49th ASEAN Summit in Pasay City, with Marcos due to inspect the venues.
**Why it matters:** The summit opens this weekend and is expected to be dominated by energy and the South China Sea.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/10/asean-news-headlines-as-at-10pm-on-tuesday-nov-10), [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/world/news.php?id=2631572)
