# Asia Top 10 - Saturday, 28 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Beijing's pledge of more state market support sparked a huge rally across Asian stocks, even as the US proposed new tariffs on China, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Vietnam.

## 1. China's State Council pledges more market support; stocks surge [Markets]

**What happened:** After a meeting chaired by Premier Li Qiang on 27 November backed a stronger role for state funds and Central Huijin's stabilization function, the Shanghai Composite rose 3.73% to 3,606 and the Shenzhen Component about 7%. Chinese chip stocks posted record one-day gains. No figures for the support were disclosed.
**Why it matters:** It shows Beijing stepping in after an AI-chip selloff.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/china-chip-stocks-beijing-market-support.html), [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/world/2026/11/28/china-stocks-surge-after-rare-midsession-pledge-to-steady), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/27/asian-stock-markets-rise-after-earlier-tech-rout)

## 2. Kospi jumps over 4% on stabilization-fund hopes [Markets]

**What happened:** South Korea's Kospi rose about 4.3% to 4,916 (reports range 4.1% to 4.3%) on expectations of a bigger state-backed stabilization fund. Seoul had earlier held back from deploying such a fund.
**Why it matters:** It follows a week of heavy losses in Korean chip stocks.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/27/asian-stock-markets-rise-after-earlier-tech-rout), [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/economy/20261111/taiwan-china-tap-market-rescue-funds-why-wont-korea)

## 3. Nikkei gains 2% as AI stocks recover [Markets]

**What happened:** Japan's Nikkei 225 closed at 58,711, up 2.11%, on buying in AI and chip stocks. Taiwan's Taiex rose 0.40% to 39,308 and India's Nifty 50 fell 1.07%.
**Why it matters:** Asia's tech-heavy markets track the swings in AI sentiment.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/27/asian-stock-markets-rise-after-earlier-tech-rout), [Asia Market Wrap](https://edgeconsultancykw.com/asia-market-wrap-27-november-2026/)

## 4. US proposes new tariffs hitting Asian exporters [Trade]

**What happened:** Washington proposed an extra 20% on China (possibly 40% if talks fail), 15% on Japan, and rates for South Korea, Taiwan and Vietnam (Vietnam 20%). Semiconductors, autos and steel are excluded. China warned a trade war serves no party.
**Why it matters:** It reopens trade uncertainty for Asia's export economies, though the plan still faces public comment.
**Sources:** [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/11/26/us-china-eu-tariffs-overcapacity/), [ITV News](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-11-25/us-announces-new-tariffs-hikes-on-the-eu-china-and-others)

## 5. Gulf attacks threaten Asia's energy supply [Economy]

**What happened:** Attacks on Saudi refineries and Hormuz tankers pushed Brent to about $94 to $97. Iran's Guards said tankers hit mines in the strait.
**Why it matters:** Asia imports most of its crude via the Gulf.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/brent-oil-wti-hormuz-middle-east.html), [RTE](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1127/1600877-world-oil-prices/)

## 6. Quad summit statement opposes coercion; Japan-Australia defence ties grow [Security]

**What happened:** The Quad leaders opposed unilateral attempts to change the status quo by force or coercion. Separately, Australia's defence department announced a new Australia-Japan defence communications arrangement signed on 28 October.
**Why it matters:** Indo-Pacific security cooperation is deepening while China-Japan ties stay frozen.
**Sources:** [Japan Prime Minister's Office](https://japan.kantei.go.jp/105/diplomatic/202611/1117quad.html), [Australian Defence](https://defence.gov.au/news-events/news/2026-11-27/australia-japan-strengthen-partnership)

## 7. China-Japan freeze persists over Taiwan remarks [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Xi and Takaichi exchanged smiles at the APEC banquet without formal talks; Beijing still demands a retraction. No new development found today.
**Why it matters:** The standoff affects trade, tourism and Taiwan Strait risk.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374944/xi-greets-takaichi-smile-no-words-china-japan-freeze-lingers)
