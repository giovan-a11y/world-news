# Asia Top 10 - Monday, 16 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Manila's ASEAN and Quad diplomacy leads into APEC in Shenzhen, with oil shortages from the Iran war and US-China rivalry setting the backdrop.

## 1. ASEAN summit opens in Manila amid oil shortages [Politics / Economy]

**What happened:** The ASEAN summit runs 15-17 November in the Philippines. Southeast Asia continues to bear the brunt of oil shortages from the Iran war, and ASEAN and China again failed to conclude a South China Sea code of conduct.
**Why it matters:** Energy security and the maritime dispute are the region's most pressing shared risks.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/asean-summit-whos-attending-and-whats-at-stake), [Pacific Winds](https://pcisguam.substack.com/p/pacific-winds-20261116)

## 2. Possible Quad leaders' meeting in Manila [Security]

**What happened:** Leaders of Australia, India, Japan and the US are expected to meet in Manila, the first Quad leaders' summit in over two years. As of 16 November, confirmation from all four governments had not been independently verified (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It would signal Indo-Pacific security coordination just before the Shenzhen summit.
**Sources:** [Asia Pacific Security Magazine](https://asiapacificsecuritymagazine.com/quad-leaders-expected-to-meet-in-manila-amid-push-for-indo-pacific-security-cooperation), [Pacific Winds](https://pcisguam.substack.com/p/pacific-winds-20261116)

## 3. APEC leaders gather in Shenzhen 18-19 November [Politics]

**What happened:** The APEC leaders' meeting is set for 18-19 November in Shenzhen. The White House says Trump, Putin and Xi will meet on 19 November on the sidelines, and Trump intends to raise Iran. The Kremlin earlier said the trilateral format was not final.
**Why it matters:** A first three-way summit of the nuclear powers could touch Ukraine, arms control and trade.
**Sources:** [Wikipedia: APEC China 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APEC_China_2026), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/14/shifting-iran-rhetoric-trump-leaves-mideast-limbo-path-ahead/)

## 4. Trump to meet Marcos; Japan seeks Takaichi-Trump talks [Politics]

**What happened:** Trump is set to hold a one-on-one with Philippine President Marcos in Manila. Japan reportedly sought a Takaichi-Trump meeting next week, reportedly over US defence spending demands, and plans to propose APEC language on supply-chain transparency.
**Why it matters:** Allies are being asked to pay more for security while China presses its regional case.
**Sources:** [Pacific Winds](https://pcisguam.substack.com/p/pacific-winds-20261116), [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/asean-summit-whos-attending-and-whats-at-stake)
