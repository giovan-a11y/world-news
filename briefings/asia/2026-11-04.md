## 1. Australia's central bank holds the cash rate at 4.85% [Economy]

**What happened:** Rio Times' Asia brief of 4 November lists Australia's rate at 4.85%, consistent with the Reserve Bank's first-Tuesday-of-the-month decision on 3 November (single source: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Rates this high keep pressure on Australian households and the currency.
**Sources:** [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/asia-intelligence-brief-wednesday-november-4-2026/)

## 2. China's military command down to two of seven seats after purge [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** The Rio Times brief says China's Central Military Commission is down to two of its seven seats, following a Communist Party plenum that expelled 33 members (single source: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** The scale of the purge raises questions about command stability in the PLA.
**Sources:** [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/asia-intelligence-brief-wednesday-november-4-2026/), [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/asia-intelligence-brief-tuesday-november-3-2026/)

## 3. North Korea denies US$6.3 billion crypto theft; Seoul probes a leak [Security]

**What happened:** Pyongyang denied a reported US$6.3 billion cryptocurrency theft, and Seoul opened a probe into a leak (single source: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Crypto theft is a key funding channel for North Korea's weapons programmes.
**Sources:** [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/asia-intelligence-brief-tuesday-november-3-2026/)

## 4. Indonesia's smoke toll reported at 3,560 [Disasters]

**What happened:** The Rio Times brief cites a smoke-related toll of 3,560 in Indonesia; Australia is sending firefighting aircraft to East Kalimantan from 6-12 November (toll single-source: unconfirmed).
