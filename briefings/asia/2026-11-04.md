# Asia Top 10 - Wednesday, 4 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Australia's central bank raises rates to an 18-year high on Melbourne Cup Day, Indonesia detains its former police chief over wiretapping, and Asian markets brace for the US midterm results.

## 1. Australia's central bank lifts cash rate to 4.85%, an 18-year high [Economy]

**What happened:** The Reserve Bank of Australia raised the cash rate by 25 basis points to 4.85% on Melbourne Cup Day, 3 November, in a 6-3 vote, its fifth hike of 2026. It cited hotter-than-expected inflation (4.3% in September), the Middle East war and higher oil prices, and sees inflation above 3% until late 2027. The statement dropped a warning of further increases, and three board members voted to hold.
**Why it matters:** Mortgage holders face another squeeze, and the split vote hints the tightening cycle may be close to its end.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/more-rba-lifts-cash-rate-to-4-85-in-split-6-3-vote-sees-inflation-above-3-until-late-2027/), [Market Index](https://www.marketindex.com.au/news/rba-hikes-interest-rate-to-4-85-but-a-split-vote-hints-the-end-may-be-near), [Reserve Bank of Australia](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/smp/2026/nov/overview.html)

## 2. Asian shares fall as tech is sold ahead of US midterm results [Markets]

**What happened:** Asian shares fell as traders sold tech stocks while watching the US midterm vote count, with AP reporting that Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 3.6% to 60,835.36, its lowest since early May, and South Korea's Kospi fell 3.2% to 5,466.83 in the following session. The Hang Seng slipped 0.2% to 23,489.88. Investors worried a Democratic Congress could tighten AI rules.
**Why it matters:** It was the first leg of a selloff that deepened later in the week and exposed how heavily Asian indexes lean on AI stocks.
**Sources:** [AP via WTOP](https://wtop.com/world/2026/11/asian-shares-fall-on-selling-of-tech-shares-as-markets-watch-for-results-of-us-midterm-elections), [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/05/asian-shares-are-mixed-after-us-midterm-elections-as-ai-related-stocks-sink-in-japan-and-south-korea/)

## 3. Indonesia detains three police generals, including ex-police chief Listyo, over alleged wiretapping [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** Indonesian police announced in a televised news conference that three senior generals, including former national police chief Listyo Sigit Prabowo, who had been named presidential chief of staff on 1 October, were detained as suspects in illegal interception of communications. Police allege they tapped the president and others in 2025-2026 via the force's Lawful Interception Management System. Authorities said the motive was not yet clear.
**Why it matters:** The case, which led Prabowo to sack Listyo the next day, exposes a serious rift in the security elite and the Prabowo-Jokowi alliance.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/indonesia-detains-3-police-generals-suspected-of-wiretapping-president-prabowo/), [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/?p=312458), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/indonesian-presidents-chief-of-staff-dismissed-over-alleged)
