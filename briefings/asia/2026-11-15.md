# Asia Top 10 - Sunday, 15 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A planned Trump-Xi-Putin meeting in Shenzhen and the ASEAN gathering in Manila frame the week, while Nvidia's leadership shock and a fragile US-China truce keep Asian tech nervous.

## 1. Trump, Xi and Putin set for rare three-way meeting in Shenzhen [Politics]

**What happened:** The White House said President Trump will hold a three-way meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC summit next week. A US official said Iran will be on the agenda, while Ukraine is not expected to come up.
**Why it matters:** It would be the first such trilateral among the three nuclear powers and could shape arms control, energy and trade talks across Asia.
**Sources:** [Anadolu Agency morning briefing](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-14-2026/4124855), [Wikipedia: Current events 14 November 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Current_events/2026_November_14)

## 2. ASEAN leaders gather in the Philippines for the 49th summit [Politics]

**What happened:** Leaders and delegations converged on Manila for the 49th ASEAN Summit and related meetings, with live coverage of arrivals and sideline talks on 14 November. The agenda is dominated by regional energy supply, the South China Sea and Myanmar.
**Why it matters:** The bloc is trying to show unity while members face fuel shortages and pressure from both Washington and Beijing.
**Sources:** [Philippine Daily Inquirer live updates](https://www.inquirer.net/495619/live-updates-asean-summit-2026-and-related-meetings-nov-14-2026/), [Al Jazeera Asia](https://www.aljazeera.com/asia/)

## 3. Philippines and Canada conclude free-trade negotiations [Economy]

**What happened:** Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that negotiations for a free-trade agreement with the Philippines have concluded, with signing expected in 2027. The announcement came during the ASEAN meetings in Manila.
**Why it matters:** It adds another Western trade link for Manila as Southeast Asian states diversify away from tariff-exposed US and China trade.
**Sources:** [Wikipedia: Current events 14 November 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Current_events/2026_November_14)

## 4. China rejects US Nvidia spy claims as trade truce called fragile [Politics / Tech]

**What happened:** China said Washington was "fabricating" espionage claims tied to an Nvidia executive to justify a tech "blockade". Analysts quoted by regional media described the trade truce reached after the Xi-Trump summit as still fragile.
**Why it matters:** Chip export controls are a central test for the truce just before the Shenzhen summit.
**Sources:** [South China Morning Post China section](https://www.scmp.com/news/china), [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/13/asian-shares-are-mixed-as-investors-assess-impact-of-nvidias-fallout-on-global-ai-development/)

## 5. Nvidia CEO exit rattles Asian chip stocks [Economy / Tech]

**What happened:** Asian shares were mixed to lower on Friday after Jensen Huang's exit as Nvidia CEO, with SK Hynix down about 3% and the Kospi off about 1% in AP's report. Nvidia fell more than 4% on Wall Street, and analysts linked worries to possible tighter US chip export rules.
**Why it matters:** Korea, Taiwan and Japan are the core of the AI hardware supply chain, so Nvidia's leadership uncertainty feeds straight into their index levels.
**Sources:** [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/13/asian-shares-are-mixed-as-investors-assess-impact-of-nvidias-fallout-on-global-ai-development/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/asian-stocks-steady-as-nikkei-gains-1-4-hang-seng-slips-on-nvidia-leadership-fallout/)

## 6. COMAC's CE-25A electric aircraft completes first piloted flight [Tech]

**What happened:** COMAC's CE-25A, described as China's first domestically developed fixed-wing electric commercial aircraft, completed its first piloted test flight in Binzhou, Shandong, on 14 November. Separately, China released a plan to accelerate new energy storage during the 2026-2030 Five-Year Plan period.
**Why it matters:** It is a symbolic step in China's push into low-altitude and electric aviation and in domestic industrial self-reliance.
**Sources:** [AeroTime](https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/chinas-comac-completes-first-piloted-flight-of-ce-25a-electric-aircraft), [South China Morning Post China section](https://www.scmp.com/news/china)

## 7. Umno sweeps Melaka state election [Politics]

**What happened:** Malaysia's Umno reportedly won 24 of 28 seats in the Melaka state election (single-source roundup: unconfirmed). The same roundup said former prime minister Najib Razak, under house arrest since 11 November, was reported at Friday prayers at Kajang Prison.
**Why it matters:** A strong state result strengthens Umno's hand within the national unity coalition and fuels talk of early elections.
**Sources:** [Anadolu Agency morning briefing](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-14-2026/4124855), [Wikipedia: Current events 14 November 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Current_events/2026_November_14)

## 8. Philippines wins first Eurovision Song Contest Asia in Bangkok [Society]

**What happened:** The Philippines won the inaugural Eurovision Song Contest Asia held in Bangkok, with "Sobra-Sobra" performed by Carmelle.
**Why it matters:** The launch of a pan-Asian contest is a major cultural-broadcast event, and the win comes as Manila hosts regional leaders.
**Sources:** [Wikipedia: Eurovision Song Contest Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_Asia), [Wikipedia: Current events 14 November 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Current_events/2026_November_14)

## 9. Bangladesh garment wage protests shut dozens of factories [Society / Economy]

**What happened:** Wage protests by garment workers forced the shutdown of 55 units and halted production at 147 others, and employers threatened indefinite closures (single-source roundup: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Bangladesh's export economy depends on garments, and prolonged stoppages would hit global brands and fragile state finances.
**Sources:** [Anadolu Agency morning briefing](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-14-2026/4124855), [Al Jazeera Asia](https://www.aljazeera.com/asia/)

## 10. Japan beat Brazil 3-2; South Korea lose to Saudi Arabia [Sports]

**What happened:** In friendlies on 14 November, Japan beat Brazil 3-2 in Singapore, and South Korea, ranked 32nd, lost 3-2 to 55th-ranked Saudi Arabia. In cricket, New Zealand beat India by 244 runs in the fourth of five matches, leaving the series at 2-1 to India.
**Why it matters:** The results give Japan a high-profile win over a top-ranked side and raise questions in Seoul, while the cricket series heads to a decider.
**Sources:** [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/sports/2026/11/15/japan-beats-brazil-3-2-as-korea-falls-to-saudi-arabia), [Anadolu Agency morning briefing](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-14-2026/4124855)
