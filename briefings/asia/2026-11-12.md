# Asia Top 10 - Thursday, 12 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Indonesia's vice president resigns over a diploma dispute while Asian chip stocks extend their rebound and Washington and Beijing finalise the Shenzhen summit.

## 1. Indonesia's Vice President Gibran resigns amid eligibility dispute [Politics]

**What happened:** Reports on 11 November said Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka submitted his resignation to President Prabowo, after a presidential spokesperson said on 10 November that he intended to step down over court decisions on his education records. Bloomberg reported, citing people with knowledge, that Prabowo accepted it; the spokesperson did not confirm acceptance. Gibran said he would keep defending his credentials in the State Administrative Court.
**Why it matters:** The MPR must convene within 60 days to choose a successor from two candidates nominated by Prabowo, raising political uncertainty in Southeast Asia's largest economy.
**Sources:** [Tribune (Pakistan)](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2639789/indonesias-vice-president-has-resigned-aide-says-stoking-political-uncertainty), [New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/world/region/2026/11/1575730/indonesias-gibran-quits-vice-president-amid-eligibility-controversy), [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/11/10/breaking-vp-gibran-intends-to-resign-says-state-secretary)

## 2. Nikkei and Kospi extend rebound on AI and chip stocks [Economy]

**What happened:** On 11 November the Nikkei 225 rose for a third straight session to about 61,190 (up roughly 1.2%) and the Kospi gained about 1.7% to near 5,330, with Samsung and SK Hynix leading. KOSDAQ jumped nearly 5% at midday. The Shanghai Composite eased about 0.2%. The gains recoup most of the sell-off after the US midterms.
**Why it matters:** Analysts describe the move as a positioning reset rather than a trend change; Nvidia's results next week are the next test.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/asia-midday-nikkei-up-for-third-day-kosdaq-jumps-5-as-ai-stocks-rebound/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/nikkei-kospi-extend-rebound-as-ai-and-chip-stocks-surge-recouping-friday-s-rout/)

## 3. Rubio and Wang Yi finalise Trump-Xi Shenzhen meeting [Politics]

**What happened:** US Secretary of State Rubio held a phone call with Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi to finalise preparations for next week's Trump-Xi meeting in Shenzhen on the APEC sidelines. Beijing has said discussions on a possible Xi-Trump-Putin meeting are under way, but no agenda is fixed.
**Why it matters:** Expected deliverables are limited, with AI cooperation and an extension of the trade truce among the reported topics.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374120/rubio-wang-hold-phone-call-trump-xi-meeting-next-week), [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261107/china-confirms-discussions-underway-on-historic-xi-trump-putin-meeting)

## 4. Philippines readies the 49th ASEAN Summit [Politics]

**What happened:** President Marcos conducted a final inspection of ASEAN summit venues, and Philippine Airlines announced flight cancellations and reschedulings from 14 to 18 November to handle delegate arrivals. A US envoy said no announcement on "Pax Silica" would come during the summit.
**Why it matters:** The summit, with the South China Sea and energy security on the agenda, opens in Manila this weekend.
**Sources:** [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/world/news.php?id=2631572), [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/category/home-page/page/1460/')

## 5. South Korea: justice minister nominee on charges against the president [Politics]

**What happened:** Justice minister nominee Lee Yong-cheol said it is time to focus on uncovering the truth in the criminal cases against President Lee Jae-myung, without ruling out dropping the charges. Lee also dined with Democratic Party leader Kim Min-seok on 11 November (single-source: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** The handling of the president's cases is a continuing political flashpoint in Seoul.
**Sources:** [Herald Corp](https://mbiz.heraldcorp.com/article/10933250), [Kyunghyang Shinmun](https://www.khan.co.kr/en/article/202611112233007)
