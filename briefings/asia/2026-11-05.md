# Asia Top 10 - Thursday, 5 November 2026

**Today in one line:** North Korea fires more missiles and Asian equities slide as the Democrats' midterm sweep hits AI and chip stocks, while Indonesia's president fires his chief of staff over wiretapping.

## 1. North Korea fires multiple missiles toward the East Sea [Security]

**What happened:** South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said North Korea fired multiple missiles from the Sondok area on 4 November, the fourth weapons launch in a month, days after nearly a dozen short-range ballistic missiles were launched near Wonsan. The JCS called them missiles rather than confirmed ballistic missiles, which is unusual. State media later said Pyongyang tested a new air-defence system and a long-range cruise missile.
**Why it matters:** The run of tests raises pressure on Seoul, Tokyo and Washington while Pyongyang keeps rejecting talks.
**Sources:** [NewKerala](https://newkerala.com/news/a/north-korea-fires-multiple-missiles-toward-east-sea-214.htm), [The Star (Reuters)](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/11/04/north-korea-fires-multiple-missiles-in-fourth-weapons-launch-in-a-month-seoul-says), [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/asia_pacific/2026-11-05/north-korea-missile-technology-diplomacy-23369224.html)

## 2. Asian stocks fall after US midterms; AI names hit [Markets]

**What happened:** Asian markets fell on 4 November as US midterm results came in, with the Kospi down about 3.25% and the Nikkei dropping as much as 4.8% intraday (130 of 225 Nikkei stocks fell), according to separate reports whose figures differ. Selling was tied to fears that Democratic gains would bring tighter AI regulation and slow data-centre buildout.
**Why it matters:** It was the start of a sell-off that deepened later in the week.
**Sources:** [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/global-markets/asian-market-rotation-after-us-midterms-elections), [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/trading/asian-markets-tumble-nikkei-225-crashes-3-000-points-kospi-tanks-4-amid-us-midterm-vote-count-ai-sell-off/article-202952/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-stocks-slide-as-trump-slammed-in-mid-terms-loss/)

## 3. Democrats win US midterms; Beijing and Tokyo react [Politics]

**What happened:** CNN projected Democrats winning more than 250 House seats and also taking the Senate. China's Foreign Ministry said in its 4 November briefing that Beijing stands ready to continue working with the US, and spokesperson Mao Ning called the vote an internal US affair. Japan's Chief Cabinet Secretary Kihara said the Japan-US alliance remains the cornerstone of Japan's policy.
**Why it matters:** A Democratic Congress could change US tariff, chip and defence-burden policy toward Asia ahead of the APEC summit.
**Sources:** [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/05/politics/live-news/midterm-election-congress), [China Foreign Ministry briefing 4 Nov](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/eng/xw/fyrbt/lxjzh/202611/t20261104_12053240.html), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/04/2026-midterm-election-results-world-leaders-react)

## 4. Indonesia's Prabowo fires chief of staff over alleged wiretapping [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** President Prabowo dismissed his chief of staff, former national police chief Listyo Sigit Prabowo, on 4 November, a day after police detained him and two other senior officers over alleged illegal wiretapping of the president and others. The presidential office said the conduct could amount to treason, troops were deployed, and Lt Gen Ariyo Windutomo was named acting chief of staff.
**Why it matters:** Analysts link the affair to a rift with former president Joko Widodo, raising political risk in Southeast Asia's largest economy.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/indonesian-presidents-chief-of-staff-dismissed-over-alleged), [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/politics/article/3373235/indonesias-prabowo-sacks-chief-staff-wiretap-scandal-tests-alliance-widodo), [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/indonesia-deploys-thousands-of-troops-after-generals-arrested-over-wiretapping-allegations/)

## 5. Australia's central bank lifts cash rate to 4.85%, an 18-year high [Economy]

**What happened:** The Reserve Bank of Australia raised the cash rate by 25 basis points to 4.85% on 3 November in a 6-3 vote, its fifth hike of 2026, citing the Middle East war, higher oil prices and inflation of 4.3% in September. It forecast inflation above 3% until late 2027, and the statement dropped a warning about further increases.
**Why it matters:** Mortgage holders face another squeeze, and the split vote hints the tightening cycle may be near its end.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/more-rba-lifts-cash-rate-to-4-85-in-split-6-3-vote-sees-inflation-above-3-until-late-2027/), [Reserve Bank of Australia](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/smp/2026/nov/overview.html)
