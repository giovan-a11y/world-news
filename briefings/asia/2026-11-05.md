# Asia Top 10 - Thursday, 5 November 2026

**Today in one line:** North Korea fires more missiles and Asian equities slide as US midterm results hit AI and chip stocks.

## 1. North Korea fires multiple missiles toward the East Sea [Security]

**What happened:** South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said North Korea fired multiple missiles from the Sondok area on 4 November, days after nearly a dozen short-range ballistic missiles were launched near Wonsan. The JCS called them missiles rather than confirmed ballistic missiles, which is unusual. State media said Pyongyang also test-fired a newly designed air-defence missile system on 4 November.
**Why it matters:** The launches continue a run of tests that raises pressure on Seoul, Tokyo and Washington.
**Sources:** [NewKerala](https://newkerala.com/news/a/north-korea-fires-multiple-missiles-toward-east-sea-214.htm), [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/asia_pacific/2026-11-05/north-korea-missile-technology-diplomacy-23369224.html)

## 2. Asian stocks fall after US midterms; AI names hit [Economy]

**What happened:** Asian markets fell after US midterm results, with the Kospi down about 3.25% and the Nikkei dropping as much as 4.8% intraday on 4 November (130 of 225 Nikkei stocks fell). Reports tied the selling to fears that tighter AI regulation under Democratic gains would hurt semiconductor stocks.
**Why it matters:** It was the start of a sell-off that deepened later in the week.
**Sources:** [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/global-markets/asian-market-rotation-after-us-midterms-elections), [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/trading/asian-markets-tumble-nikkei-225-crashes-3-000-points-kospi-tanks-4-amid-us-midterm-vote-count-ai-sell-off/article-202952/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/nikkei-drops-3-as-democratic-midterm-gains-hit-ai-and-chip-stocks/)
