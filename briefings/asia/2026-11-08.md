# Asia Top 10 - Sunday, 8 November 2026

**Today in one line:** New Zealand's election ends without a clear winner and Asia reels from a brutal AI-stock selloff that sent the Kospi and Nikkei down about 6% in a day.

## 1. New Zealand election leaves no clear winner as leaders prepare for weeks of talks [Politics]

**What happened:** After Saturday's vote, AP reported that neither the right nor the left secured a decisive win, with 131 seats elected and 66 needed for a majority. Luxon's National-led coalition appeared to be close to the slimmest possible return to power, by one seat on election night, with National and Labour both near 29% of the party vote; ACT won 13 seats, NZ First 12 and the Opportunity Party seven. Seat totals differ between outlets and are provisional.
**Why it matters:** A hung result means weeks of coalition bargaining, with a fresh vote possible if no government can be formed.
**Sources:** [AP via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/08/world/new-zealanders-wont-know-for-weeks-who-won-election/), [NPR via OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/08/no-clear-winner-in-new-zealand-election-as-leaders-prepare-for-weeks-of-talks/), [The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/politics/world/new-zealand-election-results-vote-counting-continues-as-chris-luxon-says-goodbye-to-key-ministers-c-23130739)

## 2. Kospi and Nikkei plunge about 6% in worst AI-led rout in months [Markets]

**What happened:** On Friday the Kospi closed down 6.02% at 5,059.69, its lowest close since March, after plunging as much as 8.5% intraday and triggering a trading halt. The Nikkei 225 lost 5.96% and briefly sat about 20% below its June peak. The selloff followed a roughly $3 trillion rout in US tech shares after Democratic gains in the US midterms raised fears over AI data-centre rules.
**Why it matters:** Korea and Japan are the most AI-exposed large markets in Asia, and a sell-off of this size tests margin lending and retail positioning.
**Sources:** [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/06/kospi-crash-trading-halt-november-6/), [SBS News](https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008834281), [Business Standard / newsonair](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/all-major-asian-indices-fall-japans-nikkei-plunges-6-south-koreas-kospi-lost-3-79/)

## 3. AI stocks halt worldwide selloff as Wall Street trims weekly loss [Markets]

**What happened:** Wall Street rebounded Friday, with the Nasdaq up about 1.1% to 24,579.42 and the S&P 500 up 0.31%, though the Nasdaq still fell 3.37% for the week, its fourth straight weekly loss. The Dow had plunged 3.44% on Thursday. Analysts questioned whether the bounce would hold into the new week.
**Why it matters:** Asian markets did not share in Friday's bounce, so Monday's open is the next test of the AI trade.
**Sources:** [Korea Times (AP)](https://koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261107/ai-stocks-halt-brutal-worldwide-sell-off-as-wall-street-trims-its-loss-for-the-week), [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824942)

## 4. South Korea's opposition demands Lee apologise for Kospi plunge [Politics]

**What happened:** South Korea's opposition demanded that President Lee Jae Myung apologise for the Kospi plunge, Seoul Economic Daily reported on 8 November. SBS dubbed the 6% drop a "Blue Wave Shock" tied to the US midterm results, and Kospi barely defended the 5,000 level after breaching it intraday.
**Why it matters:** The market rout, after a long run-up in Korean shares, is turning into a political attack line against the government.
**Sources:** [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/politics/2026/11/08/opposition-demands-lee-apologize-for-kospi-plunge), [SBS News](https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008834281)

## 5. Taiwan authorises market intervention after Taiex falls 4.5% [Markets]

**What happened:** Taiwan's stabilisation fund committee authorised market intervention on Friday after the Taiex tumbled 1,873 points, or 4.51%, to close below 40,000, with foreign institutions heavy sellers. It was a 5.67% two-day drop on worries about heavy AI spending.
**Why it matters:** The fund has been activated only a handful of times since 2000, a sign of how seriously Taipei treats the AI-driven swings.
**Sources:** [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611060020), [Silicon Report](https://www.siliconreport.com/taiwan-activates-market-stabilization-measures-to-stem-ai-selloff)

## 6. China pledges stock-market stabilisation; GE signs $12.9 billion at import expo [Economy] (unconfirmed)

**What happened:** A China business digest said the State Council pledged improved policy mechanisms to stabilise stock markets after another selloff, with CICC and China Galaxy Securities deploying funds under the third round of the central bank's securities swap facility. It also reported GE Aerospace signed record sales and service agreements worth more than $12.9 billion during the China International Import Expo in Shanghai. I could not confirm either item with a second source.
**Why it matters:** Beijing is moving to cushion equity volatility while showcasing trade openness before APEC in Shenzhen.
**Sources:** [City News Service: Weekend Buzz 7-8 Nov](https://www.citynewsservice.cn/articles/china-biz-buzz/daily-buzz/weekend-buzz-7-8-november-2026-3n70vqym), [China This Week newsletter](https://shenshiweioffical.substack.com/p/china-this-week-five-things-you-need-a31)

## 7. Delhi building collapse: six dead, owner and contractors arrested [Disaster]

**What happened:** Police said on Saturday the death toll in the 5 November collapse of a three-storey under-construction building at Chawri Bazar in Old Delhi had reached six, and the owner and three contractors were arrested. Rescue teams from the NDRF, fire services and police continued searching, and an MCD official said no building plan had been sanctioned.
**Why it matters:** The case highlights illegal construction in India's crowded old city areas and could prompt a crackdown.
**Sources:** [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/death-toll-in-delhi-building-collapse-rises-to-six-4-arrested-3571422/), [The Week (PTI)](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2026/11/06/rescue-efforts-enter-2nd-day-at-old-delhi-building-collapse-site.html)

## 10. Thailand tightens borders ahead of ASEAN talks on Myanmar [Security] (unconfirmed)
