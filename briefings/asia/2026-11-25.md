# Asia Top 10 - Wednesday, 25 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asian chip stocks staged record rebounds on Nvidia's CEO reversal while incoming US tariffs and a Japan-China chill kept regional policy makers cautious.

## 1. Taiwan's Taiex posts record gain [Markets]

**What happened:** The Taiex closed up about 9% at around 40,122, its biggest single-day gain, with TSMC at its daily limit. It reversed a roughly 18% fall the previous week.
**Why it matters:** TSMC is about 40% of the index, so the move tracks AI-chip sentiment.
**Sources:** [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2026/11/25/2003868320), [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611240019)

## 2. Nikkei, Kospi surge; Shanghai flat [Markets]

**What happened:** The Nikkei rose 3.9% to 58,644.63, the Kospi 6.3% to 5,006.03 and the Shanghai Composite about 0.2-0.3% to roughly 3,548. Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell 0.8%.
**Why it matters:** Gains were concentrated in chip names such as Samsung and SK Hynix.
**Sources:** [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/24/asian-stocks-rally-after-wall-street-rebound-as-jensen-huang-returns-as-nvidias-ceo/), [Coin Edition](https://coinedition.com/asia-market-recap-taiwan-and-kospi-rally-as-huang-returns-to-nvidia/)

## 3. New US tariffs target Asian exporters [Trade]

**What happened:** A Section 301 probe covers China, Japan, South Korea, India, Taiwan, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Bangladesh and Cambodia, among others. China rejects the accusations.
**Why it matters:** Supply chains and export-driven economies face fresh costs.
**Sources:** [Globe and Mail/Reuters](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-trump-tariffs-excess-factory-capacity-november-24/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/24/trump-tariffs-china-affordability)

## 4. BOJ seen raising rates in December [Economy]

**What happened:** Most analysts expect a hike from 1.25% to 1.5% in December or January. The yen breached 160 per dollar on 13 November, prompting a finance minister warning.
**Why it matters:** Weak spending complicates the path, and Takaichi's team may resist further hikes.
**Sources:** [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/11/10/economy/boj-summary-opinions-october/), [AOL/Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/boj-keep-hiking-rates-soft-080109000.html)
