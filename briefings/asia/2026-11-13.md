# Asia Top 10 - Friday, 13 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns amid a China spy scandal, Beijing and Washington talk ahead of the Shenzhen summit, and Indonesia's vice-president quits.

## 1. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns amid China spy scandal [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia's board announced on Thursday that Jensen Huang resigned effective immediately, with CFO Colette Kress as interim chief executive. The exit follows reports tying him to Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive US officials reportedly suspect was recruited by Chinese intelligence; China called the allegations baseless smears. Reports differ on the share drop: one outlet had Nvidia closing down 4.3% on Thursday, while CBS put losses near $500 billion in value over the week.
**Why it matters:** Taiwan, Korea and Japan supply and depend on Nvidia, and the scandal feeds fears of tighter US chip export controls just before the US-China summit.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/ai-titan-jensen-huang-out-as-nvidia-ceo), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns/), [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-amid-alleged-affair/story?id=138076256)

## 2. Wang Yi and Rubio hold call ahead of Trump-Xi meeting [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** China's foreign minister held a call with US Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Chinese state media reported, describing it as positive and constructive and expressing willingness to strengthen communication. The call came as the two sides prepared for the leaders' meeting at APEC in Shenzhen. A US-China political dialogue also took place in Beijing, hosted by the CPC International Department.
**Why it matters:** Working-level channels are being used to keep the fragile trade truce on track before the leaders meet.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/world/article/348229/China's-foreign-minister-holds-call-with-US-Rubio-China's-state-media-reports), [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374120/rubio-wang-hold-phone-call-trump-xi-meeting-next-week)

## 3. Xi renews Taiwan reunification pledge on Sun Yat-sen anniversary [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** Speaking on 12 November, Xi urged Chinese people at home and abroad to unite more closely, carry forward Sun Yat-sen's legacy and pursue national rejuvenation, and Al Jazeera reported he said Taiwan's "complete reunification" will happen. I could not find a second source for the exact wording (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** The rhetoric lands just days before Xi hosts Trump, with Taiwan arms sales still unresolved.
**Sources:** [Morning Recap, The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348252/Morning-Recap-November-12-2026), [Al Jazeera China](https://www.aljazeera.com/where/china/)

## 4. Indonesia's vice-president Gibran resigns, stoking political uncertainty [Politics]

**What happened:** Reuters, citing political sources and an aide, reported that Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka had resigned after court rulings on his education certificate; the palace had earlier said he intended to resign. Bloomberg reported Prabowo accepted it. The MPR must convene within 60 days to elect a successor from two nominees.
**Why it matters:** The succession fight tests Prabowo's coalition and investor confidence in Southeast Asia's largest economy.
**Sources:** [The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/11/11/gibran-offers-to-resign), [The Star (Reuters)](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/11/11/indonesia039s-vice-president-has-resigned-three-political-sources-say), [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/825416)

## 5. Asian markets shrug off Nvidia scandal as Nikkei holds near records [Markets]

**What happened:** Asian equities largely absorbed the Nvidia news, with investingLive reporting that Asia shrugged off the scandal while oil steadied. Earlier in the week the Nikkei had climbed 1.3% to 61,248.59 on 11 November and the Kospi rebounded sharply from a tech-led selloff.
**Why it matters:** Regional AI trades remain strong but sensitive to US chip policy and Nvidia's results due next week.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-asia-shrugs-off-nvidia-scandal-oil-steadies/), [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/asian-shares-mostly-rise-on-ai-chip-demand-despite-wall-streets-retreat-on-inflation-data/)

## 6. Premier Li Qiang calls for deeper social security reform [Politics / Economy]

**What happened:** Premier Li Qiang called for further deepening social security reform, including a multi-tiered system covering urban and rural residents. The APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen is set for 18-19 November, and South Korea's president plans to attend.
**Why it matters:** Pension and health coverage reform is central to boosting household consumption in the 15th Five-Year Plan.
**Sources:** [Morning Recap, The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348252/Morning-Recap-November-12-2026), [APEC China 2026, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APEC_China_2026)
