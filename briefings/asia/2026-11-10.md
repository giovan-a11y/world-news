# Asia Top 10 - Tuesday, 10 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asian tech stocks rebound after a brutal week, New Zealand's knife-edge election stays unresolved, and China's factory prices surge while consumers stay cautious.

## 1. Asian stocks rebound after tech rout; Nikkei up 2.3% [Markets]

**What happened:** On Monday the Nikkei 225 rose 2.33% after plunging about 6% on Friday, while Kospi, which fell about 9% on Friday, gave up early gains and ended down 0.8% and the Hang Seng dipped 0.22%. The selloff followed a $2.5 trillion Wall Street drop after US midterm results raised fears that Democratic-led legislatures could slow AI data-centre rollouts.
**Why it matters:** Asia's AI-heavy indexes are now moving on US political risk as well as earnings, and volatility is extreme.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/9/asian-stock-markets-climb-after-earlier-tech-rout), [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/money/2026/11/10/tech-leads-asian-stock-rally-as-traders-track-wall-street-rebound/242685)

## 2. Taiex surges 4.9% after Taiwan activates market stabilisation fund [Markets]

**What happened:** Taiwan's stock market posted its second-largest daily point gain on Monday, with the Taiex rising 1,960 points, or 4.94%, to 41,661.70 after authorities authorised intervention on Friday. It was only the tenth time the stabilisation fund has been activated since 2000, and followed a 5.67% two-day fall tied to worries about heavy AI spending.
**Why it matters:** The fund's use shows how seriously Taipei views the AI-driven volatility in its largest industry.
**Sources:** [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611090017), [Morningstar / Dow Jones](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202611081423/taiex-jumps-after-taiwan-activates-market-stabilization-fund)

## 3. China's factory prices jump 5.7% while consumer inflation stays weak [Economy]

**What happened:** China's producer price index rose 5.7% year on year in October, the fastest since June 2022, driven partly by higher oil prices, while consumer prices rose only 0.7%, below the 0.8% forecast. Core inflation eased to 0.8%.
**Why it matters:** Manufacturers are absorbing costs while households hold back, a squeeze that complicates Beijing's push to lift domestic demand.
**Sources:** [Intelligence Report](https://intelligencereport.org/2026/11/09/china-inflation-october-ppi-cpi-gap/), [investingLive preview](https://investinglive.com/news/china-october-inflation-preview-cpi-seen-near-0-8-ppi-above-5/)

## 4. New Zealand's election too close to call [Politics]

**What happened:** In the 7 November vote, the National-ACT-NZ First coalition held a slim majority in the preliminary count, 67 of 131 seats on one tally, with National and Labour both near 29% of the party vote. Luxon and Hipkins both declined to claim victory on election night, and official results are due on 27 November. Seat figures differ across outlets and are provisional.
**Why it matters:** Coalition bargaining could take weeks and affects New Zealand's economic and China policy.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/governing-party-leads-in-new-zealands-knife-edge-vote-early-results), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/11/07/new-zealands-election-results-are-too-close-call/), [Wikipedia: 2026 New Zealand general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Zealand_general_election)

## 5. Delhi Chawri Bazar building collapse toll rises to seven [Disaster]

**What happened:** The death toll from the 5 November collapse of an under-construction building in Old Delhi's Chawri Bazar reached seven after a 70-hour rescue operation, with another body recovered on Sunday. The owner and three contractors have been arrested.
**Why it matters:** The case highlights unsafe construction in India's old city areas and pressure on municipal enforcement.
**Sources:** [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/news-brief/death-toll-rises-to-seven-in-delhi-building-disaster-as-70-hour-rescue-operation-continues-in-hauz-qazi), [The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/news/cities/new-delhi/death-toll-climbs-to-7-in-delhi-building-collapse-1140354)

## 6. Indonesia's wiretap scandal tests Prabowo-Jokowi alliance [Politics]

**What happened:** President Prabowo sacked his chief of staff, former national police chief Listyo Sigit Prabowo, on 4 November after police arrested him and two other senior officers over alleged illegal wiretapping of the president and others. The presidential office said the conduct could amount to treason, and troops were deployed in Jakarta. Analysts link the case to a rift with former president Joko Widodo.
**Why it matters:** The affair, followed by Vice President Gibran's expected resignation, deepens the political instability around Prabowo's government.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/indonesian-presidents-chief-of-staff-dismissed-over-alleged), [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/politics/article/3373235/indonesias-prabowo-sacks-chief-staff-wiretap-scandal-tests-alliance-widodo)

## 7. Sonia Gandhi memoir "Belonging" due in India on 10 November [Society / Politics]

**What happened:** HarperCollins India said Sonia Gandhi's 544-page memoir goes on sale on 10 November, after Penguin Random House India pulled out in August over editorial changes. **Why it matters:** The book's release revives a debate over publishing freedom and gives Congress a political talking point.
**Sources:** [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle/books/sonia-gandhi-memoir-belonging-harpercollins-india-penguin-release-date), [Madhyamam](https://madhyamamonline.com/india/harpercollins-announces-nov-release-for-sonia-gandhis-memoir-1551558)
