# Asia Top 10 - Saturday, 21 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asian chip-heavy markets suffered their worst day in months as the AI selloff hit, while a Shenzhen summit left China-Japan ties frozen.

*Editor's note: regional coverage found today was limited, so only 4 verified stories are listed.*

## 1. Asian markets plunge on AI selloff [Markets]

**What happened:** On Friday the Nikkei 225 fell about 5.1% to roughly 56,437, the Kospi about 5.2% to around 4,870, and Taiwan's Taiex about 6.2%, reported as its worst week since 1991 (that comparison is from one source). Shanghai fell about 1.2% to near 3,534.
**Why it matters:** Japan, Korea and Taiwan are heavily weighted to AI chipmakers, so Wall Street's reversal hit them hardest.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/20/asias-stock-markets-dive-as-anthropic-debut-fails-to-allay-ai-bubble-fears), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/taiwan-stock-market-falls-6-2-to-cap-its-worst-week-since-1991-as-kospi-closes-down-5-2-and-nikkei-225-drops-5-1-in-ai-chip-selloff/)

## 2. APEC Shenzhen: Xi, Trump and Putin meet; Takaichi kept at a distance [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** The first trilateral among the three leaders produced few concrete outcomes on trade or tech disputes. Japan's Takaichi did not meet Xi and received a cool reception.
**Why it matters:** The summit shows Beijing hosting great-power diplomacy while Tokyo stays shut out.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/trump-xi-putin-photos-us-china-russia-trilateral-.html), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/20/tough-times-japans-takaichi-chinas-apec-summit/)

## 3. China-Japan freeze continues over Taiwan remarks [Politics and security]

**What happened:** Beijing has again demanded Japan retract Takaichi's 2025 comments on Taiwan, and ties have been frozen for over a year, with a Chinese visa-fee hike for Japanese nationals reported in September.
**Why it matters:** The rift affects tourism, trade and regional security.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3373577/tokyo-still-dodging-issue-year-takaichi-row-beijing-says), [China Global South](https://chinaglobalsouth.com/2026/11/13/japan-takaichi-china-visit-apec-summit-taiwan-tensions/)

## 4. US-China trade, Taiwan and tech tensions unresolved [Economy]

**What happened:** CNBC reports no public breakthrough on tariffs or rare earths after the Trump-Xi talks, while tighter US chip export licensing weighed on regional chip stocks.
**Why it matters:** Export-dependent Asian economies face continued uncertainty.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/apec-summit-trump-xi-china-trade-taiwan.html)
