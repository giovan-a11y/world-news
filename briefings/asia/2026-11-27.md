# Asia Top 10 - Friday, 27 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asian markets bounced on Thursday after a brutal AI-chip selloff, while the China-Japan freeze over Taiwan shows no sign of thawing.

## 1. AI-chip rout hammers Korea, Taiwan and Japan [Markets]

**What happened:** The Kospi fell 6.16% to 4,697.51 on 25 November and the Nikkei dropped 2.78%. The week before, Taiwan's market fell 6.2% to cap its worst week since 1991. On Thursday, the Nikkei rose about 0.9% to 57,499.16 and the Kospi 0.3% to 4,711.21.
**Why it matters:** Samsung, SK Hynix and TSMC are priced on Nvidia's order book, so doubts about AI spending hit Asian indexes hardest.
**Sources:** [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/2026/11/26/global-stocks-rise-and-tech-shares-rebound-as-us-closes-for-thanksgiving/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/taiwan-stock-market-falls-6-2-to-cap-its-worst-week-since-1991-as-kospi-closes-down-5-2-and-nikkei-225-drops-5-1-in-ai-chip-selloff/)

## 2. Xi and Takaichi exchange smiles, no talks [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** At the APEC welcome banquet in Shenzhen the two leaders met in person for the first time since the Taiwan row began a year ago, but held no formal talks. Beijing still demands that Takaichi retract her Taiwan remarks.
**Why it matters:** The freeze affects trade, tourism and security across East Asia.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374944/xi-greets-takaichi-smile-no-words-china-japan-freeze-lingers), [Reuters via Internazionale](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/11/19/japan-s-position-on-taiwan-has-not-changed-pm-takaichi-says)

## 3. Nvidia's CEO exit unsettles Asian supply chains [Technology]

**What happened:** Jensen Huang's resignation on 12 November left CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO. SCMP reports on the successor hunt, including a possible Trump-favoured pick.
**Why it matters:** Taiwanese and Korean suppliers depend on Nvidia's roadmap and orders.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374268/nvidias-hunt-jensen-huangs-successor-insiders-rivals-and-trump-pick), [NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)

## 4. Shanghai flat as China markets lag the rebound [Markets]

**What happened:** The Shanghai Composite was roughly flat at 3,476.88 on Thursday while Japan and Korea recovered. A less reliable source put it near 3,539 on 23 November.
**Why it matters:** China's market shows less AI-driven volatility but also less momentum.
**Sources:** [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/2026/11/26/global-stocks-rise-and-tech-shares-rebound-as-us-closes-for-thanksgiving/), [Nero Signal](https://nerosignal.substack.com/p/the-nikkei-has-a-price-1673-points)

## 5. Gulf crisis threatens Asian energy supplies [Economy]

**What happened:** Hormuz oil traffic is down about 40 percent after tanker mine strikes, and Asia is the main buyer of Gulf crude.
**Why it matters:** Japan, Korea, India and China are the most exposed importers if the standoff worsens.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/hormuz-oil-traffic-is-down-40-percent-are-iran-s-tanker-attacks-working), [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/article-oil-touches-three-month-lows-as-rising-supply-offsets-hormuz-threat/)

## 6. China-Taiwan trade pressure continues [Politics]

**What happened:** Reports describe China ending tariff exemptions on dozens of Taiwanese food imports and blocking a Taiwanese executive from returning home (dates not confirmed).
**Why it matters:** Economic pressure on Taiwan is escalating alongside the market turmoil.
**Sources:** [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/asia?page=231), [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/category/region/east-asia/page/14)
