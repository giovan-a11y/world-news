# Asia Top 10 - Sunday, 22 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asia's tech-heavy markets plunged into bear territory on AI worries, as leaders gathered in Shenzhen and the UN court delivered a landmark genocide ruling on Myanmar.

*Note: search coverage was thin; this list holds the 6 items that could be sourced.*

## 1. Nikkei and Kospi crash about 5% [Markets]

**What happened:** On 20 November the Nikkei 225 fell 5.06% to 56,436.65, about 22% below its June record, and Kospi fell 5.2% to 4,869.96, with Samsung and SK Hynix down over 6% and SoftBank hit hard.
**Why it matters:** Both indexes are heavily weighted to chips and AI, making Asia the epicentre of the selloff.
**Sources:** [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/trading/asian-markets-crash-nikkei-225-kospi-tumble-nearly-5-amid-ai-selloff-pressure-what-s-impacting-markets-today/article-203742/), [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/companies/nikkei-225-crashes-to-56-436-65-as-softbank-and-advantest-lead-the-ai-rout)

## 2. Shanghai Composite nears 3,500 [Markets / China]

**What happened:** The Shanghai Composite fell 1.22% to 3,533.79, closing about 33 points above 3,500, as China held lending rates steady.
**Why it matters:** The 3,500 level is a key psychological support for mainland investors.
**Sources:** [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/global-economy/sse-composite-drops-to-3-533-79-as-the-ai-trade-cools-and-china-holds-rates)

## 3. Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** The 19 November three-way meeting on the APEC sidelines produced pledges on nuclear risk and Hormuz oil flows but little concrete progress.
**Why it matters:** China hosted the leaders of both rivals, underscoring its diplomatic weight, while Iran and Ukraine stayed unresolved.
**Sources:** [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-summit/), [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12157687/trump-xi-and-putin-reach-agreements-on-oil-and-nuclear-risks/)

## 4. ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya [Law / Southeast Asia]

**What happened:** The UN's top court found on 19 November that Myanmar's military committed genocide in 2016-17, the first such finding against a state; the ruling is final and compensation will be decided later.
**Why it matters:** It is a legal landmark for the 700,000+ Rohingya in Bangladesh, though Myanmar's response was not yet known.
**Sources:** [Human Rights Watch](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/11/19/world-court-finds-myanmar-committed-rohingya-genocide), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/asia/2026/11/19/top-un-court-rules-myanmar-responsible-for-acts-of-genocide-against-rohingya/)
