# Asia Top 10 - Friday, 6 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Asia digests the Democrats' sweep of the US midterms as Seoul's Kospi tumbles more than 5%, North Korea tests new missiles and Indonesia's wiretap scandal deepens.

## 1. Democrats sweep US midterms; Asian governments respond cautiously [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats were projected to win the House with more than 250 seats and also take control of the Senate. China's Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning called the elections "an internal affair of the United States", while Japan's Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara said the Japan-US alliance remains the cornerstone of Japan's foreign and security policy. CFR noted no Southeast Asian leader had commented publicly and that Asian policymakers expect the White House to soften on tariffs.
**Why it matters:** A Democratic Congress could reshape US trade, AI and alliance policy toward Asia, from chip rules to defence-cost demands.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/seismic-shift-world-reacts-to-us-midterm-election-results), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/04/nx-s1-6028664/world-reacts-midterm-elections), [Council on Foreign Relations](https://www.cfr.org/articles/they-wont-say-it-out-loud-but-asian-leaders-are-happy-about-the-midterms)

## 2. Asian shares diverge as AI names are sold after the midterms [Markets]

**What happened:** On Thursday, AP reported Seoul's Kospi fell more than 5% and Tokyo's Nikkei lost nearly 2% as AI-related stocks were sold, while Hong Kong's Hang Seng jumped 3.2% to 24,675.22 and the Shanghai Composite slipped 0.1% to 3,682.30. Another report put the Kospi down 3.25% and the Nikkei down 0.44% at a different point in the session; Toyota and Honda rose about 5% each. Investors feared a Democratic Congress could tighten AI rules.
**Why it matters:** Markets are now pricing US political risk into the AI trade, with Korea and Japan the most exposed.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/asian-shares-mostly-advance-following-us-midterms-though-kospi-falls-on-selling-of-chipmakers/), [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/global-markets/asian-market-rotation-after-us-midterms-elections), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/nikkei-drops-3-as-democratic-midterm-gains-hit-ai-and-chip-stocks/)

## 3. South Korea's "Black Thursday": Kospi and Kosdaq fall more than 5% [Markets / Politics]

**What happened:** Seoul's Kospi and Kosdaq each fell more than 5% on 5 November, closing at 5,383.80 and 678.41, on worries that US data-centre regulation could slow chip demand. The presidential office said the midterm results would not affect the alliance with the US, and Prime Minister Han Seong-sook said no major change in relations was expected.
**Why it matters:** Korea's AI-driven rally has run far above pre-election levels, and the drop is a political test for President Lee.
**Sources:** [Korea Center (Yonhap-based)](https://www.koreancenter.or.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=1435407), [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/kospi-dips-over-3-following-escalating-ai-concerns/12953462)

## 4. North Korea says it tested new air-defence system and long-range cruise missile [Security]

**What happened:** State media said Pyongyang test-fired a new air-defence missile system and a long-range cruise missile, after South Korea's military detected several missiles launched toward the East Sea on Wednesday, the fourth North Korean weapons launch in a month. The launches came from the Sondok area, days after nearly a dozen short-range ballistic missiles were fired near Wonsan.
**Why it matters:** The pace of tests comes as Seoul seeks talks and amid speculation about a Trump-Kim contact around APEC.
**Sources:** [The Star (Reuters)](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/11/04/north-korea-fires-multiple-missiles-in-fourth-weapons-launch-in-a-month-seoul-says), [Tasnim (KCNA summary)](https://www.tasnimnews.ir/en/news/2026/11/05/3740977/north-korea-says-test-fired-new-air-defense-missile-system-long-range-cruise-missile/amp), [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/asia_pacific/2026-11-05/north-korea-missile-technology-diplomacy-23369224.html)

## 5. Old Delhi building collapse kills at least two, workers trapped [Disaster]

**What happened:** A three-storey under-construction building collapsed near Chawri Bazar Metro station in Old Delhi around 9:45 am on 5 November, with at least two dead and several feared trapped under debris. Police, NDRF and fire crews launched a large rescue operation.
**Why it matters:** The death toll later rose, and the collapse exposed unsanctioned building work in the dense old city.
**Sources:** [Dynamite News](https://www.dynamitenews.com/national/at-least-2-dead-several-feared-trapped-as-three-storey-building-collapses-in-chawri-bazar), [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/breaking-news/chawri-bazar-building-collapse-two-dead-rescue)

## 6. Indonesia deploys troops as wiretap scandal engulfs police and palace [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** President Prabowo sacked his chief of staff, former police chief Listyo Sigit Prabowo, after police detained him and two other senior officers over alleged illegal wiretapping of the president and others. Troops were deployed in Jakarta, and the government said the conduct may amount to treason. Lt Gen Ariyo Windutomo was named acting chief of staff.
**Why it matters:** The affair exposes a rift with former president Joko Widodo and raises concern about stability in Southeast Asia's biggest economy.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/indonesian-presidents-chief-of-staff-dismissed-over-alleged), [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/indonesia-deploys-thousands-of-troops-after-generals-arrested-over-wiretapping-allegations/), [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/world/2026-11-04-troops-deployed-in-indonesia-as-president-sacks-aide-accused-of-wiretapping/)

## 7. Australia's rate hike to 4.85% reverberates [Economy]

**What happened:** The Reserve Bank of Australia raised its cash rate by 25 basis points to 4.85%, an 18-year high, in a 6-3 vote on 3 November, its fifth hike of 2026, citing the Middle East war, oil prices and inflation of 4.3% in September. Reports on 4-5 November looked at the impact on mortgages and said the statement dropped its warning of further increases.
**Why it matters:** The hike squeezes households and signals that the Iran-war energy shock is driving tightening across Asia-Pacific.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/more-rba-lifts-cash-rate-to-4-85-in-split-6-3-vote-sees-inflation-above-3-until-late-2027/), [Reserve Bank of Australia](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/smp/2026/nov/overview.html)
