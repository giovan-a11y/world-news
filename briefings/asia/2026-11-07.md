# Asia Top 10 - Saturday, 7 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A US-midterm-driven AI sell-off crushes Tokyo and Seoul markets, while Beijing confirms talks on a rare Xi-Trump-Putin meeting.

## 1. Tokyo and Seoul stocks crash after US midterms and AI sell-off [Economy]

**What happened:** On 6 November the Nikkei 225 fell about 5.96% to roughly 57,435, its worst week since 2020, while the Kospi dropped as much as 8% (triggering a 20-minute halt) and closed down about 6% near 5,060. TSMC fell about 8.5% and SoftBank Group about 11%. Investors feared Democratic control of Congress could slow data-centre building and tighten AI regulation after a tech rout on Wall Street.
**Why it matters:** The sell-off shows how exposed Asian bourses are to the AI trade and US politics.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/6/tech-stock-rout-driven-by-us-ai-fears-continues-in-asian-trading), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/east-asia/article/3373478/post-us-midterms-ai-worries-pummel-shares-japan-south-korea), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/nikkei-drops-3-as-democratic-midterm-gains-hit-ai-and-chip-stocks/)

## 2. China confirms talks on possible Xi-Trump-Putin meeting [Politics]

**What happened:** China said discussions are under way on a historic meeting among Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen later this month. No agenda or date was finalised.
**Why it matters:** A three-way summit of nuclear powers would be unprecedented and could touch trade, Ukraine and arms control.
**Sources:** [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261107/china-confirms-discussions-underway-on-historic-xi-trump-putin-meeting), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3371206/chinas-shenzhen-ready-host-xi-trump-and-putin-during-apec-summit)

## 3. Trump blames Democrats' midterm wins for market plunge [Politics / Economy]

**What happened:** Trump blamed the Democrats' midterm victories for Thursday's US market plunge (Nasdaq down 5.5%, semiconductors down 12.2%), which spilled into Asia on Friday.
**Why it matters:** US political change could affect AI investment, tariffs and alliance projects across Asia.
**Sources:** [HNGN](https://www.hngn.com/articles/273719/20261106/sp-500-drops-44-ai-stocks-sink-after-democratic-sweep.htm), [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/6/tech-stock-rout-driven-by-us-ai-fears-continues-in-asian-trading)

## 4. Kospi slips below 5,000 level President Lee promoted; retail investors vent [Politics / Markets]

**What happened:** The Kospi fell below the 5,000 mark that President Lee Jae Myung championed during his campaign, with retail investors mocking the pledge on online forums, The Edge Malaysia reported; Kyunghyang Shinmun said the index lost about 11% over two days and barely defended 5,000. Koreans bought about US$13 billion of US stocks in the four months to October while net selling Kospi shares.
**Why it matters:** A market that Lee tied to his political identity is now a source of public frustration and opposition attacks.
**Sources:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824828), [Kyunghyang Shinmun](https://www.khan.co.kr/en/article/202611061703007)

## 5. Taiwan's Taiex plunges 4.5%; stabilisation fund authorises intervention [Markets]

**What happened:** The Taiex tumbled 1,873 points, or 4.51%, to close below 40,000 on 6 November, its fourth-steepest single-session drop, as foreign institutions sold heavily. The market stabilisation fund's committee authorised intervention.
**Why it matters:** Taiwan's index is the most concentrated bet on AI hardware in Asia, so the fund's activation signals officials see systemic risk.
**Sources:** [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611060020), [Silicon Report](https://www.siliconreport.com/taiwan-activates-market-stabilization-measures-to-stem-ai-selloff)

## 6. Delhi building collapse rescue enters second day [Disaster]

**What happened:** Rescue teams were still searching for trapped workers on 6 November, a day after a three-storey under-construction building collapsed at Chawri Bazar in Old Delhi around 9:45 am on 5 November. Around 15 construction workers were at the site, and police later arrested the owner and contractors.
**Why it matters:** The disaster exposes unsanctioned construction in Old Delhi; an official said no building plan had been approved.
**Sources:** [The Week (PTI)](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2026/11/06/rescue-efforts-enter-2nd-day-at-old-delhi-building-collapse-site.html), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/chawri-bazar-building-collapse-rescue-operation-enters-second-day-126110600497_1.html)

## 7. China's top legislator meets Gabon's president and Austria's chancellor [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** Xinhua reported that Zhao Leji met Gabonese President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema, on his first state visit to China, and Austrian Chancellor Christian Stocker in Beijing on Friday. Austria's leader said Vienna adheres to the one-China policy, and Gabon is a first-time national exhibitor at the China International Import Expo in Shanghai, which runs to 10 November. A newsletter credited Xi with talks with Gabon's leader; sources differ on who met whom.
**Why it matters:** Beijing is showcasing trade openness and outreach to Europe and Africa before hosting APEC.
**Sources:** [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261106/a46fecf9a99c41eda678bbabffb2fce5/c.html), [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202611/1373554.shtml)

## 8. Australia sends firefighters to Indonesia's East Kalimantan [Disaster]

**What happened:** Australia agreed to send two aircraft and 20 firefighting personnel to help tackle forest and land fires in East Kalimantan from 6 to 12 November, according to a Bernama regional roundup. Indonesia's smoke haze has been a continuing issue this fire season.
**Why it matters:** The deployment shows regional cooperation on haze, which also affects Malaysia and Singapore.
**Sources:** [Bernama](https://bernama.com/tv/news.php?id=2632603), [The Star: ASEAN headlines 6 Nov](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/06/asean-news-headlines-at-10pm-on-friday-nov-6-2026)
