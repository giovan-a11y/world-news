# Asia Top 10 - Saturday, 14 November 2026

**Today in one line:** US officials confirm a three-way Trump-Xi-Putin meeting in China, while Nvidia's leadership shock rattles Asian tech stocks and Indonesia's vice-president crisis deepens.

## 1. Trump to meet Putin and Xi in China next week, US officials say [Politics]

**What happened:** US officials said President Trump will take part in the first trilateral meeting of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen next week. Officials said they did not expect Trump to raise with Xi the espionage allegations against tech executive Alice Huang, and that trade would be the focus. China's State Council confirmed Xi will attend the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen.
**Why it matters:** The format puts the three largest nuclear powers at one table, with Iran, trade and chip controls all in play.
**Sources:** [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-meet-putin-xi-china-week-us-officials/story?id=138128914), [China This Week newsletter](https://shenshiweioffical.substack.com/p/china-this-week-five-things-you-need-a31)

## 2. Asian shares slip as Nvidia CEO's exit raises AI doubts [Economy / Tech]

**What happened:** Asian shares were mixed to lower on Friday after Jensen Huang's exit as Nvidia CEO. AP had the Nikkei up about 0.5-0.8% near 61,100-61,300, the Kospi down about 1% near 5,290 with SK Hynix off 3%, and the Hang Seng down more than 1%. Nvidia shares fell over 4% in the US and analysts flagged fears of tighter US chip export rules before the Trump-Xi talks.
**Why it matters:** Korea, Taiwan and Japan carry the AI hardware chain, so leadership uncertainty at its biggest customer spreads quickly through their markets.
**Sources:** [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/13/asian-shares-are-mixed-as-investors-assess-impact-of-nvidias-fallout-on-global-ai-development/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/asian-stocks-steady-as-nikkei-gains-1-4-hang-seng-slips-on-nvidia-leadership-fallout/)

## 3. China's October data due as stimulus is switched on [Economy]

**What happened:** Official figures due Monday are expected to show slower growth in industrial output and retail consumption in October, according to Bloomberg's economist survey. The data is the first full scorecard since new stimulus measures took effect.
**Why it matters:** It tests whether Beijing's support measures are lifting demand just before it hosts APEC.
**Sources:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/china-gets-first-economic-scorecard-with-stimulus-taps-turned-on), [China This Week newsletter](https://shenshiweioffical.substack.com/p/china-this-week-five-things-you-need-a31)

## 4. Indonesia's vice-president crisis: Gibran's exit stirs coalition strain [Politics]

**What happened:** Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka told President Prabowo this week he intends to step down after court rulings on his education certificate; Reuters and Bloomberg reported he had resigned and Prabowo accepted it, while the palace described an intention to resign. Analysts say the episode strains Prabowo's alliances. The MPR must convene within 60 days to choose a successor from two nominees.
**Why it matters:** Southeast Asia's largest economy faces an unusual succession fight with market and coalition implications.
**Sources:** [The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/11/11/gibran-offers-to-resign), [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/politics/article/3374034/indonesian-vp-resignation-drama-raises-stakes-prabowo-alliances-strain)

## 5. ASEAN summit preparations finish in Manila [Politics]

**What happened:** President Marcos conducted a final inspection of the ASEAN summit venues as Finance Secretary Recto oversaw last preparations, with the 49th summit opening this weekend. Indonesia and the Philippines had earlier agreed to deepen defence and economic cooperation.
**Why it matters:** The summit will test the bloc's response to energy shortages and the South China Sea dispute.
**Sources:** [Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/world/news.php?id=2633025), [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/b1oC6e33-indonesian-and-philippines-discuss-defense-modernization-and-protection-of-citizens)

## 9. MSCI adds Chinese AI and energy names; China clears Lilly pill [Economy] (unconfirmed)

**What happened:** MSCI will add Zhongji Innolight, Huadian New Energy and Yunnan Germanium to its China Index from 1 December. A weekly newsletter also says China approved Eli Lilly's first oral weight-loss drug; Lilly had said launch could come between late 2026 and early 2027.
