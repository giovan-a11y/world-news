# Asia Top 10 - Tuesday, 24 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The aftermath of the Shenzhen APEC summit and Manila meetings shapes Asia's agenda, with Japan feeling isolated and Indonesia facing a market-status reckoning.

*Editor's note: source access was limited, so this edition has 5 items rather than 10.*

## 1. Shenzhen summit puts Xi at centre stage [China] 

**What happened:** Xi hosted Trump and Putin for the first trilateral; Xi greeted Trump personally while Putin received a smaller welcome. Output was a declaration on energy and nuclear risk, with no Ukraine language and no Kim Jong Un meeting.
**Why it matters:** Beijing gains prestige as broker while concrete trade and tech disputes stay open.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3375124/nuclear-arms-energy-supplies-no-kim-main-takeaways-historic-apec-summit), [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/groundbreaking-trump-xi-putin-summit-five-key-takeaways)

## 2. Japan's Takaichi left out in the cold [Japan]

**What happened:** No Xi meeting, a low-level airport greeting, and Beijing's demand that Tokyo "correct" its Taiwan stance. Takaichi spoke with Trump by phone for about 20 minutes on 19 November and said the Quad summit was the main result.
**Why it matters:** Japan-China ties remain frozen, with consequences for trade, tourism and regional security.
**Sources:** [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/20/tough-times-japans-takaichi-chinas-apec-summit/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/trump-xi-putin-photos-us-china-russia-trilateral-.html)

## 3. Quad summit in Manila announces joint patrols, AI and cyber programmes [Security]

**What happened:** Leaders of the US, Japan, Australia and India plan a first joint maritime patrol exercise next year; wording on China was reportedly softened from earlier drafts.
**Why it matters:** Signals continuity of the grouping, with limited public confrontation of Beijing.
**Sources:** [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/?p=315368), [The Wire](https://thewire.in/diplomacy/first-quad-leaders-summit-under-trump-softens-criticism-china-maritime)

## 4. ASEAN and China miss South China Sea code-of-conduct goal [Southeast Asia]

**What happened:** The Philippines, as ASEAN chair, did not conclude the code of conduct this year; Beijing's ambassador says recent clashes have not changed its intent to conclude one.
**Why it matters:** Maritime tensions persist without a rulebook.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/16/asean-news-headlines-at-10pm-on-monday-nov-16-2026), [Herald](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10940501)

## 5. Indonesia faces possible MSCI exclusion [Markets]

**What happened:** MSCI proposed in October to drop Indonesia to "standalone" status, removing it from global indexes; the November review is the next checkpoint and Barito Pacific is removed on 30 November. Officials called it a wake-up call.
**Why it matters:** More than $1.8 trillion benchmarked to MSCI emerging-market indexes could shed Indonesian stocks, risking capital outflows.
**Sources:** [NST](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/corporate/2026/10/1556569/msci-set-remove-indonesia-global-indices), [The Sun](https://thesun.my/business/indonesia-faces-removal-from-msci-global-indices/)
