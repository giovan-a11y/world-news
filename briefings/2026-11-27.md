# World Top 10 - Friday, 27 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Iran war's Strait of Hormuz standoff and a violent AI-chip market selloff dominate, while the US pauses for Thanksgiving and Democrats prepare to take Congress.

## 1. Strait of Hormuz standoff: tankers hit mines, talks stalled [Conflict]

**What happened:** Iran's Revolutionary Guards said three oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines in the strait on Monday. Al Jazeera reports Hormuz oil traffic is down about 40 percent. Diplomacy is reported stalled, with Iran demanding an end to the US blockade of its ports and release of frozen assets before reopening the strait.
**Why it matters:** About a fifth of the world's oil passes through Hormuz, so shipping disruption feeds directly into energy prices and inflation.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/hormuz-oil-traffic-is-down-40-percent-are-iran-s-tanker-attacks-working), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/commodities/oil-prices-rise-on-supply-concerns-as-iran-claims-mine-strikes-on-ships-126112600085_1.html)

## 2. US-Iran talks: Vance says "ball is in Iran's court" [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** Vice President JD Vance has said the US has laid out firm red lines and that the next move is Iran's, while the US keeps its blockade on Iranian ports. Qatar's prime minister has been shuttling between the sides. Reports also mention a third US carrier arriving in the region (single-source, unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** A deal would reopen Hormuz; failure raises the risk of a wider campaign.
**Sources:** [WLOS/ABC](https://wlos.com/news/nation-world/vance-says-the-ball-is-in-the-iranian-court-as-us-military-continues-blockade-on-ports-president-donald-trump-strait-of-hormuz-oil-pakistan-pope-leo-france-macron-uk-keir-starmer-sanctions-lebanon-israel-middle-east-war), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/?update=4215990)

## 3. Global AI-chip selloff rattles Asian and US markets [Markets]

**What happened:** Wall Street erased a 3.2% intraday drop in the S&P 500 on Wednesday to close about 0.25% lower, with Nvidia down 3.9%. Asian markets rebounded on Thursday after the Kospi fell 6.2% a day earlier. The week before, Taiwan's market suffered its worst week since 1991.
**Why it matters:** Chipmakers and AI infrastructure are a large share of global equity gains, so doubts about AI spending move whole indexes.
**Sources:** [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/2026/11/26/global-stocks-rise-and-tech-shares-rebound-as-us-closes-for-thanksgiving/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/taiwan-stock-market-falls-6-2-to-cap-its-worst-week-since-1991-as-kospi-closes-down-5-2-and-nikkei-225-drops-5-1-in-ai-chip-selloff/)

## 4. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; interim chief runs record-earning company [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia announced on 12 November that Huang resigned effective immediately; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Reports tie the exit to an alleged affair with an employee. Days later Nvidia reported $115.8 billion quarterly revenue and guided to $130 billion.
**Why it matters:** The world's most valuable AI supplier is without a permanent leader just as investors question the AI cycle.
**Sources:** [NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-next-ceo-jensen-huang-predictions), [TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/354965/nvidia-reports-q3-2026-earnings-of-usd-115-8-billion)

## 5. Oil near three-month lows despite Hormuz risk [Economy]

**What happened:** Reports say global supply has exceeded demand for the first time since the Iran war began, keeping crude near three-month lows even as mine claims cause spikes. WTI was quoted near $81; Brent quotes vary widely by source and time.
**Why it matters:** A supply glut offsets war risk, easing pressure on inflation but leaving prices vulnerable to any diplomatic breakthrough or escalation.
**Sources:** [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/article-oil-touches-three-month-lows-as-rising-supply-offsets-hormuz-threat/), [FinScans](https://finscans.com/news/2026/11/25/wti-crude-oil-holds-at-8115-as-middle-east-tensions-offset)
