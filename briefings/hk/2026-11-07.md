# Hong Kong Top 10 - Saturday, 7 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Ten governments condemned Joshua Wong's 10-year sentence and Hong Kong hit back, while a joint task force arrested 28 in its first bid-rigging operation and the Hang Seng fell 1.27%.

## 1. Ten governments condemn Joshua Wong's sentence; Hong Kong rejects statement [Politics]

**What happened:** The US, UK, France, Germany, Japan, Italy, Australia, Canada, the Netherlands and New Zealand issued a joint statement criticising Joshua Wong's 10-year sentence and calling for an end to "unjust prosecutions" under the national security law. The Hong Kong government expressed strong dissatisfaction and firm opposition, saying the statement ignored facts and interfered with judicial independence. The Chinese Embassy in London also condemned it.
**Why it matters:** It sharpens the diplomatic dispute over the national security law.
**Sources:** [CP24](https://www.cp24.com/news/world/2026/11/06/canada-us-uk-and-others-condemn-10-year-sentence-for-hong-kong-activist-joshua-wong/), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347817/Hong-Kong-strongly-rejects-ten-nation-statement-on-Joshua-Wong-sentencing-as-foreign-interference), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/hong-kong/politics/article/3373638/hypocritical-hong-kong-hits-back-criticism-joshua-wongs-sentence)

## 2. Task force arrests 28 in first bid-rigging operation after Tai Po fire [Courts]

**What happened:** The joint task force of police, the ICAC and the Competition Commission announced 28 arrests on Friday, including directors and staff of at least six contractors and 10 consultant firms. A suspected triad-linked syndicate allegedly secured 12 works contracts at residential estates. A 24-hour reporting hotline was announced. Security chief Chris Tang had earlier said the unit would investigate proactively without waiting for complaints.
**Why it matters:** It is the first operation under the post-fire push to criminalise bid-rigging in building maintenance.
**Sources:** [The Star (RTHK)](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/2026/11/07/hong-kong-arrests-28-in-building-bid-rigging-probe-rthk-says), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/law-and-crime/article/3373575/hong-kong-authorities-arrest-28-crackdown-bid-rigging-after-tai-po-fire), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347151/Chris-Tang-pledges-stronger-deterrent-against-bid-rigging-with-new-task-force-and-reforms)

## 3. About 30 defective pillars found at Tung Chung West station [Transport]

**What happened:** About 30 pillars at the under-construction Tung Chung West station were found defective as of 6 November. MTR was told to stop the contractor's works and the Transport and Logistics Bureau ordered a report within two weeks. The Highways Department said the contractor used an unapproved repair method; the Buildings Department said there is no immediate danger. The 2029 opening target is unchanged.
**Why it matters:** It is a quality-control test for a new rail project.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347832/30-defective-pillars-found-at-Tung-Chung-West-station-as-MTR-ordered-to-report-within-two-weeks), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/transport/article/3373646/mtr-corporation-halts-unauthorised-remedial-works-station-after-column-concerns)

## 4. Hang Seng falls 1.27% on Friday to 24,481.86 [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index fell 315.12 points, or 1.27%, to 24,481.86 on 6 November after opening 1.58% lower, on turnover of HK$247.428 billion. The China Enterprises Index fell 1.13% to 8,274.10 and the Tech Index 1.83% to 4,078.23. It followed Thursday's 3.67% jump to 24,796.98.
**Why it matters:** The market gave back part of the post-US-midterm rally.
**Sources:** [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20261106/9f4ade0f744643b69131fe558ccc94b4/c.html), [Xinhua (5 Nov)](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20261105/30dca13060c845e8aeb2926ad0009301/c.html)

## 5. National security police arrest man over importing seditious books [Courts]

**What happened:** National security police arrested a 34-year-old company director on suspicion of importing seditious publications from Taiwan for local bookstores. The Star reports he is in charge of prisoner support group Waiting Bird; The Standard cites sources calling him a member. The group says more than 60 ordered books went missing and denies seditious intent.
**Why it matters:** It extends enforcement to book imports and civil-society groups.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/07/hong-kong-police-arrest-operator-of-prisoner-support-group-over-allegedly-seditious-books), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347682/National-Security-Department-arrests-man-over-importing-seditious-books-for-local-bookstores)

## 6. Eala beats Ruzic to reach Hong Kong Open semi-finals [Sport]

**What happened:** Second seed Alex Eala beat Antonia Ruzic 7-6(7), 7-5 in Friday's quarter-final. China's Wang Xinyu's season ended at the tournament.
**Why it matters:** Eala's run keeps the city's WTA event in the headlines going into the weekend.

## 7. Labour Department halts works at Sha Tin roadworks site after worker's death [Society]

**What happened:** The Civil Engineering and Development Department ordered its contractor to investigate and the Labour Department halted all works on the carriageway after a 61-year-old woman worker was struck by a truck at a Sha Tin Road site on Thursday. The vehicle type differs between reports.
**Why it matters:** It adds to scrutiny of site safety in public works.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347695/Sha-Tin-roadworks-death-contractor-ordered-to-investigate-as-Labour-Department-halts-all-works-on-carriageway), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347650/Woman-worker-killed-after-being-struck-by-truck-at-Sha-Tin-roadworks-site)

## 8. No. 3 alarm fire at Yuen Long paper offerings warehouse [Society]

**What happened:** A No. 3 alarm fire broke out around 11pm at a joss paper warehouse in Pak Sha Tsuen. Twelve people were evacuated and no casualties were reported; the cause is under investigation.
**Why it matters:** It is another New Territories storage-site fire.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347772/No-casualties-reported-after-paper-product-warehouse-in-Yuen-Long-hit-by-No-3-alarm-fire)

## 9. Tuen Mun estate cancels owners' meeting after ICAC notice [Society]

**What happened:** Elegance Gardens cancelled a special owners' meeting to choose a repair contractor after an urgent ICAC notice; sources said staff of its maintenance consultant were among those arrested (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It shows the probe reaching individual estates.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347784/Tuen-Muns-Elegance-Gardens-cancels-owners-meeting-after-ICAC-notice-consultant-firm-members-reportedly-arrested)
