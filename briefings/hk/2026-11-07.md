# Hong Kong Top 10 - Saturday, 7 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Hang Seng fell 1.27% on Friday but still posted its best week since March, while the Exchange Fund's third-quarter income dropped 69%.

Note: my sources for this date were thin, so this list has 7 items rather than 10.

## 1. Hang Seng falls 1.27% to 24,481.86 but gains 5.10% on the week [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index closed Friday 6 November at 24,481.86, down 315.12 points or 1.27%, after jumping 3.67% to 24,796.98 on Thursday. Tech stocks drove the pullback on a global AI-related sell-off and profit-taking. For the week it rose 5.10%, its best since early March. (One Yahoo Finance page showed a slightly different close of 24,533.33; most sources report 24,481.86.)
**Why it matters:** Hong Kong's tech-heavy market swung sharply on the AI trade.
**Sources:** [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-1-27-percent-lower-at-24-481-86-points/70471524), [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/hong-kong-stock-exchange-weekly-review-hang-seng-jumps-to-24-481-86-in-its-best-week-since-march)

## 2. Exchange Fund third-quarter investment income falls 69% to HK$18.6 billion [Economy]

**What happened:** The Exchange Fund, which backs the Hong Kong dollar, posted investment income of HK$18.6 billion for the third quarter, down 69% from a year earlier, according to HKMA data.
**Why it matters:** It shows how market swings affect the reserves behind the peg.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347765/Night-Recap-November-6-2026)

## 3. Abby Choi trial hears forensic evidence on day 32 [Courts]

**What happened:** Forensic testimony at the High Court on the 32nd day of the murder trial described bone cut marks consistent with a meat chopper.
**Why it matters:** The evidence is central to the prosecution's case in a high-profile trial.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347765/Night-Recap-November-6-2026)

## 4. Police to roll out facial recognition across CCTV network [Government]

**What happened:** RTHK reported police plan to roll out facial recognition across their CCTV network this month; SCMP reported the rollout was set for Monday.
**Why it matters:** It raises privacy questions alongside crime-fighting benefits.
**Sources:** [RTHK](https://gbcode.rthk.hk/TuniS/news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1876862-20261106.htm?spTabChangeable=0), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong)

## 5. Canada "lifeboat" PR applications from Hong Kong expected cleared by 2027 [Society]

**What happened:** Hong Kong residents' permanent residency applications under Canada's "lifeboat" scheme are reportedly expected to be cleared by 2027 (single source, unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It matters to families who have emigrated or plan to.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347765/Night-Recap-November-6-2026)
