# Hong Kong Top 10 - Tuesday, 10 November 2026

**Today in one line:** About 40 visitors were stranded on the Ocean Park tower, the chief executive met Asia's football chief, and a cold front dropped temperatures by about 9 degrees.

## 1. About 40 stranded when Ocean Park Tower stalls near its top [Society]

**What happened:** About 40 people were stranded near the top of Ocean Park Tower after the ride malfunctioned on Monday afternoon. One visitor was reported to have felt unwell.
**Why it matters:** It raises safety questions at a major attraction.
