# Hong Kong Top 10 - Thursday, 5 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Hang Seng rose 1.67% to 23,919.50 after a volatile session as US midterm results came in, and LegCo moved on crypto tax reporting and a new crematorium.

Note: my sources for this date were limited, so this list has 5 items rather than 10.

## 1. Hang Seng closes up 1.67% at 23,919.50 [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index closed at 23,919.50 on Wednesday 4 November, up 393.53 points, from 23,525.97 the day before. It first fell to 23,385 before healthcare, gold and AI hardware shares led an afternoon recovery. Turnover was about HK$236.9 billion. The China Enterprises Index rose 1.44% to 8,057.59 and the Tech Index rose 1.77% to 3,936.02.
**Why it matters:** Traders weighed a Democratic majority in both chambers of the US Congress and stronger mainland markets.
**Sources:** [Futu News](https://news.futunn.com/en/post/1003379723/hong-kong-stock-market-close-nov-4-hang-seng-index), [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-1-67-prozent-higher-at-23-919-50/70458945)

## 2. LegCo passes crypto tax-reporting law [Government]

**What happened:** LegCo passed a law requiring crypto holders to report for tax purposes.
**Why it matters:** It aligns Hong Kong with global tax-transparency rules as it markets itself as a digital-asset hub.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347657/Night-Recap-November-5-2026)

## 3. LegCo subcommittee approves HK$2.03 billion for Wo Hop Shek crematorium [Government]

**What happened:** A LegCo subcommittee approved HK$2.03 billion for a new crematorium at Wo Hop Shek.
**Why it matters:** It addresses pressure on cremation capacity.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347529/Night-Recap-November-4-2026)

## 4. Gregory Wong's final appeal over 2019 LegCo rioting conviction rejected (reported 2 November) [Courts]

**What happened:** The top court rejected actor Gregory Wong's final appeal over his 2019 LegCo rioting conviction (dated 2 November, outside the window; included as context, unconfirmed for this date).
**Why it matters:** It ends one of the cases tied to the 2019 unrest.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/2026/11/02/hong-kong-top-court-rejects-actor-gregory-wongs-final-appeal-over-2019-legco-rioting-conviction/)

## 5. Joshua Wong sentencing expected [Courts]

**What happened:** The High Court sentenced Joshua Wong on 5 November (reported in the next briefing); on 4 November the case was awaited.
**Why it matters:** It is a major national-security case.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347657/Night-Recap-November-5-2026)
