# Hong Kong Top 10 - Thursday, 5 November 2026

**Today in one line:** John Lee began a Beijing and Shanghai trip, the Abby Choi trial heard her ex-husband's DNA was on the hammer handle, and the Hang Seng rose 1.67% for a fourth straight gain.

## 1. Hang Seng rises 1.67% to 23,919.50, a fourth straight gain [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index gained 393.53 points, or 1.67%, to close at 23,919.50 on Wednesday after a weaker start, with healthcare, gold and technology shares leading (single source for the close, unconfirmed). It had closed at 23,525.97 on Tuesday.
**Why it matters:** The run set up Thursday's much larger jump after the US midterm elections.
**Sources:** [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-1-67-higher-at-23-919-50/70458945), [Xinhua (3 Nov)](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20261103/0a2dca6afb04493092b0413ccfe3b2f9/c.html)

## 2. Abby Choi trial: ex-husband's DNA matches hammer handle [Courts]

**What happened:** A government lab expert told the High Court that DNA on the hammer handle matched Alex Kwong, with only nine markers found and a match statistic of one in 6.2 billion. The hammer head yielded too little DNA to analyse, and earlier agreed facts said no trace of Choi's DNA was detected on the hammer. Anthony Kwong and Kwong Kau were excluded as sources of the handle DNA. All three defendants deny murder.
**Why it matters:** It is key forensic evidence in the city's most closely followed murder trial, expected to run about eight weeks.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347484/Ex-husbands-DNA-found-on-hammer-handle-court-hears-in-Abby-Choi-trial), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/hong-kong/law-and-crime/article/3373639/ex-husbands-dna-found-hammer-allegedly-used-kill-abby-choi-prosecution-says)

## 3. John Lee begins Beijing and Shanghai trip to deepen mainland ties [Politics]

**What happened:** The Chief Executive began a four-day visit to Beijing and Shanghai. In Beijing he spoke at the 29th Beijing-Hong Kong Economic Cooperation Symposium, presenting the Northern Metropolis as a research and innovation platform. He said the government is pushing for a "brother port" with Shanghai this year.
**Why it matters:** It shows the administration's push for economic integration with the mainland.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/politics/article/3373225/john-lee-begins-beijing-shanghai-trip-deepen-mainland-economic-ties), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347544/John-Lee-eyes-deeper-trade-ties-with-mainland-overseas-on-Shanghai-trip)

## 4. HKMA's fifth Global Financial Leaders' Investment Summit concludes [Economy]

**What happened:** The three-day summit, with about 280 financial leaders, ended on Wednesday with a "Conversations with Global Investors" forum. John Lee said Hong Kong remains the nexus of global finance. Vice-Premier He Lifeng had pledged by video to add measures to strengthen Hong Kong as an international financial centre, and regulators outlined plans to expand offshore renminbi business.
**Why it matters:** It is the city's flagship finance event and a signal of Beijing's backing.
**Sources:** [HKMA](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261103-4/), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/article/3373105/vice-premier-he-lifeng-vows-support-hong-kong-international-financial-centre), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/347305/Rethinking-the-Playbook-Hong-Kong-stays-the-nexus-for-global-finance-John-Lee-says)

## 5. Fire at Richland Gardens in Kowloon Bay; 150 evacuated [Society]

**What happened:** A fire broke out in a low-floor unit at Richland Gardens early on Wednesday. About 150 residents evacuated and three people were taken to hospital with suspected smoke inhalation; a man and woman from the unit escaped barefoot.
**Why it matters:** It is a reminder of residential fire risk as fire-safety reviews continue.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347427/Fire-at-Richland-Gardens-in-Kowloon-Bay-150-residents-evacuated-3-taken-to-hospital)

## 6. Investment scams top HK$60 million in a week; one victim loses HK$45 million [Society]

**What happened:** Police recorded more than 30 online investment scam cases with losses over HK$60 million in the past week. One victim lost HK$45 million to a fake environmental investment platform and made more than 60 transfers on instructions from fake customer service staff.
**Why it matters:** Investment fraud remains the largest scam category by losses.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347511/Hong-Kong-investment-scams-top-60-million-in-a-week-with-one-victim-losing-45-million)

## 7. Huanggang Port crossings reported to have doubled [Transport]

**What happened:** The Standard's recap reported that the revamped Huanggang Port now sees more than 86,000 crossings on weekdays, more than double the old Lok Ma Chau figure. Other reports gave different figures (about 80,000 daily, above 92,000 on one Friday), so the exact number is unconfirmed.
**Why it matters:** It indicates how quickly cross-boundary traffic has shifted to the new port.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347529/Night-Recap-November-4-2026), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/hong-kong/hong-kong-economy/article/3371240/hong-kongs-new-huanggang-port-seemingly-quiet-ahead-expected-holiday-rush)

## 8. Airport city SKYTOPIA to open most facilities by 2028 [Transport]

**What happened:** The Airport Authority said most amenities at SKYTOPIA, including a minibus connection to Tung Chung, are scheduled to debut by 2028 (single source, unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It sets a timeline for a major tourism and airport-city project.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347529/Night-Recap-November-4-2026)

## 9. Oracle reportedly drops US$7 billion Tencent AI chip deal [Markets]

**What happened:** The Financial Times reported that Oracle cancelled a five-year, US$7 billion deal to lease about 100,000 AI chips to Tencent after US officials raised concerns. Oracle, Tencent and the White House did not comment to Reuters.
**Why it matters:** It bears on Tencent, a Hang Seng heavyweight, and US-China technology tensions.
**Sources:** [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/oracle-cancels-tencent-ai-chip-lease), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347529/Night-Recap-November-4-2026)

## 10. Hong Kong startup funding falls 77% in October [Economy]

**What happened:** TNGlobal reported that Hong Kong startup funding in October 2026 totalled US$94.09 million across five rounds, down 77% from September.
**Why it matters:** It tempers the city's strong headline growth data.
**Sources:** [TNGlobal](https://technode.global/2026/11/04/hong-kong-startup-funding-slips-to-94-1m-in-october-2026-down-77-from-september/)
