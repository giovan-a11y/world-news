# Hong Kong Top 10 - Friday, 6 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Joshua Wong was jailed for 10 years on a foreign-collusion conspiracy charge, and the Hang Seng jumped 3.67% in a post-midterm rally.

Note: my sources for this date were limited, so this list has 8 items rather than 10.

## 1. Joshua Wong jailed for 10 years over foreign-collusion conspiracy [Courts]

**What happened:** The High Court sentenced former Demosisto secretary-general Joshua Wong to 10 years after he pleaded guilty to a conspiracy charge involving foreign collusion.
**Why it matters:** It is one of the most closely watched national-security sentences in the city.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347657/Night-Recap-November-5-2026), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/where/hong-kong/)

## 2. LegCo says it firmly supports the court's sentencing in the Wong case [Government]

**What happened:** The HKSAR LegCo issued a statement backing the court's lawful sentencing in the "conspiracy to commit collusion" case of Wong Chi-fung.
**Why it matters:** It shows official backing for national-security enforcement.
**Sources:** [GovHK](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/05/P2026110500796.htm)

## 3. Hang Seng jumps 3.67% to 24,796.98 [Markets]

**What happened:** Hong Kong stocks jumped nearly 900 points on Thursday 5 November, helped by tech and gold gains after the US midterm elections.
**Why it matters:** It was a sharp rebound that set up the week's best gain since March.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347657/Night-Recap-November-5-2026), [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-1-27-percent-lower-at-24-481-86-points/70471524)
