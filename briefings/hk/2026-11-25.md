# Hong Kong Top 10 - Wednesday, 25 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The approaching first anniversary of the Wang Fuk Court fire dominates the agenda while the Hang Seng sank to its lowest close in over a year.

## 1. Beijing backs post-fire reforms, warns against destabilisation [Politics]

**What happened:** The liaison office and the Hong Kong and Macau Affairs Office backed building reforms and warned against exploiting the Tai Po fire anniversary to stir division.
**Why it matters:** It sets the tone ahead of the anniversary on 26 November.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/politics/article/3375659/beijing-warns-against-exploiting-tai-po-fire-destabilise-hong-kong), [The Standard](https://thestandard.com.hk/news/article/349360/HK-accelerates-sweeping-building-maintenance-overhaul-following-fatal-Wang-Fuk-Court-fire)

## 2. New cash grant for fire victims [Society]

**What happened:** Fire victims will receive a new grant of up to HK$300,000, and the No 2 official pledged a resident-oriented approach.
**Why it matters:** It aims to ease ongoing hardship for displaced residents.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/society/article/3375545/hong-kongs-wang-fuk-court-fire-victims-receive-new-cash-grant-hk300000)

## 3. Lee orders faster building maintenance overhaul [Government]

**What happened:** Chief Executive John Lee told bureaus and law enforcers to speed up an end-to-end overhaul of maintenance and safety rules, and a new public housing maintenance unit launched.
**Why it matters:** It signals the scope of reforms after the fire.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://thestandard.com.hk/news/article/349360/HK-accelerates-sweeping-building-maintenance-overhaul-following-fatal-Wang-Fuk-Court-fire), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong)

## 4. Hang Seng hits lowest close in over a year [Markets]

**What happened:** The index slid 0.8% to 22,205.65 on Tuesday after a 0.12% fall Monday to 22,383.74, extending a losing streak. Monday saw a deep-V recovery from a 19-month low.
**Why it matters:** AI valuation worries are weighing on Hong Kong tech even as Taiwan and Korea rebounded.
**Sources:** [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/24/asian-stocks-rally-after-wall-street-rebound-as-jensen-huang-returns-as-nvidias-ceo/), [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/companies/hong-kong-stock-exchange-hang-seng-slips-0-12-to-22-383-74-after-deep-v-recovery-from-19-month-low)

## 5. InnoHK marine intelligence centre announced [Innovation]

**What happened:** The government announced a Hong Kong Centre for Marine Intelligence and Sustainable Development under InnoHK.
**Why it matters:** It adds to the city's research push in ocean technology.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://thestandard.com.hk/news/article/349474/Hong-Kong-to-set-up-marine-intelligence-research-center-under-InnoHK), [info.gov.hk](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/today.htm)

## 6. Abby Choi murder trial continues [Courts]

**What happened:** At the High Court, her former father-in-law told the court he takes full responsibility for dismembering her body (reported 17 November).
**Why it matters:** The case has drawn wide public attention. Not dated 24 November (unconfirmed).
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/topics/crime-hong-kong)

## 7. Second arrest over Tai Po stray dog cruelty [Crime]

**What happened:** Police arrested a second man over the hanging of a stray dog and found traps and cages at his home.
**Why it matters:** The case has stirred animal welfare concerns.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong)

## 8. LegCo bills and teacher certification plan [LegCo]

**What happened:** LegCo has been taking second readings on the Statute Law, Evidence and Banking Legislation amendment bills. The government also planned to gazette an Education (Amendment) Bill for teacher practising certificates.
**Why it matters:** The education bill would bar those convicted of national security offences from teaching. Timing of readings is from mid-November (unconfirmed for now).
**Sources:** [Bastille Post](https://www.bastillepost.com/global/article/6340016-legco-to-consider-northern-metropolis-development-bill), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348346/Night-Recap-November-12-2026)

## 9. Ombudsman to host Asian summit; Customs smuggling update [Government]

**What happened:** The Ombudsman will host the Asian Ombudsman Association's 30th anniversary summit, and Customs issued a smuggling enforcement update.
**Why it matters:** Minor but routine government business.
**Sources:** [info.gov.hk](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/today.htm)

## 10. Wang Fuk Court flat buyback and sales (unconfirmed) [Property]

**What happened:** Earlier reports said the government proposed buying back flats at HK$8,000-10,500 per sq ft, around HK$6.8 billion. Recent transactions are listed around HK$3.5-3.9 million per unit.
