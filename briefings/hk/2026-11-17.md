# Hong Kong Top 10 - Tuesday, 17 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Hong Kong prepares for APEC delegates transiting to Shenzhen while its economic outlook brightens and data breaches hit consumers.

## 1. John Lee pledges stronger regional ties ahead of APEC trip [Government]

**What happened:** Chief Executive John Lee pledged stronger regional ties ahead of a trip tied to the APEC summit in Shenzhen, SCMP reports on 16 November. Hong Kong is also preparing to host delegates transiting to Shenzhen.
**Why it matters:** The summit next door raises Hong Kong's profile as a gateway and a hub for regional business.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348662/Night-Recap-November-16-2026)

## 2. Standard Chartered lifts 2026 growth forecast to 5% [Economy]

**What happened:** Standard Chartered raised its full-year Hong Kong GDP growth forecast from 4.3% to 5%.
**Why it matters:** A stronger outlook supports jobs, property and fiscal revenue, though global AI-led market volatility is a risk.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348662/Night-Recap-November-16-2026)

## 3. LegCo passes bill to streamline Northern Metropolis development [Government / LegCo]

**What happened:** Lawmakers passed a bill intended to streamline development of the Northern Metropolis (single listing: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** The bill is meant to speed up land and infrastructure for the northern New Territories growth zone.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348662/Night-Recap-November-16-2026)

## 4. Retail and tourism data breaches [Society / Tech]

**What happened:** SOGO and Chow Sang Sang reported unauthorised access to their systems, and Hong Kong Disneyland reportedly suspended online bookings after 17,000 e-tickets were stolen (reported in one listing; I could not confirm the details).
**Why it matters:** Stolen customer data fuels scams, and the privacy watchdog has recorded rising data-scam inquiries this year.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348662/Night-Recap-November-16-2026), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/342866/Privacy-watchdog-records-558-data-scam-inquiries-in-first-half-of-2026-up-16pc)

## 5. Green shipping tie-up with a Greek port [Business]

**What happened:** Hong Kong began a green shipping collaboration with a Greek port, SCMP reports.
**Why it matters:** It supports Hong Kong's ambitions as a maritime centre amid decarbonisation rules.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong)

## 6. Domestic helper visa rejections and online scams [Society]

**What happened:** Hong Kong rejected 455 domestic helper visa applications over suspected job-hopping, and a woman lost HK$1.3 million in an online hotel-booking job scam.
**Why it matters:** Both reflect pressure on household labour rules and the continuing scam wave.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348662/Night-Recap-November-16-2026), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong)
