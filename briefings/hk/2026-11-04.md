# Hong Kong Top 10 - Wednesday, 4 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Hong Kong's economy grew 5.2% in the third quarter and September retail sales rose 6.4%, while Beijing's He Lifeng opened the HKMA finance summit and October was confirmed the hottest on record.

## 1. Hong Kong GDP grows 5.2% in third quarter, 15th straight quarter of growth [Economy]

**What happened:** The advance estimate put third-quarter real GDP up 5.2% year on year, up from 4.2% in the second quarter and well above a 3.6% Reuters poll average. Goods exports rose 30.4% and fixed capital formation 7.3%, while private consumption rose 2.8%. Seasonally adjusted GDP rose 1.9% from the previous quarter, and growth for the first three quarters was 5.1%. Financial Secretary Paul Chan has said the 3.5% to 4.5% full-year forecast may be raised; revised figures were due on 13 November.
**Why it matters:** It is the fastest nine-month growth in five years and points to an upgrade of the official forecast.
**Sources:** [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/gangao/20261103/158e579d03b6466680aa4c3c5437f64c/c.html), [Dim Sum Daily](https://www.dimsumdaily.hk/?p=649463), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/hong-kong/hong-kong-economy/article/3372830/hong-kong-may-raise-gdp-forecast-growth-poised-5-year-high-paul-chan)

## 2. Retail sales rise 6.4% in September, 17th straight month of growth [Economy]

**What happened:** Provisional retail sales were HK$33.4 billion in September, up 6.4% from a year earlier and faster than August's revised 5.6%. Volume rose 3.9%. Online sales were HK$4.4 billion, up 12.0%. Electrical appliances and other durables rose 9.7%, while supermarket products fell 1.0%. Sales for the first nine months were up 8.3%.
**Why it matters:** It shows consumer spending and inbound tourism strengthening.
**Sources:** [Dim Sum Daily](https://www.dimsumdaily.hk/?p=649472), [Inside Retail Asia](https://insideretail.asia/2026/11/04/hong-kong-retail-sales-accelerate-as-local-demand-inbound-tourism-pick-up/)

## 3. He Lifeng opens HKMA Global Financial Leaders' Investment Summit [Economy]

**What happened:** At the fifth summit, Vice-Premier He Lifeng said by video that finance is the most important part of Hong Kong's "four centres and one highland" agenda and that Beijing would add measures as needed to strengthen the city as an international financial centre. About 280 financial leaders attended. John Lee said Hong Kong remains the nexus of global finance, and regulators outlined plans to expand offshore renminbi business and deepen mainland capital-market links.
**Why it matters:** It is a high-level signal of mainland backing for the city's role as a financial hub.
**Sources:** [HKMA](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261103-4/), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/article/3373105/vice-premier-he-lifeng-vows-support-hong-kong-international-financial-centre), [China Daily HK](https://www.chinadailyhk.com/article/642200)

## 4. Hang Seng rises 0.57% to 23,525.97 for a third straight gain [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index rose 132.65 points, or 0.57%, to 23,525.97 on 3 November on turnover of HK$236.40 billion. The China Enterprises Index rose 0.47% to 7,943.10 and the Tech Index 0.74% to 3,867.73. Gains in technology and consumer stocks outweighed renewed selling in AI hardware shares.
**Why it matters:** The index entered the US midterm election with a three-day winning streak.
**Sources:** [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20261103/0a2dca6afb04493092b0413ccfe3b2f9/c.html), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/347224/Hong-Kong-shares-close-higher-on-tech-gains)

## 5. Hong Kong logs hottest October on record [Weather]

**What happened:** The Observatory reported an October mean temperature of 27.6 degrees, 1.9 degrees above the 1991-2020 normal and a record for the month, beating the 27.4 degrees set in October 2025. The mean daily maximum was 30.5 degrees and the mean minimum 25.8 degrees, both October highs. The Observatory linked the heat to climate warming and a strong El Nino.
**Why it matters:** It extends a run of record warmth ahead of a cold front forecast for the following week.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/03/hong-kong-logs-hottest-october-on-record), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/hong-kong/health-environment/article/3372881/el-nino-weak-monsoon-behind-hong-kongs-warmest-october-142-years)

## 6. Baidu's Kunlunxin Hong Kong IPO may slip to 2027, Bloomberg says [Markets]
