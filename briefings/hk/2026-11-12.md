# Hong Kong Top 10 - Thursday, 12 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Cathay Pacific ordered 14 more aircraft and the Abby Choi trial heard a tearful accusation between brothers, while the Hang Seng fell 0.98% on rate worries.

## 1. Cathay Pacific orders 14 more aircraft [Business]

**What happened:** Cathay Pacific (0293) announced on Wednesday an order for 14 more aircraft: 11 widebody and three narrowbody, for fleet expansion and upgrade.
**Why it matters:** It signals confidence in long-term growth at Hong Kong's flag carrier.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348211/Night-Recap-November-11-2026)

## 2. Hang Seng falls 0.98% to 24,315.92 [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index closed at 24,315.92 on 11 November, down 0.98%, after closing at 24,557.04 on Tuesday. The tech index fell 1.41% to 4,027.06. Stronger US inflation and hawkish Fed signals raised rate-hike expectations. Biotech and EV makers weighed on the index.
**Why it matters:** Under the peg, US rate rises feed into Hong Kong borrowing costs.
**Sources:** [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-at-24-315-92-down-0-98-percent/70494407), [ad-hoc-news (10 Nov)](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-0-44-higher-at-24-557-04-points/70485793)
