# Hong Kong Top 10 - Wednesday, 18 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Hang Seng fell about 1% on tech weakness even as the economy posts its fastest growth in five years.

## 1. Hang Seng falls about 1% on tech selloff [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng closed around 23,508 on Tuesday with turnover near HK$198 billion, and the tech index fell about 1.1%. Alibaba and Baidu led declines. Reports differ on the exact close.
**Why it matters:** The index has lost about 844 points in the week to 13 November as AI-linked shares wobble.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/348767/Hang-Seng-falls-245-points-on-Tuesday-Alibaba-Baidu-down-5-percent), [Moomoo](https://moomoo.com/news/post/1004865413/etf-daily-report-nov-17-nvidia-earnings-window-approaches-hong), [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/hong-kong-stock-exchange-today-hang-seng-index-drops-1-55-to-23-637.60-as-tech-index-sinks-3-on-nvidia-fallout)

## 2. Q3 GDP up 5.2%, forecast raised [Economy]

**What happened:** GDP grew 5.2% year on year in Q3, the 15th straight quarter of expansion, driven by AI-related exports. The government raised its 2026 forecast to 4.8%.
**Why it matters:** It is the fastest growth in five years for the first three quarters.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/347363/HK-economy-grows-at-fastest-pace-in-five-years-for-first-three-quarters-Q3-GDP-up-52pc), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/hong-kong-economy/article/3373121/hong-kong-records-15th-straight-quarter-growth-gdp-rises-52)

## 3. Tai Po fire reforms after inquiry report [Government / Society]

**What happened:** After the inquiry report into the November 2025 Wang Fuk Court fire, which killed 168 people, the government apologised and admitted decades of failure. Housing chief Winnie Ho resigned, and it plans to criminalise bid-rigging and tighten fire-safety law.
**Why it matters:** The reforms are the largest regulatory change from the disaster, a year on.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/hong-kong/society/article/3369874/hong-kong-prepares-reforms-accountability-measures-ahead-tai-po-fire-report), [China Daily HK](https://www.chinadailyhk.com/article/641968)

## 4. Education (Amendment) Bill 2026 to be gazetted [Government]

**What happened:** The bill would require practising certificates for all teachers and bar people convicted of national security offences from teaching.
**Why it matters:** It changes entry rules for the teaching profession.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348346/Night-Recap-November-12-2026)
