# Hong Kong Top 10 - Saturday, 21 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Hang Seng sank to its lowest since April 2025 as Hong Kong heads into Sunday's Election Committee polls.

*Editor's note: Hong Kong coverage was very thin in today's searches, so only 3 items are listed.*

## 1. Hang Seng closes at 22,410.80, lowest since April 2025 [Economy and markets]

**What happened:** The index fell 430.82 points (1.89%) on Friday and dropped 5.19% on the week. It briefly reached bear-market territory (20% below its January peak) intraday. The Hang Seng Tech index fell 3.78%, with tech and chip stocks leading and HSBC down 3.3%.
**Why it matters:** The slide erodes household and pension wealth and chills IPOs and the property outlook.
**Sources:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/hong-kong-stocks-hang-seng-hsi-hkex-2026-ai-selloff-bear-market), [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/hong-kong-stock-exchange-hang-seng-index-slides-to-22-410-80-amid-us-iran-negotiations)

## 2. Election Committee subsector polls on Sunday [Politics]

**What happened:** On 22 November, 9am-6pm, voters pick members in 36 subsectors, with 1,115 hopefuls for 982 elected seats. The 1,500-member committee will choose the next chief executive in March.
**Why it matters:** It is the first step toward the 2027 leadership choice.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/336635/Election-Committee-subsector-elections-set-for-Nov-22), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/politics/article/3370090/more-300-hopefuls-sign-hong-kongs-election-committee-poll)

## 3. Exchange Fund Notes tender on 24 November [Economy]

**What happened:** The HKMA will tender 2-year Exchange Fund Notes on Tuesday, with settlement on 25 November.
**Why it matters:** The tender is a gauge of local liquidity and rates.
**Sources:** [HKMA](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261113-4/)
