# Hong Kong Top 10 - Monday, 9 November 2026

**Today in one line:** About 30 defective columns at the Tung Chung West MTR station and a 28-arrest bid-rigging sweep dominated the weekend, while a strong cold front arrived and the Hang Seng fell 1.27% on Friday.

## 1. About 30 defective pillars found at Tung Chung West station [Transport]

**What happened:** About 30 concrete pillars at the Tung Chung West station, under construction, were found defective. The Buildings Department said it first learned of a defective pillar in early October and that the situation does not pose an immediate danger. The Highways Department said the contractor tried repairs by an unapproved method, and MTR told the main contractor to stop work. The Transport and Logistics Bureau ordered MTR to submit a report within two weeks. MTR said it would take firm action against the contractor. Engineers and lawmakers called the defects "unimaginable"; experts differed on whether it was unfinished work or unacceptable error.
**Why it matters:** It revives memories of earlier MTR construction scandals and tests the oversight of the 2029 opening target, which the government says is unchanged.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347832/30-defective-pillars-found-at-Tung-Chung-West-station-as-MTR-ordered-to-report-within-two-weeks), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347998/MTR-to-take-firm-action-against-contractor-over-defective-Tung-Chung-West-station-pillars), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/society/article/3373705/defects-hong-kong-mtr-station-unimaginable-engineers-and-lawmakers-say)

## 2. Task force arrests 28 in first bid-rigging operation after Tai Po fire [Courts]

**What happened:** A joint task force of police, the ICAC and the Competition Commission arrested 28 people on Friday, including directors and staff of at least six contractors and 10 consultant firms. A suspected triad-linked syndicate allegedly obtained 12 works contracts at residential estates. Security chief Chris Tang said a 24-hour reporting hotline had been set up. A further 38 arrests reported by SCMP in a vice and gambling raid are single-source (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It is the first operation of the task force set up after the Wang Fuk Court fire report and signals tougher enforcement on renovation contracts.
**Sources:** [The Star (RTHK)](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/2026/11/07/hong-kong-arrests-28-in-building-bid-rigging-probe-rthk-says), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/law-and-crime/article/3373575/hong-kong-authorities-arrest-28-crackdown-bid-rigging-after-tai-po-fire)

## 3. John Lee on Beijing and Shanghai trip to deepen mainland ties [Politics]

**What happened:** The Chief Executive was on a four-day Beijing and Shanghai visit. In Beijing he spoke at the 29th Beijing-Hong Kong Economic Cooperation Symposium on the Northern Metropolis; in Shanghai he attended the China International Import Expo and the Hongqiao forum, and said the government is pushing for a "brother port" with Shanghai this year (exact end date of trip unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It shows the administration's economic push toward mainland integration and Chinese firms going global.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/politics/article/3373225/john-lee-begins-beijing-shanghai-trip-deepen-mainland-economic-ties), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347544/John-Lee-eyes-deeper-trade-ties-with-mainland-overseas-on-Shanghai-trip)

## 4. Hang Seng falls 1.27% on Friday to 24,481.86 [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index fell 1.27%, or 315.12 points, to 24,481.86 on 6 November, on turnover of HK$247.428 billion. The Hang Seng China Enterprises Index fell 1.13% to 8,274.10 and the Tech Index fell 1.83% to 4,078.23.
**Why it matters:** It sets the baseline for the week, with APEC in Shenzhen and US inflation data ahead.

## 5. Strong cold front to cut temperatures by 9 degrees from Monday [Weather]

**What happened:** The Observatory said a strong cold front would reach Hong Kong on Monday, dropping urban temperatures to around 16 degrees by Tuesday, lower than an earlier forecast of 18.
**Why it matters:** It affects commuters, outdoor events and demand for health services.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347816/Hong-Kong-temperatures-to-drop-9-degrees-as-strong-cold-front-arrives-Mon), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/weather/hong-kong/article/3373687/winters-knocking-cold-front-slash-hong-kong-temperatures-16-degrees)

## 6. Claire Liu beats Alex Eala 6-3, 6-0 in Hong Kong Open final [Sport]

**What happened:** The world No. 102 won the last nine games to beat second seed Eala on Sunday, taking her first WTA singles title. Liu dropped no set all week and won 88% of first serves.
**Why it matters:** It closes the city's WTA tournament (31 October to 8 November) with a surprise champion.
**Sources:** [WTA](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4606250/liu-reels-off-nine-straight-games-to-beat-eala-for-hong-kong-title), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/sport/tennis/article/3373695/hong-kong-blow-alexandra-eala-american-claire-liu-ends-title-wait)
