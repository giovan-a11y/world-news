# Hong Kong Top 10 - Sunday, 22 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Hang Seng sank to its lowest close since April 2025 on the AI selloff while the government marked Xi's APEC address and a cold snap was forecast.

*Note: search coverage was thin; this list holds the 6 items that could be sourced.*

## 1. Hang Seng nears bear market [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index fell 1.89% to 22,410.80 on 20 November, its fourth straight loss and lowest close since 30 April 2025, down 5.19% on the week. The Hang Seng Tech Index dropped 3.78% to 3,511.14, about 20% off its January peak.
**Why it matters:** Hong Kong's tech-heavy market is being hit by global doubts about AI spending returns.
**Sources:** [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/11/21/hong-kong-stocks-sink-1-89-as-hang-seng-enters-bear-market/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/hong-kong-stocks-hang-seng-hsi-hkex-2026-ai-selloff-bear-market)

## 2. Alibaba rises on earnings against the tide [Business]
