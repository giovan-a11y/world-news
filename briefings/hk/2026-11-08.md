# Hong Kong Top 10 - Sunday, 8 November 2026

**Today in one line:** About 30 defective pillars at the Tung Chung West MTR station and a 28-arrest bid-rigging sweep led Saturday's news, while Alex Eala reached the Hong Kong Open final and a strong cold front was forecast.

## 1. About 30 defective pillars found at Tung Chung West station [Transport]

**What happened:** About 30 pillars at the Tung Chung West station, under construction, were found defective. The Buildings Department said an engineer first found a defective pillar in early October and that there was no immediate danger. The Highways Department said the contractor tried repairs with an unapproved method; MTR told the main contractor to stop work. The Transport and Logistics Bureau ordered MTR to report within two weeks. The 2029 opening target is unchanged.
**Why it matters:** It raises questions about construction oversight on a new rail project.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347832/30-defective-pillars-found-at-Tung-Chung-West-station-as-MTR-ordered-to-report-within-two-weeks), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/transport/article/3373646/mtr-corporation-halts-unauthorised-remedial-works-station-after-column-concerns)

## 2. Task force arrests 28 in first bid-rigging operation after Tai Po fire [Courts]

**What happened:** A joint task force of police, the ICAC and the Competition Commission arrested 28 people on Friday, including directors and staff of at least six contractors and 10 consultant firms. A suspected triad-linked syndicate allegedly secured 12 works contracts at residential estates. A 24-hour reporting hotline was announced.
**Why it matters:** It is the first operation of the task force set up after the Wang Fuk Court fire report.
**Sources:** [The Star (RTHK)](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/2026/11/07/hong-kong-arrests-28-in-building-bid-rigging-probe-rthk-says), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/law-and-crime/article/3373575/hong-kong-authorities-arrest-28-crackdown-bid-rigging-after-tai-po-fire)

## 3. National security police arrest man over importing seditious books [Courts]

**What happened:** National security police arrested a 34-year-old man, a company director, on suspicion of importing seditious publications from Taiwan for local bookstores in October. Officers searched his home and company and seized devices, documents and books. The Star reports he is the person in charge of the prisoner support group Waiting Bird; The Standard cites sources calling him a member. Waiting Bird said more than 60 ordered books went missing in a Taiwan shipment and denied seditious intent.
**Why it matters:** It continues enforcement under the national security laws against publications and civil-society groups.
**Sources:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/07/hong-kong-police-arrest-operator-of-prisoner-support-group-over-allegedly-seditious-books), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347682/National-Security-Department-arrests-man-over-importing-seditious-books-for-local-bookstores)

## 4. Woman rescued from Tin Shui Wai flat fire; about 100 flee [Society]

**What happened:** Police were alerted at 5.39pm on Saturday to smoke from a flat at Locwood Court, Kingswood Villas, Tin Shui Wai. Firefighters rescued a 67-year-old woman, who was taken to hospital, and about 100 residents fled. A preliminary investigation pointed to aromatherapy items as the ignition source.
**Why it matters:** It is a fire-safety reminder in a city still reviewing the Tai Po blaze.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/society/article/3373665/woman-hurt-flat-fire-suspected-have-been-caused-aromatherapy-items)

## 5. No. 3 alarm fire at Yuen Long paper offerings warehouse; 12 evacuated [Society]

**What happened:** A No. 3 alarm fire broke out around 11pm on Friday at a warehouse storing joss paper and paper offerings in Pak Sha Tsuen, off Kung Um Road. Twelve people were evacuated and no casualties were reported; the cause is under investigation.
**Why it matters:** Such storage sites are a recurring fire risk in the New Territories.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347772/No-casualties-reported-after-paper-product-warehouse-in-Yuen-Long-hit-by-No-3-alarm-fire)

## 6. Eala beats Sakkari to reach Hong Kong Open final [Sport]

**What happened:** Second seed Alex Eala beat fourth seed Maria Sakkari 7-5, 3-6, 6-4 in a rain-interrupted semi-final lasting 2 hours 43 minutes. She meets American Claire Liu, who beat Alexandra Shubladze, in Sunday's final.
**Why it matters:** The final decides the title at the city's WTA event.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/sports-updates/article/347787/Eala-to-face-Sakkari-in-HK-Tennis-Open-semi-final-after-rain-hit-win), [WTA](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4606011/eala-tops-sakkari-in-rain-impacted-semifinal-to-set-hong-kong-final-against-liu)

## 7. Strong cold front forecast to bring temperatures down to 16 degrees [Weather]

**What happened:** The Observatory expected a strong cold front to cross the coast on Monday, with temperatures dropping to around 16 degrees in urban areas on Tuesday.
**Why it matters:** It marks the first sharp cooling of the season for commuters and outdoor events.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347816/Hong-Kong-temperatures-to-drop-9-degrees-as-strong-cold-front-arrives-Mon), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/health-environment/article/3373545/hong-kong-temperatures-fall-18-degrees-next-week-cold-front-hits)
