# Hong Kong Top 10 - Monday, 16 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Hang Seng ended a losing week on a tech sell-off as Hong Kong gets ready to host APEC delegates and a sold-out football night at Kai Tak.

## 1. Hang Seng falls 1.55% to end a weak week [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index closed at 23,637.60 on Friday 13 November, down 370.95 points, after a 1.26% fall on Thursday. It lost about 844 points over the week and ended below its major moving averages. The tech index fell about 3% on Nvidia-related jitters.
**Why it matters:** Hong Kong's tech-heavy market is exposed to the AI trade, with Nvidia reporting on 17 November.
**Sources:** [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/hong-kong-stock-exchange-today-hang-seng-index-drops-1-55-to-23-637-60-as-tech-index-sinks-3-on-nvidia-fallout), [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/hong-kong/stock-market)

## 2. Hong Kong economy grew 5.2% in Q3 [Economy]

**What happened:** Reports say Hong Kong's economy expanded 5.2% year on year in the third quarter.
**Why it matters:** Strong growth supports the case for resilience even as stocks wobble and US rates look set to rise.
**Sources:** [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/hong-kong-stock-exchange-today-hang-seng-index-drops-1-55-to-23-637-60-as-tech-index-sinks-3-on-nvidia-fallout)

## 3. Hong Kong prepares to host APEC delegates [Society / Politics]

**What happened:** Hong Kong is preparing for delegates transiting to the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen on 18-19 November, where a Trump-Xi-Putin meeting is expected.
**Why it matters:** It brings extra security, traffic and business attention to the city next door to the summit.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348090/Night-Recap-November-10-2026), [The Standard on the trilateral](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/world/article/341603/Kremlin-says-Putin-Xi-and-Trump-may-hold-a-trilateral-meeting-in-November)

## 4. Fed hike fears weigh on the HKD peg outlook [Economy]

**What happened:** Markets price possible US tightening, with FOMC minutes due 18 November. Hong Kong rates follow US rates under the peg.
**Why it matters:** Higher funding costs would hit mortgages, property and small businesses.
**Sources:** [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/week-ahead-fed-minutes-inflation-data-and-nvidia-earnings-to-guide-investors-202611131033)

## 5. Abby Choi murder trial continues [Courts]

**What happened:** Testimony continues in the Abby Choi murder trial, including Anthony Kwong's accusation against a sibling.
**Why it matters:** The case has drawn intense public attention and tests the court's handling of a high-profile trial.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348211/Night-Recap-November-11-2026)

## 6. LegCo passes crypto tax-reporting law [Government]

**What happened:** LegCo passed a law requiring crypto holders to report for tax purposes (reported 4 November). A bill prioritising land development in the Northern Metropolis is also in the headlines (details unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It aligns Hong Kong with global tax-transparency rules as it markets itself as a digital-asset hub.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347657/Night-Recap-November-5-2026)

## 7. Hong Kong ranks 11th in global digital and AI index [Technology]

**What happened:** Hong Kong placed 11th in the World Digital and AI Ranking 2026. Cathay Pacific also ordered 14 more aircraft.
**Why it matters:** Supports the government's innovation push and the airline's capacity plans.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348211/Night-Recap-November-11-2026)

## 8. Government water-contract fraud case [Courts]

**Why it matters:** It is a procurement-integrity case involving public money.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347657/Night-Recap-November-5-2026)
