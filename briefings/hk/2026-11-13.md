# Hong Kong Top 10 - Friday, 13 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The government is set to gazette a bill barring people convicted of national security offences from teaching, as the Hang Seng slid 1.26% on rate worries.

## 1. Government to gazette bill tightening teacher registration [Government]

**What happened:** The Education (Amendment) Bill 2026 would require teaching certificates for all teachers and bar people convicted of national security offences from teaching.
**Why it matters:** It changes who may work in schools and extends national-security rules into education.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348346/Night-Recap-November-12-2026)

## 2. Hang Seng falls 1.26% on Thursday [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index fell 1.26% on 12 November, after closing at 24,315.92 on Wednesday (down 0.98%). Stronger US inflation and hawkish Fed signals raised expectations of further rate hikes.
**Why it matters:** Hong Kong rates follow US rates under the peg, so higher US rates hit stocks and property.
**Sources:** [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/hong-kong-stock-exchange-today-hang-seng-index-drops-1-55-to-23-637-60-as-tech-index-sinks-3-on-nvidia-fallout), [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-at-24-315-92-down-0-98-percent/70494407)
