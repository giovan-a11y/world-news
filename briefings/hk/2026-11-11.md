# Hong Kong Top 10 - Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The government approved the Northern Link Part 2 agreement at an estimated HK$86.2 billion, as the Hang Seng edged up 0.44% and a winter tourism push takes shape.

## 1. Government approves Northern Link Part 2 agreement; combined cost HK$86.2 billion [Transport]

**What happened:** The government approved the Northern Link Part 2 agreement, bringing the estimated cost of the combined Main Line and Spur Line to HK$86.2 billion.
**Why it matters:** It is a major rail project underpinning the Northern Metropolis plan.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348090/Night-Recap-November-10-2026)

## 2. Hang Seng rises 0.44% to 24,557.04 [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index closed at 24,557.04 on 10 November, up 108.09 points or 0.44%. The tech index rose 1.08% to 4,084.45.
**Why it matters:** It was a modest gain after a volatile month, though rate-hike worries remain.
**Sources:** [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-0-44-higher-at-24-557-04-points/70485793)

## 3. Hong Kong plans its most extensive winter tourism campaign [Economy / Culture]

**What happened:** The city plans its largest-ever WinterFest, with a New Year's Eve fireworks show themed on its 30th anniversary. A Symphony of Lights is preparing for a final bow.
**Why it matters:** It supports tourism and retail spending into the year-end season.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348090/Night-Recap-November-10-2026)

## 4. Cold snap to last only days [Weather]

**What happened:** The Observatory said the chill would last only days, with temperatures climbing back to 29 degrees next week. A cold front dropped temperatures by about 9 degrees around 9 November.
**Why it matters:** It affects commuters and seasonal health demand.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348090/Night-Recap-November-10-2026), [The Standard morning recap](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348005/Morning-Recap-November-10-2026)

## 5. Juror discharged in Abby Choi murder trial [Courts]

**What happened:** A juror was discharged from the trial of Abby Choi's murder, leaving eight panel members as proceedings entered their 34th day in the High Court.
**Why it matters:** The case is the city's most closely followed murder trial.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348090/Night-Recap-November-10-2026)

## 6. Dozens rescued after Ocean Park observation tower breaks down again [Society]

**What happened:** About 40 visitors were stranded when the Ocean Park Tower stalled near its top (earlier reported 9 November recap); HKFP reported dozens rescued after it broke down again.
**Why it matters:** Repeated failures raise safety questions at a major attraction.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/hong-kong-news/), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347972/Night-Recap-November-9-2026)

## 7. Housing provider apologises over fire drill for Wang Fuk Court survivors [Society]

**What happened:** A housing provider apologised over a fire drill held for Wang Fuk Court survivors.
**Why it matters:** The insensitivity touches survivors of the Tai Po fire (single source, unconfirmed).
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/hong-kong-news/)

## 8. Independent book fair complains after exhibitors barred [Culture]

**What happened:** An independent book fair complained after exhibitors were barred under "force majeure"; it urged transparency.
**Why it matters:** It feeds the debate over cultural space in the city.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/hong-kong-news/)

## 9. Hong Kong prepares for APEC delegates transiting to Shenzhen [Politics]

**What happened:** Hong Kong is preparing for delegates transiting to the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen on 18-19 November.
**Why it matters:** It brings security, traffic and business attention to the city.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348090/Night-Recap-November-10-2026)
