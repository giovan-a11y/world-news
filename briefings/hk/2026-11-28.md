# Hong Kong Top 10 - Saturday, 28 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Hong Kong stocks jumped on Beijing's market-support pledge, while the first anniversary of the Wang Fuk Court fire keeps accountability and reform at the top of the agenda.

## 1. Hang Seng jumps 2.1% on China support pledge [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index rose 2.11% to 22,695 on 27 November, helped by the State Council's pledge of more state market support. Mainland and Asian markets rallied the same day.
**Why it matters:** It was a sharp rebound after weeks of AI-chip-driven weakness.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/27/asian-stock-markets-rise-after-earlier-tech-rout), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/china-chip-stocks-beijing-market-support.html)

## 2. Wang Fuk Court fire first anniversary [Society]

**What happened:** Hong Kong marked a year since the 26 November 2025 Tai Po fire that killed 168 people. Human Rights Watch said the government should ensure accountability and stop suppressing criticism; one report says police shut down a book launch of survivors' stories (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** The anniversary tests public trust in the government's response.
**Sources:** [Human Rights Watch](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/11/25/hong-kong-fire-anniversary-underscores-growing-repression), [HKFP](https://hongkongfp.com/2026/11/26/wang-fuk-court-fire-tragedy-why-hong-kongs-works-regulation-must-break-from-civil-service-culture/)

## 3. Government to approach 13 remaining Wang Fuk Court families [Housing]

**What happened:** Michael Wong said the government will approach the 13 families yet to sign next month. Reports say more than 1,730 owners have completed title transfers, and the estate is to be demolished with the future use undecided.
**Why it matters:** Rehousing and redevelopment are the practical next steps for survivors.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/349728/Govt-to-approach-13-Wang-Fuk-Court-families-yet-to-sign-next-month-Michael-Wong)

## 4. Fire inquiry reforms and accountability continue [Government]

**What happened:** The inquiry found systemic regulatory failings and the government announced sweeping reforms, replacing the Housing Bureau's Independent Checking Unit. Housing Secretary Winnie Ho resigned in October and Michael Wong took over her duties. The Public Service Commission is running disciplinary inquiries.
**Why it matters:** Oversight reform will affect building and fire-safety rules citywide.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/politics/article/3373076/beijing-removes-hong-kong-housing-chief-winnie-ho-after-tai-po-fire-resignation), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/346926/Structural-reform-announced-for-Housing-Bureau-following-Wang-Fuk-Court-report)

## 5. LegCo week: shipping tax concessions, Northern Metropolis aerospace motion [Government]

**What happened:** LegCo handled a bill on tax concessions for shipping-related activities and physical commodity trading this week, and backed a motion calling for aerospace development at the Northern Metropolis University Town.
**Why it matters:** The measures target Hong Kong's shipping and development ambitions.
**Sources:** [LegCo](https://www.legco.gov.hk/en/), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/349776/Night-Recap-November-26-2026)

## 6. Election Committee subsector polls passed smoothly [Politics]

**What happened:** The 22 November Election Committee subsector elections were reported to have gone smoothly, with turnout reported above 98%, and the Chief Executive welcomed them.
**Why it matters:** They shape the committee that picks the city's leader.
**Sources:** [DimSum Daily](https://www.dimsumdaily.hk/legco-and-chief-executive-welcome-2026-election-committee-subsector-polls/)

## 7. US tariff plan and Hormuz risks weigh on Hong Kong trade [Economy]

**What happened:** The proposed US tariffs of an extra 20% on China and attacks on Gulf shipping raise risks for Hong Kong's trade and shipping sector.
**Why it matters:** Hong Kong is a major trade and shipping hub.
**Sources:** [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/11/26/us-china-eu-tariffs-overcapacity/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/brent-oil-wti-hormuz-middle-east.html)
