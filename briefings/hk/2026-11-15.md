# Hong Kong Top 10 - Sunday, 15 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A theft of Disneyland ticket data, a coming police AI facial-recognition roll-out and a rail operator's cash warning lead a quiet weekend, after the Hang Seng ended a losing week.

## 1. Hong Kong Disneyland suspends online sales after 17,000 ticket QR codes stolen [Society]

**What happened:** Hong Kong Disneyland halted online ticket sales and hotel bookings after about 17,000 ticket QR codes were reported stolen (unconfirmed, single source summary).
**Why it matters:** It is a data-security and consumer-protection test for one of the city's biggest tourist attractions.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/)

## 2. Police to switch on AI facial recognition across SmartView network on Monday [Government]

**What happened:** The police chief said an AI roll-out across the expanding SmartView surveillance network would begin on Monday, and could help identify masked suspects and deter crime.
**Why it matters:** It raises privacy and oversight questions alongside the policing benefits.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/)

## 3. Rail operator warns of cash crunch despite Northern Metropolis land grant [Economy / Transport]

**What happened:** The city's rail operator warned of a cash shortfall even after receiving a land grant in the Northern Metropolis.
**Why it matters:** Funding for new rail lines underpins the Northern Metropolis plan and future fares.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/)

## 4. Hang Seng closes the week at 23,637.60 [Markets]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index closed Friday 13 November at 23,637.60, down 1.55%, as the tech index fell about 3% on Nvidia-related worries. It lost about 844 points over the week.
**Why it matters:** The tech-heavy market faces Nvidia earnings on 17 November and US rate worries.
**Sources:** [BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/hong-kong-stock-exchange-today-hang-seng-index-drops-1-55-to-23-637-60-as-tech-index-sinks-3-on-nvidia-fallout)

## 5. All five defendants in San Po Kong subversion case intend to plead guilty [Courts]

**What happened:** The court heard that all five defendants in the San Po Kong subversion case intend to plead guilty.
**Why it matters:** It points to a quick end to a national-security case and to sentencing ahead.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/)

## 6. Northern Metropolis logistics cluster draws 16 expressions of interest [Economy]

**What happened:** Authorities said on Friday 13 November that 16 submissions came from developers, logistics operators, industry associations and professional services providers. They were not named.
**Why it matters:** It is an early measure of market appetite for the Northern Metropolis plan.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong)

## 7. Migrant worker activists push back on visa rejections over "job-hopping" [Society]

**What happened:** Activists criticised visa rejections linked to what authorities describe as job-hopping.
**Why it matters:** It bears on the position of the city's large domestic and migrant workforce.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/)

## 8. Trump, Xi and Putin expected to meet in China [Politics]

**What happened:** The White House said a historic Trump-Xi-Putin meeting would be held in China, around the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen on 18-19 November.
**Why it matters:** It brings security, traffic and business attention to Hong Kong, next to the summit.
**Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press](https://hongkongfp.com/), [APEC China 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APEC_China_2026)

## 9. Weekend events and racing [Culture / Sport]

**What happened:** The Shi Fu Miz open-air electronic music festival ran on Saturday at Tai Tong Organic Ecopark in Yuen Long. Sha Tin held a full racing card.
**Why it matters:** Light weekend fare after a heavy news week.
**Sources:** [Sassy Hong Kong](https://www.sassyhongkong.com/hong-kong-events-weekend-activities-things-to-do-whats-on/)

## 10. Abby Choi murder trial in its sixth week [Courts]

**What happened:** The trial continued into mid-November, with Anthony Kwong accusing his brother Alex Kwong in testimony earlier in the week and one juror discharged, leaving eight.
**Why it matters:** The case keeps drawing intense public attention.
**Sources:** [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348211/Night-Recap-November-11-2026)
