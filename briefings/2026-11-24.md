# World Top 10 - Tuesday, 24 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The first Trump-Xi-Putin summit left big-power rivalries unresolved while the US-Iran standoff and Russia's war on Ukraine stayed hot.

*Editor's note: source access was limited today, so this edition carries 7 verified-enough items rather than 10; nothing has been padded.*

## 1. Trump, Xi and Putin hold first trilateral summit in Shenzhen [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The three leaders met for about 90 minutes on the sidelines of APEC on 19 November and issued a joint declaration on energy, nuclear risk and cooperation. It contained no binding agreement and did not mention the Ukraine war. Trump said all three agree Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon.
**Why it matters:** It signals a three-way great-power channel, with Xi cast as host and main winner, while the US secured no visible concessions on trade, rare earths or Iran.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/groundbreaking-trump-xi-putin-summit-five-key-takeaways), [CFR](https://www.cfr.org/articles/six-experts-on-what-trump-xi-and-putins-first-trilateral-means), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/apec-summit-trump-xi-china-trade-taiwan.html)

## 2. US-Iran confrontation continues around Hormuz and Bahrain [Conflict]

**What happened:** On 13 November Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed to have destroyed a US early-warning radar in Bahrain; US Central Command denied it and said it intercepted two drones. A Reuters summary reports Iran vowing to use all its tools against the US naval blockade, and new US Treasury sanctions on dozens of people and entities.
**Why it matters:** The blockade and tit-for-tat strikes threaten Gulf shipping and energy supply, and talks remain conditioned on sanctions relief that Washington has not accepted.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/iran-says-it-struck-us-radar-site-in-bahrain), [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-us-sirik-bahrain-war-nuclear-talks/33897951.html), [Reuters summary via Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4011043-reuters-world-news-summary)

## 3. Japan's Takaichi snubbed at APEC; Beijing demands Tokyo "correct" Taiwan stance [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** Takaichi was greeted at Shenzhen airport by a low-ranking official, did not hold talks with Xi, and Trump reportedly walked past her after the leaders' photo. Beijing then publicly demanded Japan correct its position on Taiwan. Takaichi says the Quad summit was the week's main outcome.
**Why it matters:** The China-Japan row that began over Taiwan remarks a year ago is unresolved, and Tokyo looks squeezed between Beijing and a distracted Washington.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/trump-xi-putin-photos-us-china-russia-trilateral-.html), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/20/tough-times-japans-takaichi-chinas-apec-summit/)

## 4. Quad leaders meet in Manila, announce AI and maritime initiatives [Security]

**What happened:** Trump, Takaichi, Albanese and Modi held the first Quad leaders' summit in about two years, announcing a first joint maritime patrol exercise next year and AI, cyber and maritime-security programmes. Reports differ on how strongly the final text criticised China.
**Why it matters:** It shows the Indo-Pacific grouping still functions, though with softer language toward Beijing than earlier drafts.
**Sources:** [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/?p=315368), [The Wire](https://thewire.in/diplomacy/first-quad-leaders-summit-under-trump-softens-criticism-china-maritime)

## 5. Zelensky warns of new Russian strikes; drone strays into Poland [Conflict] (unconfirmed)

**What happened:** A Reuters summary says Zelensky cited intelligence that Russia is planning new strikes, and that a Russian drone briefly entered Polish airspace during an overnight attack on western Ukraine.
**Why it matters:** Repeated airspace violations keep NATO's eastern flank on alert while diplomacy on Ukraine stays stalled.
**Sources:** [Reuters summary via Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4011043-reuters-world-news-summary)

## 6. Israeli strikes in Gaza kill at least three, including a UN agency worker [Conflict] (unconfirmed)

**What happened:** The same Reuters summary reports Israeli strikes killed at least three people in Gaza, among them a UNRWA worker.
**Why it matters:** Continued deadly strikes strain the fragile ceasefire arrangements and humanitarian operations.
**Sources:** [Reuters summary via Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4011043-reuters-world-news-summary)

## 7. Oil slides on supply-surplus worries [Economy]

**What happened:** Brent fell about 1.3% to roughly $91.95 on 18 November and US crude hit its lowest close since late August as inventories built; Brent and WTI lost about 2.5% in the week to 13 November.
**Why it matters:** Cheaper crude eases inflation pressure, even as the Iran blockade keeps a risk premium in play.
**Sources:** [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/world/40073656), [Nation Thailand (weekly)](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/world/40073488)
