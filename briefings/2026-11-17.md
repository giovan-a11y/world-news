# World Top 10 - Tuesday, 17 November 2026

**Today in one line:** An AI-led stock sell-off and rate-hike fears dominate markets, while Iran war tensions around Hormuz and a packed Shenzhen APEC week keep geopolitics on edge.

## 1. Wall Street slides again as AI stocks sell off [Markets]

**What happened:** US stocks fell sharply on Monday, led by Nvidia and other AI names, with the S&P 500 down about 1.9% to 7,416.59 for a third straight loss and the Nasdaq off roughly 2.5% (single AP-based report: unconfirmed). The 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 5.13%.
**Why it matters:** The slide extends the AI-valuation rout that began after the US midterms and puts weight on Nvidia's results this week.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/more-drops-for-nvidia-and-other-ai-chip-stocks-erase-wall-streets-rally-from-the-day-before/), [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-dow-jones-industrial-average-down-141-5030993)

## 2. Iran war and Hormuz: strikes, mines and stalled mediation [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The US carried out limited strikes near the Strait of Hormuz on the eve of the midterms, and mediators are working on a framework under which Iran would stop attacks in the strait in return for the US lifting its port blockade. Iran's Revolutionary Guard also says two tankers hit mines near Hormuz (not independently verified). Reuters reports Trump may hold off on new strikes absent fresh provocations.
**Why it matters:** The strait's disruption keeps oil, fertiliser and shipping costs elevated worldwide.
**Sources:** [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/u-s-strikes-strait-of-hormuz-hours-before-tuesday-midterms), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-negotiations-midterms/), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-war-us-signals-restraint-as-iran-warns-of-retaliation-1.500746947)

## 3. Fed hike odds rise after hot US inflation [Economy]

**What happened:** US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above forecasts, with core up 0.3% for a third straight month. Traders had priced a quarter-point hike at the 8-9 December meeting at over 80%, and odds of a half-point hike rose to about 14%.
**Why it matters:** A hiking cycle weighs on equities, bonds and emerging markets and complicates the Fed's independence debate.
**Sources:** [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2026/october-2026-cpi.html), [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/us-cpi-rises-october-2026/)

## 4. Nvidia earnings loom over the AI trade [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia is expected to report fiscal Q3 results this week (most sources say 18 November after the close; one says 17 November), with consensus near $2.47 a share. The report follows the early-November AI sell-off tied to data-centre spending worries.
**Why it matters:** As the biggest AI bellwether, its results and guidance could set the tone for global tech stocks.
**Sources:** [Earnings preview](https://news.ifyouspent.com/en/news/nvidia-disney-siemens-allianz-earnings-preview-november-2026), [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/a-brutal-sell-off-for-ai-stocks-sends-wall-street-reeling-and-the-sp-500-tumbles-44/)

## 5. Japan's Takaichi heads to Manila and Shenzhen amid China row [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi travels to the Philippines and China from 16 to 19 November for ASEAN meetings and the APEC summit in Shenzhen. Beijing is demanding she retract her remarks on Taiwan, and Kyodo reports she may be the only major-economy leader denied a sit-down with Xi.
**Why it matters:** Japan-China ties are at historic lows, with Chinese travel warnings and reported rare-earth supply cuts.
**Sources:** [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/11/13/japan-pm-takaichi-heads-to-china-for-first-visit-amid-deeply-strained-ties/243086), [Tribune.net.ph](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/16/takaichi-may-get-frosty-xi-welcome)

## 6. COP31 in Antalya enters its final days [Climate]

**What happened:** The UN climate talks run 9-20 November under a Turkey-Australia arrangement, with about 190 countries represented. Climate finance has been the first flashpoint, the US sent no official delegation, and a UN official warned Hormuz-linked fertiliser shortages could push millions into poverty.
**Why it matters:** The outcome tests whether climate cooperation can advance without the US in a year expected to be among the hottest on record.
**Sources:** [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/cop31-opens-leaders-pressure-tackle-climate-crisis), [Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/cop31-opens-amid-warnings-that-2026-will-be-among-hottest-years-on-record/)

## 7. APEC week: possible Trump-Xi-Putin encounter in Shenzhen [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Xi hosts APEC leaders in Shenzhen, and the Kremlin has said a trilateral with Trump and Putin may take place around 19 November (format and date unconfirmed). SCMP previews expect major deliverables to wait for the G20.
**Why it matters:** A first three-way summit of the nuclear powers could touch Ukraine, arms control and trade.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3371206/chinas-shenzhen-ready-host-xi-trump-and-putin-during-apec-summit), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3373969/xi-and-trump-set-apec-talks-major-deliverables-expected-wait-g20)

## 8. Pope Leo XIV ends first Latin America tour [Society]

**What happened:** The pope's 6-17 November journey through Uruguay, Argentina and Peru ends with his return to Rome on 17 November. Reports put attendance at an Argentine Mass at about 2.5 million, though sources differ on which event drew it.
**Why it matters:** It was his longest trip so far and the first papal visit to Argentina since 1987.
**Sources:** [Vatican](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/travels/2026/documents/uruguay-argentina-peru-6-17novembre2026.html), [Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/pope-leo-xiv-argentina-tour-mass-lujan-601188)
