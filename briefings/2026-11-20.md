# World Top 10 - Friday, 20 November 2026

## 1. Trump, Xi and Putin hold first trilateral summit, issue joint statement [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The three leaders met for about 55 minutes in closed-door talks on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen. Their joint statement said a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought, and pledged a dialogue on reducing nuclear risk and preventing proliferation. It also called for UN reform and warned international courts not to "abuse their jurisdiction". Trump has left China and is due to brief the press on Friday.
**Why it matters:** It is the first joint meeting of the leaders of the three largest nuclear powers. Whether it leads to formal arms-control talks is the open question; coverage did not confirm that Iran was part of the statement.
**Sources:** [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/trump-xi-putin-apec-meeting-9.7427718), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/live-blog/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-congress-2026-live-updates-rcna609888), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/trump-meets-putin-and-xi-ahead-of-us-russia-china-trilateral-talks)

## 2. Wall Street suffers its sixth straight loss as AI stocks slide [Markets]

**What happened:** On Thursday the S&P 500 fell 2.0% to 7,175.83, its longest losing streak in about two and a half years. The Dow dropped 1,101 points (2.2%) to 49,097 and the Nasdaq fell 2.25%. Nvidia lost 3.3%, erasing an early gain of 4.7%, even after an upbeat revenue forecast.
**Why it matters:** The AI trade that has driven global equities is under strain, and rising bond yields are adding pressure.
**Sources:** [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-nov-19-dow-plunges-1-100-points-as-the-s-and-p-500-suffers-its-6th-straight-day-of-losses/), [Business Journal](https://thebusinessjournal.com/dow-plunges-1101-points-ai-stocks-wall-street/), [Reuters via Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-street-ends-sharply-lower-asanthropics-debut-fails-to-revive-ai-fervor-5040108)

## 3. Anthropic's record IPO debut fails to lift AI sentiment [Technology]

**What happened:** Anthropic listed on Nasdaq on Thursday after filing to raise up to about $64.4 billion at a valuation reported near $965 billion. Reuters reported an early spike in the share price faded and the debut did not revive enthusiasm for AI stocks.
**Why it matters:** It is among the largest listings ever and a test of investor appetite for AI valuations. Closing first-day figures were not available to us.
**Sources:** [Reuters via Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-street-ends-sharply-lower-asanthropics-debut-fails-to-revive-ai-fervor-5040108), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-files-ipo-openai-trillion-164155247.html)

## 4. Nvidia leadership change unsettles chip sector [Technology]

**What happened:** Reports this week say Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO, hitting AI shares on Monday (Nasdaq down about 2.5%). Nvidia has since reported results and guidance that did not stop the shares falling for a sixth session.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the bellwether for AI-infrastructure spending, so leadership uncertainty feeds directly into market risk. (unconfirmed beyond secondary reports)
