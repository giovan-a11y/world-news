# World Top 10 - Sunday, 22 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A violent AI-driven selloff pushed Wall Street and Asia into correction or bear-market territory despite Anthropic's record IPO, while diplomacy (Trump-Xi-Putin, Ukraine) and the Iran war's energy fallout kept geopolitics tense.

## 1. Global AI selloff drives US stocks into correction [Markets]

**What happened:** On Friday 20 November the S&P 500 fell about 3.7% to roughly 6,910 and the Dow lost about 950 points, both closing in correction territory, while the Nasdaq sank about 5% and erased its 2026 gain. Asian markets had fallen sharply earlier in the day.
**Why it matters:** It is the sharpest test yet of the AI-led rally that has carried global equities, and the VIX closed near 36, its highest since March.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [ABC News/AP](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/asia-shares-drop-ai-worries-echoing-wall-streets-138309523)

## 2. Anthropic's record IPO fails to calm tech [Business / AI]

**What happened:** Anthropic priced at $83 a share, raising about $81bn in the largest US IPO on record, opened at $111.50 and closed its first day at $97.17, up 17%. Nvidia, AMD and Micron fell the same day; Nvidia is also in turmoil after CEO Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November.
**Why it matters:** A blockbuster listing was not enough to restore confidence in AI valuations; valuation estimates vary widely by outlet.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/19/anthropic-starts-trading-in-the-us-after-blockbuster-ipo), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/383669/nvidia-amd-micron-ai-stocks-sink-anthropic-ipo-price)

## 3. Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The three leaders met on 19 November on the sidelines of APEC. They agreed that nuclear war cannot be won and spoke of boosting oil output and keeping the Strait of Hormuz open, but coverage describes few concrete breakthroughs and the declaration skipped Ukraine.
**Why it matters:** A rare three-way summit signals great-power engagement on nuclear risk and energy, yet it left Iran and Ukraine unresolved.
**Sources:** [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-summit/), [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12157687/trump-xi-and-putin-reach-agreements-on-oil-and-nuclear-risks/)

## 4. Iran war leaves energy markets permanently altered [Energy / Conflict]

**What happened:** The IEA's chief wrote on 18 November that he sees no credible path back to pre-war (February 2026) conditions in the Strait of Hormuz. Gulf crude exports recovered to about 15 million barrels a day in October, 82% of pre-war levels, but tankers were hit in the strait in mid-November. (The current ceasefire status is unconfirmed; reports conflict.)
**Why it matters:** Energy security and fuel prices remain a key driver of inflation and geopolitics.
**Sources:** [City AM](https://www.cityam.com/?p=2479597), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/04/14/iea-warns-of-historic-oil-supply-shock-as-iran-war-chokes-global-markets)

## 5. UN court finds Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya [Law / Human rights]

**What happened:** On 19 November the ICJ ruled in The Gambia v. Myanmar that Myanmar committed genocide in its 2016-17 military operations, the first time the court has found a state responsible for committing genocide. The ruling is final; compensation will be set later.
**Why it matters:** It sets a legal precedent for other genocide cases, though it does not by itself change conditions for the 700,000+ displaced Rohingya.
**Sources:** [Human Rights Watch](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/11/19/world-court-finds-myanmar-committed-rohingya-genocide), [OPB/AP](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/19/top-u-n-court-rules-myanmar-committed-genocide-against-rohingya/)

## 6. Ukraine talks stall as strikes on the Kerch bridge escalate [Conflict]

**What happened:** After US envoys Kushner and Witkoff met Putin on 12 November, Kushner said Russia is comfortable with NATO-like security guarantees for Ukraine, a claim not confirmed by Moscow. Ukraine repeatedly hit the Kerch Strait bridge and launched its biggest drone attack on Russia of the war; Kyiv rejects ceding Donbas.
**Why it matters:** Peace efforts continue but positions remain far apart and fighting is intensifying.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/ukraine-holds-peace-talks-with-us-europe-as-conflict-escalates), [ADN/AP](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/11/19/kushner-says-russia-is-comfortable-with-nato-like-security-guarantees-for-ukraine-to-end-war/)

## 7. Asian markets suffer worst week since 2020 [Markets]

**What happened:** The Nikkei 225 fell 5.06% to 56,436.65 (about 22% below its June record), Kospi dropped 5.2% to 4,869.96 and the Hang Seng slid 1.89% to 22,410.80, down 5.19% on the week. SoftBank and chip names led the losses.
**Why it matters:** Asia's tech-heavy indexes are the most exposed to AI spending doubts, spreading the selloff globally.
**Sources:** [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/19/nikkei-225-falls-4-5-and-softbank-plunges-15-as-wall-streets-ai-selloff-puts-asian-stocks-on-track-for-their-worst-week-since-2020/), [Business Today Malaysia](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/11/21/hong-kong-stocks-sink-1-89-as-hang-seng-enters-bear-market/)

## 8. Poland mourns Krakow shooting victims [Society]

**What happened:** A 24-year-old gunman killed six people and wounded about 15 in Krakow's Main Market Square on 7 November; officials called it an act of terror and said he was fascinated by far-right ideologies. (Older than 24 hours; included as ongoing coverage.)
**Why it matters:** It is one of Europe's deadliest recent shootings and has put political extremism in Poland in focus.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/casualties-reported-after-shooting-in-polands-krakow), [Devdiscourse/Reuters](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4003438-poland-in-shock-after-six-die-in-krakow-mass-shooting)
