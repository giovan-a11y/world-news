# World Top 10 - Thursday, 19 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Leaders of the US, China and Russia converge on Shenzhen for APEC while Iran widens attacks on Gulf states and a tech-led market sell-off tests investors.

## 1. Trump, Xi and Putin gather in Shenzhen for APEC [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Trump arrived in China for the APEC summit and was met at the airport by Xi in a rare gesture. The three leaders attended a welcome banquet on Wednesday, with an unprecedented trilateral meeting planned for Thursday.
**Why it matters:** It is the first joint meeting of the leaders of the three biggest nuclear powers, with Iran and Ukraine expected on the agenda. Outcomes were not yet reported at time of writing.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/trump-xi-putin-apec-shenzhen-china.html), [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/politics/international-relations/apec/apec-live-banquet-brings-trump-and-putin-xi-and-takaichi-together), [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/17/politics/live-news/trump-visit-china-philippines)

## 2. Iran widens attacks on Gulf neighbours [Conflict]

**What happened:** Iran fired on a US air base in Kuwait and several times at Bahrain on Wednesday, apparently in retaliation for US strikes the day before. A US official said US jets hit a missile launch site on Qeshm Island.
**Why it matters:** The fighting threatens Hormuz shipping and energy supply, and comes as diplomacy runs in parallel at APEC.
**Sources:** [AP via MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/news/iran-gulf-attacks-firing-on-u-s-air-base-kuwait-and-targets-bahrain), [Foreign Policy via Newscord](https://newscord.org/article/trump-quietly-loosens-iran-blockade-as-us-iran-standoff-persists-foreign-policy--Story_20261118_Trumpistryingtoputth8da33603)

## 4. AI sell-off hits global stocks before Nvidia results [Markets]

**What happened:** The Dow fell about 850 points (roughly 1.6%) on Tuesday and the S&P 500 dropped for a fifth session, to its lowest close since late July. Nvidia then reported record revenue and profit, which eased some worries, though shares of other AI names stayed volatile.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the bellwether for AI-infrastructure spending; leadership uncertainty adds risk.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-november-17-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-nvidia-earnings-retail-sales-143848454.html)
