# World Top 10 - Sunday, 08 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A week of Democratic midterm gains, renewed US strikes in the Strait of Hormuz and a violent AI-stock sell-off ended with Wall Street mixed after a hot US jobs report.

## 1. Trump says "no oil going to Iran" as US strikes continue near Hormuz [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** On 7 November Trump said the US blockade has full control of the Strait of Hormuz and that no oil is reaching Iran. US Central Command said it struck a St. Kitts-flagged cargo ship that tried to break the blockade in the Gulf of Oman, and Trump confirmed limited strikes on an Iranian air-defence site on Qeshm Island late on 6 November. Iranian media said the IRGC had lifted its self-imposed 2,000 km missile-range limit.
**Why it matters:** The Gulf standoff keeps energy markets on edge and the risk of wider war high, even as Qatar pushes an interim deal.
**Sources:** [Sahara Reporters](https://saharareporters.com/2026/11/07/trump-insists-strait-hormuz-blockade-claims-nothing-going-iran-no-oil-going-there), [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/trump-confirms-new-us-strikes-on-irans-qeshm-island), [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-live-blog-trump-middle-east/33803414.html)

## 2. Democrats take Congress; Republicans blame the Iran war [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats won the House and Senate in the 3 November midterms, with record turnout of more than 124 million ballots and Hakeem Jeffries set to become Speaker in January. Vice President Vance said Republicans lost because of the Iran war, and Trump said he would veto Democratic bills. Some Senate races, such as Alaska and Mississippi, were still being finalised.
**Why it matters:** Divided government limits Trump's agenda, including war funding, and raises pressure for a diplomatic exit from the Iran conflict.
**Sources:** [WSOC/Cox Media](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/trending/election-2026-democrats-capture-both-houses-congress/5ILOWTVABVDPZFYP7VBBCX4EWE/), [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/4791815/takeaways-2026-midterm-elections/)

## 3. US adds 165,000 jobs; Wall Street ends a rough week mixed [Economy]

**What happened:** October payrolls rose 165,000 against about 65,000 expected, and unemployment fell to 4.1%. On Friday the S&P 500 closed at 7,376.86 (+0.31%), the Nasdaq at 24,579.42 (+1.09%) and the Dow at 51,285.73 (-0.46%). For the week the S&P 500 lost 2.7%, the Dow 0.9% and the Nasdaq 3.4%.
**Why it matters:** A strong labour market keeps pressure on the Fed to keep rates high at a time of war-driven inflation.
**Sources:** [Hargreaves Lansdown](https://www.hl.co.uk/shares/stock-market-news/market-reports/us-close-tech-stocks-rebound-after-sell-off,-dow-slips-after-jobs-report), [EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/U-S-Adds-165-000-Jobs-in-October-as-Wall-Street-Ends-Week-Lower-1772683)

## 4. AI and chip stocks whipsaw after Thursday's rout [Technology]

**What happened:** After a Thursday sell-off, reported to have taken the Nasdaq down more than 5% amid fears of tighter AI regulation under a Democratic Congress, tech stocks rebounded on Friday. Sources differ on the exact size of Thursday's fall, and the Nasdaq entered a technical correction from its October high.
**Why it matters:** Heavy AI-related valuations now drive US indexes, so policy and earnings surprises move global markets.
**Sources:** [Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824763), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3025392/stock-market-news-for-nov-6-2026)

## 5. Oil swings on Iran diplomacy; Brent near the low $90s to high $90s [Energy]

**What happened:** Brent fell sharply earlier in the week on hopes a Democratic Congress will push an end to the war, then rebounded as talks stalled. One analyst note put Brent down about 18% in a week from highs above $110; Brent settled near $97.79 on Thursday and WTI near $90.18 (single source).
**Why it matters:** Fuel prices feed inflation and political pressure on the White House.
**Sources:** [Panda Forecast](https://pandaforecast.com/crypto-news/bitcoin-midterm-bounce-chip-rout-liquidations-etf-outflows/), [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/index%2ephp/originals/2026/11/gulf-markets-gain-oil-falls-democrats-win-congress) (unconfirmed)

## 6. Record Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak grows in DR Congo [Health]

**What happened:** WHO's 6 November bulletin, with data to 3 November, counted 10,668 confirmed cases and 5,198 deaths, including 1,463 new cases in 21 days, mostly in Ituri and North Kivu. It is the second-largest Ebola outbreak on record.
**Why it matters:** Continued spread in eastern Congo and cases in neighbouring countries strain health systems and raise regional risk.
**Sources:** [WHO](https://who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON618)

## 7. US-Russia-Ukraine talks set for Abu Dhabi [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The Kremlin confirmed Russia will join US-mediated trilateral talks with Ukraine in Abu Dhabi on 10-11 November, the first such round in months. Trump also said Ukraine agreed not to strike Russian energy targets (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It is a rare diplomatic opening, though territory and security guarantees remain unresolved.
**Sources:** [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/us-attempts-new-peace-push-first-russia-ukraine-talks-nine-months), [FDD](https://www.fdd.org/overnight-brief/november-4-2026/)

## 8. Judge blocks Trump's triumphal arch [Politics]

**What happened:** Judge Tanya Chutkan issued a 14-day order stopping work on the planned arch near Arlington Memorial Circle, shortly after a federal planning commission approved it. She said construction would likely harm Washington and Virginia and may be unlawful.
**Why it matters:** It is an early test of legal limits on Trump's building plans.
**Sources:** [WLOX](https://www.wlox.com/2026/11/06/federal-judge-orders-14-day-delay-any-work-beginning-trumps-monumental-arch/), [Political Wire](https://politicalwire.com/2026/11/06/judge-blocks-trumps-arch-after-federal-panel-approves-it/)

## 9. Hang Seng falls on Friday but gains 5% on the week [Asia]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng Index closed at 24,481.86, down 1.27%, with the tech index down 1.83%, but it rose 5.10% over the week. Hong Kong also expects a strong cold front on Monday, with urban temperatures forecast to drop about 9 degrees.
**Why it matters:** Hong Kong markets are swinging with US rates, tech sentiment and Iran headlines.
**Sources:** [Xinhua](https://www.xinhuanet.com/20261106/e84e9fa5dde846c9b3eacfaa9ca7e983/c.html), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/347896/Night-Recap-November-8-2026)
