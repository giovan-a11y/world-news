# World Top 10 - Saturday, 21 November 2026

**Today in one line:** An AI-and-chip selloff rolled from Wall Street through Asia while the Iran war, Russia's barrage on Ukraine and a thin Shenzhen summit kept geopolitics tense.

*Editor's note: search coverage was limited today, so only the stories we could verify are listed (6 of 10); several figures differ between sources and are flagged.*

## 1. Global AI and chip selloff erases Nasdaq's 2026 gains [Markets]

**What happened:** US stocks reversed an early rally on Thursday, with the S&P 500 and Dow closing in correction territory and the Nasdaq giving back all its 2026 gains. Asia followed on Friday: the Nikkei fell about 5.1%, the Kospi about 5.2% and Taiwan's Taiex about 6.2%. Tighter US chip export licensing and doubts over AI spending were cited. Friday US closes were reported by one source only (S&P 500 about 6,910, Nasdaq about 22,618) and are unconfirmed.
**Why it matters:** The AI trade has driven global equity returns, so a synchronized drop hits retirement funds, chipmakers and export economies alike.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/20/asias-stock-markets-dive-as-anthropic-debut-fails-to-allay-ai-bubble-fears)

## 2. US strikes Iran's Qeshm Island as Hormuz talks stall [Conflict]

**What happened:** The US struck Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, which Washington called self-defense. Iran says it has no negotiations with the US and lists seven conditions, including lifting the naval blockade. Iran's navy claimed a third tanker hit a sea mine. Qatar and Oman remain the mediators, and the US and Israel are reportedly weighing a new assault.
**Why it matters:** The strait carries a large share of the world's oil, so each incident feeds energy prices and shipping costs.
**Sources:** [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-nuclear-oil-strait-of-hormuz/), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-nuclear-strait-of-hormuz/)

## 3. Russia hits Kyiv bridges and the power grid after Crimean bridge strike [Conflict]

**What happened:** Russia fired dozens of missiles (84 cruise missiles, almost all reportedly intercepted) and over 400 drones overnight, killing eight people, after Putin promised a "painful" response to Ukraine's strikes on the Crimean bridge. Road and rail bridges in Kyiv and energy sites were hit, causing widespread blackouts. The EU approved 1 billion euros in military support.
**Why it matters:** Attacks on power and transport ahead of winter raise the humanitarian cost and test Western support.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/russia-unleashes-retaliatory-barrage-on-kyiv-bridges), [ABC News (Australia)](https://abc.net.au/news/2026-11-20/ukraine-russia-crimea-strikes-bridge-kyiv-power-outage-poland/107411538)

## 4. APEC in Shenzhen: first Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral, few concrete results [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** Leaders of the US, China and Russia met together for the first time at the APEC summit. Chinese-leaning outlets describe a declaration that nuclear war cannot be won and a pledge to boost oil supply, while CNBC says tariffs, rare earths, Taiwan and tech disputes remain unresolved. Japan's Takaichi did not meet Xi.
**Why it matters:** The three powers talking directly lowers some risks, but without trade or war deals markets got little relief.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/apec-summit-trump-xi-china-trade-taiwan.html), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374977/world-leaders-gather-talk-trade-and-investment-apec-chinas-tech-hub-shenzhen)

## 5. Nvidia reports record results but is hit by CEO exit [Business and technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia said Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and director effective immediately; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and the board is searching for a successor. Shares fell about 13% on the news. Nvidia then reported record Q3 revenue of $115.8 billion and guided to about $130 billion, yet the stock still slid. Reasons for the departure were not stated by the company.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the bellwether of the AI boom, and leadership uncertainty adds to the selloff.
**Sources:** [Nvidia 8-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nov-19-222025646.html)

## 6. Anthropic lists on Nasdaq at $83 per share [Business and technology]

**What happened:** Anthropic priced its IPO at $83, above the $70-$78 range, valuing it at about $1.08 trillion on shares outstanding (about $1.5 trillion fully diluted). Trading began Thursday as ANT. Coverage said the debut did not calm AI bubble fears. First-day close was not confirmed.
**Why it matters:** It is one of the largest listings ever and a test of investor appetite for AI.
**Sources:** [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)
