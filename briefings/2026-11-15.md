# World Top 10 - Sunday, 15 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A tightening US blockade of Iran, a 5% US bond yield with a December Fed hike priced in, and Nvidia's CEO exit shook markets ahead of a Trump-Xi-Putin encounter in Shenzhen and Anthropic's record IPO.

## 1. US blockade of Iran tightens as Bahrain drone clash escalates tension [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** US CENTCOM says the naval blockade has turned away nearly 150 ships. On 13 November Iran's IRGC claimed it destroyed a US radar in Bahrain; the US denies any radar was lost and says it shot down two drones. Qatar and Pakistan continue to mediate, but Washington has called Iran's latest truce terms "outrageous".
**Why it matters:** The Gulf standoff keeps oil near $96 Brent and risks a return to open fighting in a region central to global energy supply.
**Sources:** [Euronews](https://euronews.com/2026/11/13/irgc-says-it-destroyed-us-radar-in-bahrain-after-attack-on-southern-iran), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-deal-negotiations/), [Israel National News](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/436040)

## 2. US 10-year yield tops 5% as a December Fed hike is priced in [Economy]

**What happened:** After hot inflation data, the 10-year Treasury yield touched 5% this week and was near 5.1% on Friday. Futures price a December Fed rate hike as fully expected, with Governor Waller reportedly open to 50 bp.
**Why it matters:** Higher US rates lift borrowing costs worldwide, strengthen the dollar and pressure equities and emerging markets; Hong Kong's pegged currency follows US rates.
**Sources:** [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/global-economy-briefing-november-13-2026/), [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/us-bond-yield-crosses-5-as-inflation-surges-december-fed-rate-hike-fears-rattle-global-markets), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-nov-13-2026)

## 3. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; Asian tech stocks tumble [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia told the SEC that Huang resigned as CEO and director effective immediately after a board investigation into an admitted affair; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. A HSBC downgrade extended losses on Friday, the Nasdaq 100 fell 1.2%, and the Hang Seng Tech Index dropped about 3%.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the AI-chip bellwether and a huge index weight, so leadership turmoil moves the whole sector ahead of its earnings.
**Sources:** [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/tech/article/3373724/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-affair-board-orders-investigation), [Investinglive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/asian-stocks-steady-as-nikkei-gains-1-4-hang-seng-slips-on-nvidia-leadership-fallout)

## 4. Anthropic nears record AI IPO [Business]

**What happened:** Anthropic filed its S-1 on 26 October to list on Nasdaq as "ANT". Reports say the offering could raise over $100 billion at a valuation possibly above $2 trillion. Pricing and first-trade date are not confirmed.
**Why it matters:** It would be among the largest listings ever and a test of investor appetite for AI valuations amid the Nvidia shock.
**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-officially-files-for-its-public-debut/), [Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/122417/ai-model-developer-anthropic-pbc-files-for-an-estimated-75-billion-ipo)

## 5. Trump, Xi and Putin expected at APEC in Shenzhen [Geopolitics / Asia]

**What happened:** Trump says he expects to meet Xi and Putin at the APEC summit in Shenzhen (18-19 November), and the White House reportedly says trilateral talks are planned for next week. China has confirmed discussions but appears lukewarm, and the format (joint or separate meetings) is unclear.
**Why it matters:** A first-ever three-way meeting of the nuclear superpowers, on Chinese soil next to Hong Kong, could shape arms control, Ukraine and trade.
**Sources:** [EWN](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/11/09/trump-says-likely-to-meet-putin-xi-in-china), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/11/6/what-a-trump-xi-putin-summit-would-mean-and-what-it-wouldnt), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3373292/what-one-big-thing-could-stop-xi-putin-and-trump-meeting-apec)

## 6. Ukraine peace diplomacy: Europe seeks a seat, Donbas still the sticking point [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Europe is reportedly settling on a five-nation team for talks with Moscow, and the Kremlin says it is open to a European role (unconfirmed). Zelenskiy says Washington floated Ukraine ceding Donbas in exchange for NATO membership; Kyiv refuses to cede territory, and earlier US outlines offered EU membership and investment for dropping the NATO bid.
**Why it matters:** The outcome decides Europe's security order and whether the war nears a settlement.
**Sources:** [Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/feature/2026/10/23/the-u-s-proposes-a-demilitarized-zone-in-donbas-kyiv-refuses-to-surrender-territory-putin-insists-it-was-russia-s-all-along), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/kremlin-signals-possible-three-way-meeting-at-apec-summit-between-putin-trump-xi/)

## 7. Record Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak continues in DRC [Health]

**What happened:** UK monitoring data show 10,532 confirmed cases and 5,126 deaths in the DRC as of 1 November, the largest outbreak of this virus species and second-largest Ebola outbreak ever. It remains a WHO emergency; Kenya reported an imported case in October.
**Why it matters:** Regional spread and a high fatality rate strain health systems and raise travel and containment concerns.
**Sources:** [UK Health Security Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/outbreaks-under-monitoring-in-2026/outbreaks-under-monitoring-week-44-week-ending-1-november-2026) (single source, unconfirmed)

## 8. COP31 climate talks under way in Antalya without the US [Climate]

**What happened:** The summit opened on 9 November and runs to 20 November, hosted by Turkey. Finance is the central issue, amid a hot year with a strong El Nino. The US sent no delegation after leaving the Paris Agreement.
**Why it matters:** Climate finance outcomes shape emissions paths and adaptation funding for vulnerable countries.
**Sources:** [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/cop31-opens-leaders-pressure-tackle-climate-crisis), [Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/cop31-opens-amid-warnings-that-2026-will-be-among-hottest-years-on-record/)

## 9. Hong Kong stocks slide 1.55% even as city lifts growth forecast to 4.8% [Asia]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng closed at 23,637.60, down 1.55%, and Shanghai fell to 3,634.35 on the tech sell-off. Separately, the Hong Kong government raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 4.8%, the best since 2021 per SCMP.
**Why it matters:** Local growth is firming, but US rate rises and AI-stock volatility are headwinds for the HKD-pegged market.
**Sources:** [Ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/marktberichte/hang-seng-closes-1-55-percent-lower-at-23-637-60-as-tech-stocks-slide/70504434), [The Standard](https://thestandard.com.hk/news/article/348454/Night-Recap-November-13-2026)
