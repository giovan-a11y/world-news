# World Top 10 - Friday, 06 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street suffered its worst day in over a year as AI and chip stocks collapsed, while the Bank of England raised rates and Washington absorbed a Democratic midterm sweep.

## 1. AI and chip rout hits Wall Street, Nasdaq enters correction [Markets]

**What happened:** On Thursday US stocks fell sharply, led by chips and AI names a day after a post-election rally. One recap has the Nasdaq Composite down 5.5% to 24,315.00 and the S&P 500 down 4.4% to 7,353.73, with all Dow components lower and a chip index down about 12%; sources differ on the exact figures. Nvidia fell around 8% and Qualcomm about 9% after a weak smartphone-chip forecast, while investors also feared tighter AI regulation under a Democratic Congress.
**Why it matters:** AI-linked stocks dominate US indexes, so the sell-off hits retirement savings and risk appetite worldwide.
**Sources:** [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3025392/stock-market-news-for-nov-6-2026), [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/stocks/nasdaq-100-suffers-worst-day-since-2020/), [Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824756)

## 2. Bank of England raises Bank Rate to 4% [Economy]

**What happened:** The BoE raised rates by 25 basis points from 3.75% to 4.00%, its first hike since August 2023, citing energy-price inflation from the Middle East conflict. Staff see inflation near 5% in the second quarter of 2027; most reports say the vote was 8-1, though one says 9-0.
**Why it matters:** A major central bank is tightening because of the war-driven energy shock, a warning for other economies.
**Sources:** [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-kingdom/interest-rate/news/598618), [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/bank-of-england-raises-the-bank-rate-to-4-00-as-expected-in-a-9-0-vote/), [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/53075/business/boe-lifts-rate-for-first-time-since-2023-to-curb-prices/)

## 3. Vance leads quiet push for a new Iran deal; Putin meets Iranian security chief [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Axios reports Vice President Vance has spent two weeks in indirect talks with Iran aimed at a ceasefire and resumed nuclear negotiations, including a letter to Iran's security council secretary Mohsen Rezaei. Vance said "progress" had been made. Iranian officials were sceptical, and Iran International says Putin met Rezaei in Moscow on 5 November.
**Why it matters:** Diplomacy is advancing alongside strikes near Hormuz, and Russia and China are positioning themselves as Iran's backers.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/06/vance-leads-push-new-iran-deal), [Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/world/news.php?id=2629268), [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611053453)

## 4. Democrats win Congress; Republicans blame the war [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats captured both chambers, with reports of a Senate majority near 54 seats and the biggest House majority in almost two decades. Vice President Vance said on 4 November that Republicans lost because of the Iran war, while Trump said he "did the right thing" and would veto Democratic bills. Hakeem Jeffries urged Trump to end the war before the new Congress.
**Why it matters:** Congress can now block war funding, including a $67 billion request, and investigate the administration.
**Sources:** [WSOC/Cox Media](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/trending/election-2026-democrats-capture-both-houses-congress/5ILOWTVABVDPZFYP7VBBCX4EWE/), [Argus Media](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2901877-trump-faces-new-limits-after-midterms-blowout), [CS Monitor](https://www.csmonitor.com/Daily/2026/20261105)

## 5. Oil whipsaws between peace hopes and stalled talks [Energy]

**What happened:** Crude plunged after the midterms on bets Democrats would push to end the war, then jumped on Thursday toward war-time highs as talks stalled. One report has Brent up 3.35% to $97.79 and WTI up 2.34% to $90.18, after a weekly drop of roughly 18% in an analyst note.
**Why it matters:** Fuel costs drive inflation and are a political issue after the election.
**Sources:** [JournalArta](https://journalarta.com/en/2026/11/06/wall-street-plunges-nasdaq-sinks-5-27-and-sp-500-falls-4-13-in-chip-rout/), [NPR via WUNC](https://www.wunc.org/2026-11-05/does-the-blue-wave-mean-lower-gasoline-and-diesel-prices-soon-dont-hold-your-breath)

## 6. Hang Seng jumps 3.67% in Hong Kong [Asia]

**What happened:** The Hang Seng rose 877 points to 24,796.98, with the tech index up 5.55% and turnover of HK$356.7 billion. The rally followed a down open earlier in the week as mainland and Hong Kong stocks rallied on the US election result.
**Why it matters:** It is one of the biggest one-day gains this year and shows investors rotating toward China assets as US tech slumps.
**Sources:** [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20261105/30dca13060c845e8aeb2926ad0009301/c.html), [Bastille Post](https://www.bastillepost.com/global/article/6329621-hong-kongs-hang-seng-index-closes-3-67-pct-higher)

## 7. Judge Chutkan blocks Trump's arch for 14 days [Politics]

**What happened:** US District Judge Tanya Chutkan issued a temporary order about an hour after the National Capital Planning Commission approved the arch near Arlington Memorial Circle. She found it likely to harm Washington and Virginia and may be unlawful.
**Why it matters:** It is an early court check on a signature Trump project.
**Sources:** [WLOX](https://www.wlox.com/2026/11/06/federal-judge-orders-14-day-delay-any-work-beginning-trumps-monumental-arch/), [NPR via WWNO](https://www.wwno.org/npr-news/2026-11-05/a-judge-blocked-construction-of-trumps-arch-at-the-11th-hour-whats-next)

## 8. Russia and Ukraine to resume talks in Abu Dhabi [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The Kremlin confirmed trilateral talks with Ukraine and the US in Abu Dhabi on 10-11 November, the first in months. Trump also said Ukraine agreed not to hit Russian energy targets (unconfirmed), while Russian and Ukrainian strikes on civilians continue.
**Why it matters:** The talks are the first real diplomatic opening in the war in months, though no ceasefire is in sight.
**Sources:** [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/us-attempts-new-peace-push-first-russia-ukraine-talks-nine-months), [FDD](https://www.fdd.org/overnight-brief/november-4-2026/)

## 9. Gold above $4,100 as Bitcoin slides [Markets]

**What happened:** Physical gold was fixed at $4,112.85 per ounce in London on Thursday, after earlier falling below $3,900. Bitcoin had been trading in the mid-$70,000s after a post-election spike above $81,000.
**Why it matters:** Havens and crypto are both volatile as investors weigh war, rates and AI valuations.
**Sources:** [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2631215), [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/gold-breaks-below-3-900-bitcoin-struggles-below-77k-as-markets-brace-for-a-deeper-downside-202611021017)

## 10. Sudan strike kills civilians in North Kordofan [Conflict]

**What happened:** A Sudanese army strike in North Kordofan reportedly killed two women and destroyed a water station, according to a single aggregated report. Sudan also filed complaints with the UN and African Union over alleged Ethiopian drone attacks (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Sudan's war continues to cause civilian deaths and a humanitarian crisis with little global attention.
