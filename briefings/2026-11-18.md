# World Top 10 - Wednesday, 18 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The Iran war grinds on with fresh strikes on Bahrain while AI stocks and rising bond yields rattle markets ahead of Nvidia's earnings and Anthropic's IPO pricing.

## 1. Iran strikes US radar in Bahrain as talks stall [Conflict]

**What happened:** Iran's Revolutionary Guard fired drones at a US military radar system in Bahrain on Tuesday, its third round of strikes on the kingdom, which says its air defences stopped them. A tanker crew was also reported missing in the Strait of Hormuz. Mediators Oman and Qatar are still talking to both sides.
**Why it matters:** The war is in its ninth month and a June memorandum has not produced a settlement. Qatar says the attacks undermine diplomacy, and Washington has sent a third carrier and more troops to the region.
**Sources:** [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/17/nx-s1-6044255/us-iran-war-latest-bahrain), [Reuters via Investing.com](https://za.investing.com/news/commodities-news/iran-attacks-us-military-radar-system-in-bahrain-despite-talks-with-mediators-4580662), [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/iran-live-updates-americans-tired-forever-wars-araghchi/?id=137967256)

## 2. AI stocks slide again; Dow drops 848 points [Markets]

**What happened:** Wall Street fell for a second straight session on Tuesday as Nvidia and other AI stocks swung lower. The S&P 500 was down about 1% and the Dow about 1.6% in the AP report. The 10-year Treasury yield was around 5.1%.
**Why it matters:** High yields and AI-valuation worries are hitting the stocks that have driven the market. The S&P 500 was on track for its first back-to-back losses of 1.5% or more since March.
**Sources:** [AP via BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/dow-jones/2026/11/17/world-markets-mostly-drop-as-ai-stocks-remain-under-pressure/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nvidia-26-142802182.html)

## 3. Nvidia reports first earnings after Jensen Huang's exit [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia reports fiscal third-quarter results after Tuesday's close, its first since Jensen Huang left as CEO on 12 November. Analysts expected adjusted earnings of about $2.47 a share on revenue near $108.9 billion. The stock was down about 26% over the past month.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the bellwether for the AI trade. I did not find the results themselves in time for this briefing.
**Sources:** [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/anthropic-ipo-jensen-huang-resignation-nvidia/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nvidia-26-142802182.html)

## 4. Anthropic IPO expected to price on 18 November [Technology / Business]

**What happened:** Anthropic plans a Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT, with pricing expected on 18 November. Reports give a range of about $70 to $78 or $80 a share, and valuation estimates of roughly $1 trillion. Nvidia and others have agreed to buy about $26 billion of shares in a private placement (sources differ on details).
**Why it matters:** It is one of the largest tech listings ever and a test of investor appetite for AI after a rough week.
**Sources:** [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/stocks/anthropic-lines-up-26-billion-from-nvidia-google-others-as-ipo-priced-at-70-to-80/), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/13/can-the-anthropic-ipo-save-the-ai-rally/)

## 5. Spain's first general strike in 14 years over housing [Society / Economy]

**What happened:** Unions said around four million workers joined a 24-hour strike on 11 November over rents and evictions. About 5% of flights were cancelled and police reported dozens of arrests. One report says a snap election is set for 29 November.
**Why it matters:** Housing costs have become a major political fault line in Spain, with the strike coming just weeks before the vote.
**Sources:** [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/11/11/workers-across-spain-stage-first-general-strike-in-14-years-in-anger-over-housing/), [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/article/spains-workers-hold-a-general-strike-over-housing-costs-ahead-of-a-national-election/)
