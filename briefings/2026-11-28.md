# World Top 10 - Saturday, 28 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Washington's new tariff proposal on China, the EU, Japan and 11 others, fresh attacks on Saudi refineries and Hormuz tankers, and Beijing's market-rescue pledge drove a violent rebound in Asian stocks.

## 1. US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners [Trade]

**What happened:** On 25 November the Trump administration proposed new tariffs on 14 partners: an extra 20% on China, 15% on the EU and Japan, and 10% to 20% on others including South Korea, Taiwan and Vietnam. Semiconductors, autos, steel and aluminium are excluded, and the plan goes through public comment before it is final. China said a trade war serves no one.
**Why it matters:** The plan is meant to replace revenue lost after the Supreme Court struck down the earlier emergency-law tariffs, and it reopens trade tension with Asia and Europe.
**Sources:** [ITV News](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-11-25/us-announces-new-tariffs-hikes-on-the-eu-china-and-others), [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/11/26/us-china-eu-tariffs-overcapacity/), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/trump-administration-to-raise-tariffs-on-china-eu-brazil-and-others)

## 2. Attacks on Saudi refineries and Hormuz tankers lift oil [Conflict]

**What happened:** Saudi Arabia said a projectile hit Aramco's Jazan refinery on Thursday, killing one worker, and blamed the Iran-backed Houthis, who also claimed attacks on Ras Tanura. A projectile struck a tanker off Oman, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two tankers hit mines in Hormuz. Brent traded around $94 to $97 and was set for its first weekly gain in November.
**Why it matters:** Strikes on Saudi export hubs and shipping lanes add to the supply risk from the US-Iran standoff.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/brent-oil-wti-hormuz-middle-east.html), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/11/27/oil-set-to-post-weekly-gain-as-middle-east-attacks-stoke-supply-concerns/), [RTE](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1127/1600877-world-oil-prices/)

## 3. Beijing pledges more state market support; Asian stocks surge [Markets]

**What happened:** After a State Council meeting on 27 November backed a bigger role for state funds, China's Shanghai Composite rose 3.73% and the Shenzhen Component about 7%. Chinese chip stocks had record one-day gains. South Korea's Kospi rose over 4% and Japan's Nikkei about 2%.
**Why it matters:** It followed a brutal AI-chip selloff and suggests governments are stepping in to steady equity markets.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/china-chip-stocks-beijing-market-support.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/27/asian-stock-markets-rise-after-earlier-tech-rout), [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/world/2026/11/28/china-stocks-surge-after-rare-midsession-pledge-to-steady)

## 4. Wall Street rebounds in shortened Black Friday session [Markets]

**What happened:** US markets closed at 1 p.m. ET. One report says the Dow rose 1.1%, the S&P 500 1.6% and the Nasdaq 2.3%, with tech rebounding after AI worries. Another outlet gave different figures, so the exact closes are unconfirmed.
**Why it matters:** It capped an erratic week of AI-driven swings.
**Sources:** [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/seekingalpha:e521c5511094b:0-u-s-equities-close-on-top-on-black-friday/), [Eurasia Business News](https://eurasiabusinessnews.com/2026/11/27/u-s-stock-market-today-november-27-2026-tech-rebounds-in-shortened-black-friday-session)

## 5. US-Iran talks remain stalled as blockade continues [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** The US military said it disabled a commercial ship trying to run its blockade of Iran, and an Iranian adviser said the strait would stay shut. Iran's top diplomat has said a tentative deal would require Israel to withdraw from Lebanon (single-source, unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Tehran's demands for lifting the blockade and sanctions remain the main obstacle to reopening Hormuz.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/brent-oil-wti-hormuz-middle-east.html), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/11/27/oil-set-to-post-weekly-gain-as-middle-east-attacks-stoke-supply-concerns/)

## 6. OPEC+ agrees in principle to hold output into 2027 [Economy]

**What happened:** OPEC+ reached an agreement in principle at its Sunday meeting to keep output targets unchanged for at least the first quarter of 2027.
**Why it matters:** It signals producers are not adding supply while Gulf infrastructure is under attack.
**Sources:** [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/11/27/oil-set-to-post-weekly-gain-as-middle-east-attacks-stoke-supply-concerns/)

## 7. Spain heads to an early general election [Politics]

**What happened:** Spain votes in an early national election on 29 November (reported in a Reuters summary), with the conservative People's Party leading polls. Spain's courts have cleared Carles Puigdemont to return without arrest, though his party says he will not return during the campaign. A YouGov poll found 45% of Spaniards see his return as bad news.
**Why it matters:** The result will shape Spain's government and the Catalonia question (election date unconfirmed beyond one source).
**Sources:** [Reuters via Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/3987950-reuters-world-news-summary), [ARA](https://en.ara.cat/politics/three-out-of-every-four-spaniards-regret-that-puigdemont-can-return-from-exile_1_5911482.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/6/catalan-separatist-leader-puigdemont-to-return-home-after-warrant-lifted)
