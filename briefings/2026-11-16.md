# World Top 10 - Monday, 16 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Tanker mine incidents near Hormuz and a possible Trump-Xi-Putin encounter in Shenzhen dominate a week that also brings Nvidia earnings, Anthropic's Nasdaq debut and the climax of COP31.

## 1. Tankers hit mines near the Strait of Hormuz [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Iran's Revolutionary Guard says two oil tankers hit mines near the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire, with one reportedly sinking. Reuters could not independently verify the claim. Iran also says talks with Oman on the strait's future are at the "final stage" (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Hormuz flows are far below pre-war levels, so each incident feeds oil prices and shipping insurance costs worldwide.
**Sources:** [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/middle-east/irans-navy-says-oil-tanker-on-fire-after-hitting-mine-east-of-hormuz-3012121), [A News](https://www.anews.com.tr/middle-east/2026/11/15/iran-says-2-oil-tankers-hit-mines-near-strait-of-hormuz-1-sinks)

## 2. Trump, Xi and Putin expected at APEC in Shenzhen [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The Kremlin says Putin, Xi and Trump will hold a trilateral meeting in Shenzhen on 19 November, during the APEC leaders' summit. Beijing had earlier appeared lukewarm and the format was not final, so treat the date as unconfirmed.
**Why it matters:** A first three-way summit of the nuclear powers could touch on Ukraine, arms control and trade, and it takes place next to Hong Kong.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/11/6/what-a-trump-xi-putin-summit-would-mean-and-what-it-wouldnt), [The Standard HK](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/world/article/341603/Kremlin-says-Putin-Xi-and-Trump-may-hold-a-trilateral-meeting-in-November)

**Why it matters:** Attacks on energy infrastructure and the stalled US-brokered talks shape Europe's security and energy outlook.
**Sources:** [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-russia-launches-mass-attack-on-odesa-oblast-as-ukraine-strikes-airfield-drone-launch-site-in-russia/), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/world/europe/ukraine)

## 7. COP31 in Antalya enters its final week [Climate]

**What happened:** Talks run 9-20 November under a Turkey-Australia co-presidency, the first UN climate conference since the US withdrawal from the Paris Agreement took effect. Bowen has acknowledged differences over ambition, and a health-resilience initiative has been launched.
**Why it matters:** The outcome will indicate whether climate action can advance without US participation in a year expected to be among the hottest on record.
**Sources:** [Earth Negotiations Bulletin](https://enb.iisd.org/antalya-un-climate-change-conference-cop31-daily-report-9nov2026), [Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/cop31-opens-amid-warnings-that-2026-will-be-among-hottest-years-on-record/)
