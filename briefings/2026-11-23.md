# World Top 10 - Monday, 23 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A violent AI-chip rout dragged Wall Street into correction territory while Middle East tension and post-summit nuclear diplomacy kept geopolitics on edge.

## 1. Nasdaq plunges 5% as AI chip selloff pushes S&P 500 into correction [Markets]

**What happened:** On Friday 20 November the Nasdaq Composite fell about 5.3% and the S&P 500 fell about 3.7%, closing more than 10% below its October record. Nvidia dropped roughly 14% and the semiconductor index about 10%, after Reuters reported an SEC accounting inquiry into Nvidia. The Nasdaq lost about 10% on the week, its worst since March 2020, per market-data summaries.
**Why it matters:** The AI trade has driven global equity gains in 2026, so a break in it hits portfolios, tech supply chains and Asian markets that open Monday.
**Sources:** [StockInvest](https://stockinvest.us/digest/digest-us-market-close-2026-11-20), [Cappnotes](https://cappnotes.substack.com/p/closing-look-friday-november-20-2026), [Quantisnow](https://www.quantisnow.com/articles/nvidia-worst-day-on-file-chips-drag-nasdaq-100-down-5-5-percent)

## 2. Trump, Xi and Putin agree to open nuclear-risk talks, with no timetable [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** After a trilateral meeting on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen on 19 November, the leaders agreed to dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing proliferation, and reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won. They set no timetable or concrete arms-control steps. Xi called the meeting fruitful, while critics called it largely symbolic.
**Why it matters:** It is the first meeting of the three nuclear powers' leaders on the subject, but without commitments the practical effect is limited.
**Sources:** [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3374977/world-leaders-gather-talk-trade-and-investment-apec-chinas-tech-hub-shenzhen), [IBTimes](https://mx.ibtimes.com/trump-putin-xi-nuclear-dialogue-4574)

## 3. US warns Iran over alleged "dirty bomb" plot; Tehran denies it [Conflict]

**What happened:** CNN, citing US intelligence, reported Iran may be planning a dirty-bomb attack in Washington. Trump said Iran would "pay a price the likes of which no country has ever paid" if it did. Iran's foreign minister called the reports "rubbish". Anadolu's briefing on 21 November said Trump had threatened military action.
**Why it matters:** The stop-start US-Iran conflict, including Bahrain and Houthi-Saudi incidents, could escalate and move oil prices.
**Sources:** [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/17/iran-will-pay-a-price-if-dirty-bomb-is-detonated-in-us/), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/us-iran-tensions-trump-warns-iran-major-price-tehran-denies-dirty-bomb-plot-bahrain-drone-attacks-1.500749924), [Anadolu](https://aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-21-2026/4132676)

## 4. Denmark expels Russian defence attache and curbs visas [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Denmark is expelling Russia's defence attache and halting non-essential visas for Russian citizens, according to Anadolu's 21 November briefing. I could not find a second source or the stated reason. (unconfirmed)
**Why it matters:** It adds to a pattern of tightening European measures against Russia.
**Sources:** [Anadolu](https://aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-21-2026/4132676)

## 5. Fed hike bets build as markets reprice [Economy]

**What happened:** Fed Chair Kevin Warsh has declined to commit to a December hike, but futures had priced a hike as likely, and one summary put the odds near 96% after Friday's rout while Treasury yields fell. The 10-year yield closed near 4.92% from about 5.04% a day earlier.
**Why it matters:** Rate hikes into an equity correction raise the risk of a sharper slowdown; the odds figures vary by source.
**Sources:** [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/the-sp-500-just-had-its-biggest-reversal-since-march-2020/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/much-ado-about-nothing-fed-chair-warsh-talks-up-a-storm-but-december-rate-hike-bets-barely-budge/)
