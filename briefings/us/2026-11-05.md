# US Top 10 - Thursday, 5 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Democrats swept the midterms to take Congress and a majority of governorships, Wall Street staged its best post-election rally in 44 years, and Trump answered the loss by showing off his ballroom.

## 1. Democrats win control of both the House and Senate [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats flipped seven Republican-held Senate seats, in North Carolina, South Carolina, Ohio, Texas, Kansas, Iowa and Maine, for at least 54 seats on early counts, and led the House 228-139 early Wednesday after gaining 19 seats. Texas's James Talarico defeated Ken Paxton, and Ohio's Sherrod Brown beat Sen. Jon Husted. Schumer called it a "generational remake of the Senate map".
**Why it matters:** Divided government from January shifts the balance on tariffs, war powers, spending and oversight, though Trump retains his veto.
**Sources:** [NPR via KUOW](https://www.kuow.org/npr-news/2026-11-03/democrats-win-full-control-of-congress-ushering-in-a-new-era-of-divided-government), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/2026-midterm-election-results-live-updates.html), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/04/senate-republicans-trump-midterm-elections)

## 2. Dow jumps 1,569 points in its best post-midterm day in 44 years [Markets]

**What happened:** The Dow closed up 1,569.40 points, or 3.03%, at 53,356.12, its best day since April 2025 and best post-midterm session since 1982. The S&P 500 rose about 1.6% to 7,688.62 and the Nasdaq 1.44% to 25,736.51. Brent crude fell more than 8% to about $94, its first close below $100 in nearly a month, on hopes of a faster end to the Iran war.
**Why it matters:** Investors cheered election clarity and falling oil, but the rally was short-lived as AI stocks sold off the next day.
**Sources:** [WTTW (AP)](https://news.wttw.com/2026/11/04/dow-has-best-post-midterm-elections-day-44-years-democrats-take-control-congress), [Honolulu Star-Advertiser (AP)](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/11/04/breaking-news/dow-surges-1575-points-as-democrats-win-control-of-congress/), [Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/news/business/2026/11/04/us-stocks-surge-and-the-dow-soars-more-than-1500-points-as-oil-prices-tumble-following-us-midterms/)

## 3. Trump answers the midterm rout by showing off the White House ballroom [Politics]

**What happened:** In his first post-election event, Trump spoke for nearly 20 minutes about construction of his White House ballroom and signed its last steel beam, rather than addressing the losses. Asked about the results, he said he was "a little bit surprised", blamed candidates and gas prices tied to the Iran war, and said he would "get along, probably" with Democrats while noting his veto.
**Why it matters:** Democratic control of Congress puts the ballroom's financing under scrutiny the White House has so far avoided.
**Sources:** [KSAT (AP)](https://www.ksat.com/news/politics/2026/11/04/trump-speaks-at-white-house-on-ballroom-construction-after-gop-midterm-losses-where-voters-said-he-didnt-focus-on-them/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/04/trump-ballroom-focus-2026-midterm-election), [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/livestory/us-midterm-election-results-reaction-live-updates-november-4-2026-9.7408406)

## 4. Vance says the Iran war is why Republicans lost [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** In a long post on X, Vice President JD Vance wrote "The reason we lost is the war," saying many 2024 Trump voters backed Democrats. Trump said "Iran was a piece of it" while defending the war. Exit polls cited by Axios found cost of living and government corruption were the top issues.
**Why it matters:** The rare public break is read as Vance positioning for 2028 and strengthens war-powers critics in both parties.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/04/jd-vance-iran-war-trump-election), [Daily Montanan / States Newsroom](https://dailymontanan.com/2026/11/04/repub/vance-and-trump-blame-midterm-gop-elections-loss-on-their-war-in-iran/), [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/america-votes/article/us-vp-vance-says-iran-war-is-the-reason-we-lost-midterms/)

## 5. Democrats win a majority of governorships for the first time in 16 years [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats are set to hold 28 governor's offices to Republicans' 20, with flips in Texas, Georgia, Iowa and Ohio, and races in Alaska and Kansas uncalled. In Texas, Gina Hinojosa defeated three-term Gov. Greg Abbott, and in Georgia, Keisha Lance Bottoms won 54.3% to 45.7%. Wisconsin elected David Crowley and Michigan Jocelyn Benson.
**Why it matters:** Democratic governors will shape redistricting, immigration enforcement and federal-state clashes in many large states.
**Sources:** [States Newsroom](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/democrats-win-majority-governorships-first-time-16-years), [AP via WRDW](https://www.wrdw.com/2026/11/04/democrats-press-win-majority-governors-offices-midterms-after-power-struggles-with-trump/), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/democrats-seize-majority-governors-offices-election-night-shift.amp)

## 6. Speaker Johnson will not seek House Republican leadership [Politics / Congress]

**What happened:** Johnson told staff he will not seek to stay on as House Republican leader in the next Congress, AP reported. Steve Scalise and Jim Jordan are running, with a Republican vote scheduled for 10 November. Democrats are preparing to nominate Hakeem Jeffries for speaker, with the full House vote on 3 January 2027.
**Why it matters:** The GOP leadership reshuffle comes as Republicans move into the minority.
**Sources:** [AP via WECT](https://www.wect.com/2026/11/04/democrats-usher-new-era-with-sweep-house-senate-johnson-wont-seek-reelection/?outputType=amp), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/midterm-election-results-2026-democrats-house-senate-trump/)

## 7. US strikes Iranian targets in Strait of Hormuz hours before the midterms [Security]

**What happened:** US forces conducted what officials called defensive strikes on Iranian surface-to-air missile sites in the Strait of Hormuz, and Iranian state media reported explosions on Qeshm and Hengam islands and near Bandar Abbas. Iran has offered to reopen the strait and resume nuclear talks if the US lifts its blockade and waives oil sanctions. Vance said he and Trump "feel pretty good" about a negotiated settlement.
**Why it matters:** Oil, the war and the blockade were central to the voter backlash and remain unresolved after the election.
**Sources:** [AP via OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/03/us-strikes-in-the-strait-of-hormuz-create-more-questions-about-whats-ahead/), [PBS NewsHour](https://pbs.org/newshour/world/u-s-strikes-strait-of-hormuz-hours-before-tuesday-midterms), [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12134747/us-strike-strait-of-hormuz/)

## 8. Mississippi and Alaska Senate races remain uncalled [Politics]

**What happened:** In Mississippi, Democrat Scott Colom led Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith by about 1,100 votes with counting continuing. Alaska's race between Democrat Mary Peltola and Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan was also still being counted, with an unusual twist from a second candidate named Dan Sullivan on the ballot.
**Why it matters:** The two races decide whether Democrats end with 54, 55 or 56 Senate seats.
**Sources:** [CBS News live updates](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/midterm-election-results-2026-democrats-house-senate-trump/), [ADN](https://www.adn.com/politics/2026/11/03/peltola-leads-sullivan-in-limited-early-results-in-hard-fought-us-senate-race/), [Alaska Beacon](https://alaskabeacon.com/2026/11/03/democratic-candidate-mary-peltola-leads-in-early-vote-counts-for-alaskas-us-senate-election/)

## 9. First College Football Playoff rankings put Miami on top [Sports]

**What happened:** The undefeated Miami Hurricanes received top billing in the season's first College Football Playoff rankings, released Tuesday evening. Utah and BYU were among the other teams in the top 10 heading into Week 10.
**Why it matters:** The rankings set up a run of high-stakes matchups in November, starting with Miami's trip to Notre Dame.
**Sources:** [AP News in Brief, 3 Nov](https://www.ottumwacourier.com/news/national_news/ap-news-in-brief-at-6-04-p-m-est/article_bb127fe1-c1d9-53d6-9988-bcffeb3f3645.html), [NCAA.com](https://www.ncaa.com/live-updates/football/fbs/college-football-week-10-live-updates-schedule-scores-cfp-rankings)

## 10. Election Day passes with few problems despite flooding, scanner glitches [Politics / Election administration]

**What happened:** Votebeat and AP reported a relatively smooth election, with fears of federal interference largely not materializing. Documented problems included a ballot-scanner failure in Westchester County, New York, a 20-minute delay in Broward County, Florida, and storm-related polling changes in parts of Florida, Colorado and Oklahoma. Maine and South Carolina reported record turnout.
**Why it matters:** The smooth administration deflates claims of widespread problems, though Trump has since renewed fraud allegations.
**Sources:** [OPB (AP)](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/03/election-day-unfolds-with-relatively-few-problems-some-voters-disrupted-by-weather-machine-issues/), [Votebeat](https://www.votebeat.org/national/2026/11/03/election-day-2026-live-blog-midterms-trump/)
