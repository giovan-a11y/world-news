## 1. Democrats capture both chambers of Congress [Politics]

**What happened:** Counts early Wednesday showed Democrats winning the House (about 228-139 in the early count, with races still open) and reaching 54 Senate seats per NBC's tracker, with Mississippi and Alaska too close to call. **Why it matters:** It is the first time Trump faces Democratic control of both chambers, ending the steady Senate confirmation of his judicial nominees.
