# US Top 10 - Monday, 23 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street's worst week since 2020 dominates, with Fed hike bets and Iran tension in the background.

## 1. Stocks crash: Nasdaq down 5.3% on Friday, S&P 500 in correction [Markets]

**What happened:** The Nasdaq fell about 5.3% and the S&P 500 about 3.7% on 20 November. The S&P 500 is now more than 10% below its October record. For the week the S&P fell about 8.6% and the Nasdaq about 10%.
**Why it matters:** The Nasdaq is now negative for 2026, which hits retirement accounts and sentiment.
**Sources:** [Cappnotes](https://cappnotes.substack.com/p/closing-look-friday-november-20-2026), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-november-19-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-anthropic-nvidia-141644188.html)

## 2. Nvidia falls 14% on reported SEC accounting inquiry [Business and tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia fell about 14%, its biggest one-day drop on record in one dataset, after Reuters reported an SEC accounting inquiry. Chip stocks fell about 10% in aggregate. Reporting on the cause is secondhand.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the AI trade's bellwether, and the inquiry raises accounting-risk concerns.
**Sources:** [Quantisnow](https://www.quantisnow.com/articles/nvidia-worst-day-on-file-chips-drag-nasdaq-100-down-5-5-percent), [StockInvest](https://stockinvest.us/digest/digest-us-market-close-2026-11-20)

## 3. Fed hike expectations for December [Economy and Fed]

**What happened:** The Fed held rates at its last meeting with two officials voting for a hike, and Chair Warsh declined to commit to December. Futures priced a hike as likely, though sources differ on the odds.
**Why it matters:** A hike during a market rout would tighten conditions further.
**Sources:** [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/much-ado-about-nothing-fed-chair-warsh-talks-up-a-storm-but-december-rate-hike-bets-barely-budge/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/the-sp-500-just-had-its-biggest-reversal-since-march-2020/)

## 4. Iran dirty-bomb warning [Security]

**What happened:** US intelligence reportedly flagged a possible Iranian dirty-bomb plot targeting Washington. Trump warned of severe consequences, and Iran denies it.
**Why it matters:** It raises the risk of renewed US military action in the Middle East.
**Sources:** [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/17/iran-will-pay-a-price-if-dirty-bomb-is-detonated-in-us/)

## 5. Supreme Court lets border-wall construction continue in Texas [Courts]

**What happened:** The Court granted the administration's emergency request to continue construction in southwestern Texas, reportedly 8-1.
**Why it matters:** It continues a run of emergency-docket wins for the administration on immigration policy.
**Sources:** [SCOTUSblog](https://scotusblog.com/2026/11/what-trump-cases-might-soon-return-to-the-supreme-court/)
