# US Top 10 - Friday, 6 November 2026

**Today in one line:** An AI-led selloff cut the S&P 500 by 4.4% in its worst day in more than a year, as Democrats' midterm win reshaped Washington and Vance blamed the Iran war.

## 1. AI selloff sends S&P 500 down 4.4% and Nasdaq into correction [Markets]

**What happened:** On Thursday the S&P 500 fell 4.4% to 7,353.73, its worst day since April 2025, the Dow lost 1,833 points to 51,523.06 and the Nasdaq dropped about 5.5%. AMD fell 17.3%, Nvidia 9.3% and CoreWeave 9.8%, and CNN estimated about $2.5 trillion of market value was wiped out. It followed Wednesday's rally of more than 1,500 points on the Dow.
**Why it matters:** Investors linked the rout to fears that Democrats could slow data-center spending and push moratoriums, adding to rate-hike worries.
**Sources:** [AP via Chattanooga Times Free Press](https://www.timesfreepress.com/news/2026/nov/05/a-brutal-sell-off-for-ai-stocks-sends-wall-street-reeling-and-the-sp-500-tumbles-4-4/), [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/stocks-nov-5-2026), [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/05/markets/asian-markets-us-rout-ai-investment-intl-hnk)

## 2. Democrats take the House; Speaker Johnson will not seek GOP leadership [Politics]

**What happened:** CBS News estimated Democrats could end with 251 to 268 House seats, and Hakeem Jeffries is widely expected to become speaker. Speaker Mike Johnson said he will not run to lead House Republicans in the next Congress. Democrats won at least 54 Senate seats on early counts, flipping seats in North Carolina, South Carolina, Ohio, Texas, Kansas, Iowa and Maine.
**Why it matters:** Divided government from January upends the agenda on tariffs, war powers and oversight.
**Sources:** [CBS News live updates](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/midterm-election-results-2026-democrats-house-senate-trump/), [NPR via KUOW](https://www.kuow.org/npr-news/2026-11-03/democrats-win-full-control-of-congress-ushering-in-a-new-era-of-divided-government), [Just Security Early Edition](https://www.justsecurity.org/163503/early-edition-november-5-2026/)

## 3. Mississippi and Alaska Senate races remain too close to call [Politics]

**What happened:** In Mississippi, Democrat Scott Colom led Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith by about 1,100 votes with counting continuing. Alaska's Senate race between Democrat Mary Peltola and Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan was also still being counted.
**Why it matters:** The two seats decide whether the Democratic Senate majority is 54, 55 or 56.
**Sources:** [CBS News live updates](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/midterm-election-results-2026-democrats-house-senate-trump/), [Alaska Beacon](https://alaskabeacon.com/2026/11/03/democratic-candidate-mary-peltola-leads-in-early-vote-counts-for-alaskas-us-senate-election/)

## 4. Vance says the Iran war cost Republicans the midterms [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** In a long post on X, Vice President JD Vance wrote "The reason we lost is the war," adding that many 2024 Trump voters backed Democrats this year. Trump said "Iran was a piece of it" but defended the war, and former press secretary Ari Fleischer rejected Vance's claim. Exit polls cited by Axios found cost of living and government corruption were the top issues.
**Why it matters:** The rare break from Trump is read as Vance positioning for 2028 and sharpens the fight over war powers.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/04/jd-vance-iran-war-trump-election), [Daily Montanan / States Newsroom](https://dailymontanan.com/2026/11/04/repub/vance-and-trump-blame-midterm-gop-elections-loss-on-their-war-in-iran/), [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/?p=1245341)

## 5. Democrats win a majority of governorships for the first time in 16 years [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats are set to hold 28 governor's offices to Republicans' 20, with Texas, Georgia, Iowa and Ohio among states flipped. In Texas, Gina Hinojosa defeated Gov. Greg Abbott, and in Georgia, Keisha Lance Bottoms won 54.3% to 45.7%. Alaska and Kansas had not been called.
**Why it matters:** Democratic governors will control redistricting and implementation fights in many large states.
**Sources:** [States Newsroom](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/democrats-win-majority-governorships-first-time-16-years), [AP via WRDW](https://www.wrdw.com/2026/11/04/democrats-press-win-majority-governors-offices-midterms-after-power-struggles-with-trump/), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/democrats-seize-majority-governors-offices-election-night-shift.amp)

## 7. Judge Chutkan temporarily blocks construction of Trump's Washington arch [Courts]

**Why it matters:** It is another test of presidential authority over federal landmarks in the capital.
