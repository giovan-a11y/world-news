# US Top 10 - Friday, 06 November 2026

**Today in one line:** An AI-stock rout drives the worst day for Wall Street since the April 2025 tariff shock, as Democrats map out their post-election agenda.

## 1. AI sell-off sends the S&P 500 down 4.4% [Economy / Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 fell 334.89 points, or 4.4%, to 7,353.73, its worst day since the April 2025 tariffs. The Nasdaq dropped about 5.5% and the Dow about 3.4%. Nvidia fell around 8-9%, CoreWeave 9.8% and GE Vernova 7.5%. Reports cite worries that data center construction may slow because of high borrowing costs, AI bubble concerns, and the possibility that Democratic-run governments pause data center building. The 10-year yield fell to 4.84% from 4.90%.
**Why it matters:** AI stocks have driven most market gains, and the Nasdaq is about 12% below its prior-month record.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/a-brutal-sell-off-for-ai-stocks-sends-wall-street-reeling-and-the-sp-500-tumbles-44/), [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/business/article/a-wipeout-for-ai-stocks-sends-wall-street-reeling-and-the-sp-500-tumbles-more-than-3/)

## 2. Democrats set a 100-day agenda after the midterm sweep [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats won both chambers of Congress on 3 November and plan a 100-day agenda on gas, grocery and healthcare costs, plus investigations of the administration once seated in January. Schumer said voters may have been more willing to reject Trump's party than to embrace Democrats. Trump struck a conciliatory tone but threatened vetoes.
**Why it matters:** Divided government changes the outlook for nominations, oversight, tariffs and war powers.
**Sources:** [WBTV / AP](https://www.wbtv.com/2026/11/04/voters-deliver-rebuke-trump-by-flipping-several-gop-held-senate-seats/), [GV Wire](https://gvwire.com/2026/11/05/democrats-vow-to-rein-in-trump-as-he-shows-off-white-house-ballroom/)

## 3. House Republican chairs summon Nvidia CEO [Business / Politics]

**What happened:** Three House Republican chairs asked Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang for a closed-door briefing on 18 November about his ties to an executive US spy agencies reportedly believe passed intelligence to Beijing. Nvidia's board opened its own investigation.
**Why it matters:** It compounds Nvidia's losses and export-control risk.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/05/jensen-huang-nvidia-congress-china)

## 4. Trump takes questions on Iran and Russia [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** Pool reports from an Oval Office session on 5 November say Trump discussed a Russian tanker and the Iran conflict. Trump also said he is not considering replacing Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth despite voter dissatisfaction with the Iran war.
**Why it matters:** The war was a major factor in the election result.
**Sources:** [UCSB pool reports](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/pool-reports-november-5-2026), [AP via KOLD](https://www.kold.com/2026/11/04/democrats-usher-new-era-with-their-resounding-sweep-house-senate/)
