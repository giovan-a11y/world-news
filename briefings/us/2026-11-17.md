# US Top 10 - Tuesday, 17 November 2026

**Today in one line:** AI stocks slide for a third straight session ahead of Nvidia earnings, while hot inflation and Iran war politics shape Washington after the midterms.

## 1. Wall Street falls again on AI sell-off [Economy / Markets]

**What happened:** Stocks dropped Monday as Nvidia and other AI names sank. An AP-based report has the S&P 500 down 145 points to 7,416.59, its third straight loss, the Dow down 1.4% and the Nasdaq down about 2.5% (single source: unconfirmed). The 10-year yield eased to about 5.13%.
**Why it matters:** It extends the AI-valuation rout and tests retirement accounts and tech-led growth just before Nvidia reports.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/more-drops-for-nvidia-and-other-ai-chip-stocks-erase-wall-streets-rally-from-the-day-before/), [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-dow-jones-industrial-average-down-141-5030993)

## 2. October inflation hotter than expected; Fed hike looms [Economy]

**What happened:** Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above forecasts, and core prices rose 0.3% for a third month. The BLS published no annual rate because prices were not collected in October 2025 during the shutdown. Markets price a quarter-point December hike at over 80%.
**Why it matters:** Higher rates would raise mortgage and borrowing costs just as affordability dominates politics.
**Sources:** [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2026/october-2026-cpi.html), [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/story/consumer-prices-increased-04-october-higher-expected/20029965/)

## 3. AI sell-off tied to data-centre fears after Democratic midterm wins [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** On 5 November the S&P 500 fell 4.4% in its worst day since the April 2025 tariff shock, with Nvidia down 9.3%, as investors worried about costlier borrowing and possible data-centre moratoriums after Democratic gains.
**Why it matters:** Political risk is now priced into the AI buildout that has driven much of the market's gains.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/a-brutal-sell-off-for-ai-stocks-sends-wall-street-reeling-and-the-sp-500-tumbles-44/), [ADN](https://www.adn.com/business-economy/2026/11/05/ai-stock-wipeout-sends-wall-street-tumbling-as-fears-rise-over-data-center-moratoriums-following-democratic-landslide/)

## 4. Iran war politics: strikes before the vote, restraint after [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** US forces struck Iranian targets near the Strait of Hormuz hours before the midterms, and Vice President Vance reportedly cited the war as a reason Republicans lost. Reuters reports Trump may hold off on new strikes absent provocation. Gas prices are near $4 a gallon.
**Why it matters:** The war drives fuel prices, troop risk and a year-end funding fight.
**Sources:** [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/u-s-strikes-strait-of-hormuz-hours-before-tuesday-midterms), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-war-us-signals-restraint-as-iran-warns-of-retaliation-1.500746947)

## 5. Democrats push war limits in the year-end spending deal [Politics]

**What happened:** Senate Democrats say they will not accept a spending deal that fails to restrict the Iran war, with a funding deadline of 11 December. Trump has urged moving a $350 billion military reconciliation bill (carried from earlier reports).
**Why it matters:** It sets up a possible shutdown fight weeks after a record 43-day shutdown.
**Sources:** [Yeni Safak](https://en.yenisafak.com/world/us-senate-democrats-vow-to-block-funding-for-trumps-iran-war-3725516), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/us-iran-war-trump-nuclear-material-no-deal/)

## 6. Earnings week: Nvidia, Home Depot, Disney, Target, Walmart [Business]

**What happened:** Nvidia is due this week (most sources say 18 November after the close; one says 17 November). Disney and others report around the same time, with Target and Walmart later in the week.
**Why it matters:** Chip results and retailer commentary will set the tone for equities and holiday-spending expectations.
**Sources:** [Earnings preview](https://news.ifyouspent.com/en/news/nvidia-disney-siemens-allianz-earnings-preview-november-2026), [Regards of Wall Street](https://www.regardsofwallstreet.com/newsletter/2026-11-08-week-ahead)
