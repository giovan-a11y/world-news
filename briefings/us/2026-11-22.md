# US Top 10 - Sunday, 22 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street's AI-led selloff dominated a week in which Democrats prepare to take Congress and the Justice Department keeps losing senior officials.

*Note: search coverage was thin; this list holds the 8 items that could be sourced. Items marked (unconfirmed) rest on undated wire summaries.*

## 1. Stocks fall into correction as AI trade unravels [Economy / Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 fell about 3.7% to 6,910.39 and the Dow lost 949.73 points to 48,147.33 on Friday, both in correction territory; the Nasdaq slumped about 5% and is now negative for 2026.
**Why it matters:** Retirement accounts and corporate investment plans are exposed to the tech-heavy market; the VIX closed near 36.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [ABC News/AP](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/asia-shares-drop-ai-worries-echoing-wall-streets-138309523)

## 2. Anthropic debuts with the largest IPO in US history [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Anthropic raised about $81bn at $83 a share and closed day one at $97.17, up 17%, even as Nvidia, AMD and Micron fell.
**Why it matters:** It is a landmark for AI financing, but did not revive enthusiasm for tech stocks.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/19/anthropic-starts-trading-in-the-us-after-blockbuster-ipo), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/349077/Wall-Street-ends-sharply-lower-as-Anthropics-debut-fails-to-revive-AI-fervor)

## 3. 10-year Treasury yield falls back below 5% [Economy]

**What happened:** Safe-haven buying pulled the 10-year yield to 4.92% on 20 November from 5.15% a week earlier.
**Why it matters:** Yields near 5% raise mortgage and corporate borrowing costs; the drop reflects flight from equities.
**Sources:** [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/global-markets/wall-street-falls-as-nasdaq-suffers-sharp-losses-dow-and-sp-500-enter-correction-territory/), [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/21282483/stock-market-today-wall-streets-worst-week-in-a-month-closes-out-with-more-losses)

## 4. Nvidia in leadership turmoil [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO and director on 12 November; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Reports tie it to a personal scandal involving alleged Chinese intelligence recruitment, though Nvidia gave no reason. Shares fell nearly 12% that day.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the bellwether of the AI trade and the largest chipmaker in the US.
**Sources:** [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm), [SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns/)

## 5. Justice Department exodus continues after midterms [Politics / Courts]

**What happened:** Civil rights chief Harmeet Dhillon resigned on 5 November, and Reuters reported two more top DOJ officials quitting as senior departures accelerate after Democrats won both chambers. (Date of the latest departures unconfirmed.)
**Why it matters:** The department faces incoming congressional oversight with thinning leadership.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/5/top-us-civil-rights-official-resigns), [Devdiscourse/Reuters](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4002972-two-top-justice-department-officials-resign-as-post-election-exodus-accelerates)

## 7. Measles outbreak worst in decades (unconfirmed)

**Why it matters:** Elimination status and school vaccination rates are at stake.
