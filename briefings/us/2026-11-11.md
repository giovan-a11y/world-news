# US Top 10 - Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Hotter-than-expected inflation hit stocks and revived Fed rate-hike bets, House Republicans turned aside Hegseth's impeachment, and Rubio said major combat against Iran is not needed for now.

## 1. Hot October inflation sends stocks lower and revives Fed-hike bets [Economy / Markets]

**What happened:** Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October against 0.3% expected, with core prices up 0.3% against 0.2% expected; the BLS did not publish most annual comparisons because of last year's shutdown. The S&P 500 fell 0.38% to 7,520.93, the Dow 0.30% to 52,231.89 and the Nasdaq 0.74% to 25,065.87. Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan said two more rate hikes would be needed.
**Why it matters:** Sticky inflation, even as gasoline prices ease, is pushing markets toward a December tightening that would raise borrowing costs.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/us-stocks-slip-following-a-discouraging-update-on-inflation/), [Meta Trading Club recap](https://www.metatradingclub.com/?p=28238), [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/us-cpi-rises-october-2026/)

## 2. Nvidia slides again as board probes CEO Jensen Huang [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia shares fell about 5.5% to $168.80, a second straight daily decline, after the board hired an outside firm to investigate allegations involving CEO Jensen Huang's ties to an alleged Chinese intelligence operative. Huang has admitted an affair. Republican Sen. Kevin Cramer called on him to resign, while Trump has defended him.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia was the heaviest weight on the S&P 500, so its slide dragged the whole market.
**Sources:** [Meta Trading Club](https://www.metatradingclub.com/?p=28238), [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-affair-board-to-launch-investigation), [Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/11/09/nvidia-investigates-whether-ceo-jensen-huang-was-targeted-by-chinese-spy-with-whom-he-had-an-affair/)

## 3. House turns aside Massie's Hegseth impeachment [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** The House voted 211-204 to refer Rep. Thomas Massie's eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively killing them in this Congress. Massie was the only Republican to oppose the motion. The articles cite the Iran war, boat strikes and other actions.
**Why it matters:** The vote put Republicans on record on the war powers fight just after the midterms.
**Sources:** [AP News in Brief](https://www.ottumwacourier.com/news/national_news/ap-news-in-brief-at-9-04-p-m-est/article_382c9ebc-640e-5d43-8f7f-5f11f65280e1.html), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/house-gop-leaders-block-massie-hegseth-impeachment-push.amp), [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/10/headlines)

## 4. Rubio: economic pressure means no need for major combat against Iran [Security / Diplomacy]

**What happened:** After a G7 foreign ministers' meeting in Paris, Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the sanctions and naval blockade are "so devastating" that there is no need for major combat operations right now, while warning the US will respond if Iran escalates. Trump said a renewed campaign is "quite unlikely". Iran told the IAEA it cannot accept full inspections while under threat.
**Why it matters:** It signals a shift from strikes toward a long economic squeeze, with Europe pressing against escalation.
**Sources:** [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/rubio-says-no-need-major-combat-operations-against-iran-given-us-economic), [Baird Maritime / Reuters](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/security/naval/rubio-says-naval-blockade-economic-warfare-avoid-need-for-major-us-strikes-on-iran), [Carnegie Proliferation News](https://carnegieendowment.org/programs/nuclear-policy/proliferation-news/proliferation-news-november-10-2026)

## 5. Supreme Court lets border wall construction continue in Big Bend [Courts / Immigration]

**What happened:** The Supreme Court allowed the Trump administration to resume border wall construction in Big Bend National Park and along the Rio Grande in Texas, after a district judge had halted the work and declined to pause her ruling.
**Why it matters:** The emergency order lets the administration build while the litigation continues.
**Sources:** [SCOTUSblog](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/11/supreme-court-allows-trump-administration-to-continue-border-wall-construction/), [Carnegie Proliferation News](https://carnegieendowment.org/programs/nuclear-policy/proliferation-news/proliferation-news-november-10-2026)

## 6. Senate Republicans re-elect Thune as leader after midterm losses [Politics]

**What happened:** Senate Republicans re-elected John Thune as leader by acclamation for another two years after losing their majority on 3 November, with Barrasso kept as whip. A last-minute push by some conservatives to delay the vote failed.
**Why it matters:** Thune will lead a minority caucus facing a Democratic Senate and a looming 11 December funding deadline.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/11/11/thune-re-elected-as-us-senate-republican-leader-after-midterm-losses), [CBS News](https://cbsnews.com/news/john-thune-reelected-senate-republican-leader), [Philadelphia Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/politics/senate-republicans-reelect-john-thune-minority-leader-20261110.html)

## 7. Senate advances sweeping permitting reform bill [Congress / Economy]

**What happened:** The Senate voted 73-25 on Monday to proceed to H.R. 2347, a vehicle for permitting reform, and agreed to the motion to proceed 62-21 on Tuesday evening. The chamber also confirmed many military nominations by voice vote.
**Why it matters:** Permitting reform is one of the few bipartisan priorities still moving before Democrats take over committees in January.
**Sources:** [Senate floor activity, 10 Nov](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/floor_activity/11_10_2026_Senate_Floor.htm), [Senate roll call vote 258](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1192/vote_119_2_00258.htm)

**What happened:** US authorities charged three men over a plot involving nearly 20 attacks on Jewish communities in Europe and the United States, plus an attack on a British military base. Separately, London counterterror police arrested a US Army soldier on suspicion of Russia-related espionage.
**Why it matters:** The case underscores heightened antisemitic and espionage threats linked to the wars in the Middle East and Ukraine.
