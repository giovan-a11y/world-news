# US Top 10 - Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A hotter-than-expected October inflation reading pushes yields up and stocks down, raising odds of another Fed rate hike.

## 1. October inflation runs hot [Economy]

**What happened:** Finimize reports consumer prices rose 0.4% in October versus 0.3% expected, with core CPI up 0.3% versus 0.2% expected. Global News notes the core reading sat exactly at the 0.3% level that Fed Governor Christopher Waller had flagged as a threshold for a stronger policy response.
**Why it matters:** Inflation, fuel costs tied to the Iran war and tariffs are central to the political and Fed outlook after the midterms.
**Sources:** [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/hotter-cpi-and-nvidia-scrutiny-rattle-us-stocks), [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12144640/us-inflation-october-2026-stock-markets/)

## 2. Stocks slip and yields jump after the CPI print [Economy / Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 lost 0.4% to 7,520.93 and the Nasdaq fell 0.74% to 25,065.88. The Dow lost 0.3% to 52,231.89. The 10-year Treasury yield rose to about 4.98% (single aggregator: unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Higher yields pressure richly valued AI stocks that had just started to recover.
**Sources:** [JournalArta](https://journalarta.com/en/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-sp-500-slips-0-38-to-7520-93-and-nasdaq-falls-0-74-as-10-year-yield-rises-to-4-98/), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3027683/stock-market-news-for-nov-11-2026)

## 3. Fed rate-hike bets build [Economy]

**What happened:** Investing-site reports citing CME FedWatch say a rate hike is now more likely, and some traders price a December hike. Chair Kevin Warsh led the Fed to a 25-basis-point hike earlier this year, calling inflation "the problem."
**Why it matters:** A second tightening step would raise borrowing costs for mortgages and businesses (timing of the earlier hike not confirmed in my sources).
**Sources:** [InvestingLive](https://investinglive.com/technical-analysis/us-stocks-slide-yields-jump-usd-higher-after-hotter-than-expected-october-cpi/), [WJLA](https://wjla.com/news/nation-world/fed-move-to-raise-rates-was-a-serious-and-responsible-decision-warsh-committee-members-economy-iran)

## 4. Nvidia slides on China chip reports [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia shares fell about 6.6% after a report raised questions about restricted chips reaching China, and it had already dropped more than 5% earlier in the week after news of an investigation tied to its CEO. Reuters separately reports about 600,000 H200 chips are stuck in Hong Kong as Beijing withholds import approval.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia reports earnings on 17 November and is the biggest AI bellwether.
**Sources:** [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/hotter-cpi-and-nvidia-scrutiny-rattle-us-stocks), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/10/why-nvidia-stock-just-fell-again/), [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/nvidia-h200-chips-pile-up-hong-kong/)

## 5. Trade Desk reports [Business]

**What happened:** The Trade Desk reported adjusted third-quarter earnings of $0.33 per share versus a $0.26 consensus; Barrick Mining also beat estimates (Zacks).
**Why it matters:** Earnings season continues to support the "stocks vs. rates" tug of war.
**Sources:** [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3027683/stock-market-news-for-nov-11-2026), [Zacks Nov 10](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3026944/stock-market-news-for-nov-10-2026)
