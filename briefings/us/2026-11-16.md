# US Top 10 - Monday, 16 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A post-midterm Washington is split over the Iran war and a year-end spending fight, while markets brace for Nvidia earnings and Fed minutes.

## 1. Trump signals he may hold off on new Iran strikes [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** Reuters reports Trump told senior advisers he may hold off on attacking Iran if there are no new provocations from Tehran or the Houthis. He also posted that "We don't need a deal with Iran." Bahrain said it intercepted Iranian attacks that Tehran said targeted US assets.
**Why it matters:** The posture shift after the 3 November midterms shapes oil prices, US troop risk and the odds of a preliminary deal.
**Sources:** [US News / Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-11-14/trump-tells-advisers-he-may-hold-off-on-iran-attacks-barring-new-provocations-sources-say), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/14/shifting-iran-rhetoric-trump-leaves-mideast-limbo-path-ahead/)

## 2. Democrats vow to block war funding in the year-end spending deal [Politics]

**What happened:** Senators Bennet, Sanders, Van Hollen and Duckworth say they will not accept a year-end spending deal that fails to restrict the Iran war. The deadline is 11 December. Trump has urged Senate leaders to move a $350 billion military reconciliation bill.
**Why it matters:** It sets up a possible shutdown fight weeks after the record 43-day shutdown ended.
**Sources:** [Yeni Safak](https://en.yenisafak.com/world/us-senate-democrats-vow-to-block-funding-for-trumps-iran-war-3725516), [CBS News live updates](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/us-iran-war-trump-nuclear-material-no-deal/)

## 3. Post-midterm landscape: Democrats take Senate control [Politics]

**What happened:** Republicans lost badly on 3 November. A summary of the results says Democrats gained control of the Senate, and Republicans also lost state attorney general races in key states. Inflation, tariffs, affordability and the Hormuz crisis weighed on voters.
**Why it matters:** Divided power changes the outlook for nominations, oversight, tariffs and war powers from January.
**Sources:** [Wikipedia: 2026 US elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_elections), [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/the-latest-white-house-claims-criminality-in-democratic-oversight/)
