# US Top 10 - Saturday, 28 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A shortened Black Friday session saw tech rebound, while a new tariff plan, Gulf attacks on oil and a post-midterm political reset frame the week ahead.
## 1. Trump unveils proposed tariffs on 14 trading partners [Trade]

**What happened:** The administration proposed an extra 20% on China, 15% on the EU and Japan, and 10% to 20% on other partners, excluding semiconductors, autos, steel and aluminium. The plan faces a public comment period before it is final.
**Why it matters:** It is designed to replace tariff revenue lost after the Supreme Court ruling against emergency-law tariffs, and may raise prices for US importers and consumers.
**Sources:** [ITV News](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-11-25/us-announces-new-tariffs-hikes-on-the-eu-china-and-others), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/25/trump-proposes-new-tariffs-china-europe-other-trade-partners/), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/trump-administration-to-raise-tariffs-on-china-eu-brazil-and-others)

**Sources:** [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/seekingalpha:e521c5511094b:0-u-s-equities-close-on-top-on-black-friday/), [Eurasia Business News](https://eurasiabusinessnews.com/2026/11/27/u-s-stock-market-today-november-27-2026-tech-rebounds-in-shortened-black-friday-session)

**Why it matters:** Higher crude feeds gasoline prices and inflation worries.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/brent-oil-wti-hormuz-middle-east.html), [RTE](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1127/1600877-world-oil-prices/)

## 4. Trump's Thanksgiving post attacks foes and claims data falsified [Politics]

**What happened:** From his Florida golf club, Trump used a holiday post to attack political opponents, immigrants and the federal government, and accused his own government of falsifying economic data without evidence.
**Why it matters:** Attacks on official statistics could undermine trust in data the Fed and markets depend on.
**Sources:** [Detroit News](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2026/11/26/trump-thanksgiving-message-lash-out-at-political-foes-immigrants-federal-government/92973751007/), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/26/trump-uses-thanksgiving-post-provoke-critics-air-grievances/)

## 5. Democrats prepare to take Congress in January [Politics]

**What happened:** Republicans suffered heavy losses on 3 November and will cede both chambers in January. Democrats plan oversight of the Iran war and immigration enforcement.
**Why it matters:** Divided government will reshape tariffs, war powers and judicial confirmations.
**Sources:** [OPB/NPR](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/04/2026-midterms-democrats-win-the-senate/), [SCOTUSblog](https://scotusblog.com/2026/11/democrats-won-control-of-congress-what-might-that-mean-for-the-supreme-court/)

## 6. Iran war: US blockade continues, Houthi attacks hit Saudi sites [Security]

**What happened:** The US military said it disabled a commercial ship that tried to run its blockade of Iran. The Houthis claimed strikes on Saudi refineries, and Saudi Arabia said one worker was killed at Jazan.
**Why it matters:** The conflict's cost and fuel prices remain central to US politics.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/brent-oil-wti-hormuz-middle-east.html), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/11/27/oil-set-to-post-weekly-gain-as-middle-east-attacks-stoke-supply-concerns/)
