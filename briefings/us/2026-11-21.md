# US Top 10 - Saturday, 21 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street's AI-driven rout dominated the week while Congress fought over Iran war funding ahead of the 11 December shutdown deadline.

*Editor's note: US coverage found today was limited, so only 6 verified stories are listed.*

## 1. Stocks tumble as S&P 500 and Dow enter correction, Nasdaq erases 2026 gains [Economy and markets]

**What happened:** On Thursday the Dow dropped about 1,100 points, the S&P 500 fell about 2% to roughly 7,176 for a sixth straight losing day, and the Nasdaq lost about 2.3%. One source reports a much worse Friday (S&P 500 down 3.7% to about 6,910, Nasdaq down 5.3%); that is unconfirmed.
**Why it matters:** A correction in both major indexes hits household wealth and sentiment, and it follows heavy AI-driven gains.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nov-19-221002368.html)

## 2. Senate blocks Iran war powers resolution as funding fight builds [Politics]

**What happened:** A war powers resolution on Iran was blocked in the first such vote since the midterms. Democrats, who take both chambers on 3 January, are shifting to cutting off war funding and may attach an amendment to the next spending bill. Federal funding runs out on 11 December.
**Why it matters:** The standoff raises the risk of a pre-Christmas shutdown and shapes the war's future.
**Sources:** [Roll Call](http://www.rollcall.com/2026/11/19/iran-resolution-blocked-in-first-war-powers-vote-since-midterms), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/19/government-shutdown-funding-iran-democrat)

## 3. House Democrats wary of forcing a December shutdown [Politics]

**What happened:** Axios reports House Democrats are largely avoiding shutdown threats, though Reps. Ansari and Khanna say they will oppose any package that does not bar offensive Iran funds. Sen. John Kennedy has said a funding cutoff would mean a shutdown.
**Why it matters:** Lame-duck bargaining will decide whether government stays open through the holidays.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/19/government-shutdown-funding-iran-democrat), [NPR](https://npr.org/2026/11/20/g-s1-154582/up-first-newsletter-democrats-iran-war-trump-xi-jinping-iowa-army)

## 4. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; shares slide despite record quarter [Business and tech]

**What happened:** Huang stepped down immediately and CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Nvidia reported record revenue of $115.8 billion but the stock still fell about 3% on Thursday after a roughly 13% drop on the resignation.
**Why it matters:** The largest AI chipmaker's leadership vacuum is a key driver of the market jitters.
**Sources:** [Nvidia 8-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nov-19-222025646.html)

## 5. Anthropic's Nasdaq debut [Business and tech]

**What happened:** Anthropic priced at $83 a share, above its range, and began trading as ANT on Thursday, but the debut did not stop the sell-off.
**Why it matters:** It is a landmark AI listing that arrives in a fragile market.
**Sources:** [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)

## 6. Trump returns from Asia with trade, Taiwan and tech disputes unresolved [Politics]

**What happened:** After APEC in Shenzhen and meetings with Xi and Putin, CNBC reports tariffs, rare earths, Taiwan and technology competition remain unresolved.
**Why it matters:** Companies and markets get no clarity on US-China trade terms.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/apec-summit-trump-xi-china-trade-taiwan.html)
