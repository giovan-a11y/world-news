# US Top 10 - Wednesday, 4 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Americans voted in midterms that doubled as a referendum on Trump's second term, with federal monitors at polls, flooding in the Southwest and US strikes on Iranian targets in the background.

## 1. Midterm voting ends as a referendum on Trump's second term [Politics]

**What happened:** Polls began closing Tuesday evening with voters, frustrated by prices and the direction of the country, deciding control of Congress and dozens of governorships. Trump spent the final days posting dozens of messages urging Republicans to turn out. Democrats needed four Senate seats to win control, and later AP updates reported they had won the House and flipped Senate seats including in Texas and South Carolina.
**Why it matters:** The outcome decides oversight, tariffs, war powers and the funding fights that begin in January.
**Sources:** [AP News in Brief, 3 Nov](https://www.ottumwacourier.com/news/national_news/ap-news-in-brief-at-6-04-p-m-est/article_bb127fe1-c1d9-53d6-9988-bcffeb3f3645.html), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/midterm-elections-2026-live-updates.html), [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/trump-truth-social-republican-turnout-midterm-tele-rallies-1829335)

## 2. Election Day runs largely smoothly, with scattered glitches and record turnout [Politics / Election administration]

**What happened:** Votebeat reported typical problems and said fears of federal interference largely did not materialize. A software update left scanners in Westchester County, New York, rejecting paper ballots, Broward County, Florida, delayed voting by 20 minutes, and storms changed polling arrangements in parts of Florida, Colorado and Oklahoma. Dearborn, Michigan, ran short of ballots, and Maine and South Carolina reported record midterm turnout.
**Why it matters:** The smooth administration undercuts claims of widespread problems as the results come in.
**Sources:** [OPB (AP)](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/03/election-day-unfolds-with-relatively-few-problems-some-voters-disrupted-by-weather-machine-issues/), [Votebeat](https://www.votebeat.org/national/2026/11/03/election-day-2026-live-blog-midterms-trump/), [Fox News live](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/2026-midterm-elections-news-analysis-races-11-3-2026)

## 3. Federal judge declines to block Justice Department's 1,000 poll monitors [Courts / Justice]

**What happened:** US District Judge Reggie Walton denied an NAACP emergency request on Monday night to block the Justice Department from deploying about 1,000 federal monitors, finding the group had not shown it was likely to win, as the government said monitors would be in plainclothes. The NAACP's legal arm said the order bars officers with arrest authority from serving as monitors, and accounts differ on how much was granted.
**Why it matters:** The deployment was a flashpoint over federal involvement in election administration.
**Sources:** [Democracy Docket](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/judge-wont-bar-doj-from-deploying-law-enforcement-to-voting-sites/), [NAACP LDF](https://www.naacpldf.org/press-release/naacp-ldf-and-advancement-projects-lawsuit-bars-justice-department-from-deploying-federal-law-enforcement-agents-to-polls-on-election-day/), [Democracy Now headlines, 3 Nov](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/3/headlines)

## 4. US carries out strikes on Iranian targets in the Strait of Hormuz [Security]

**What happened:** A US official said strikes hit Iranians who had used surface-to-air missiles against US forces in the strait, and Iranian state media reported explosions on Qeshm and Hengam islands and near Bandar Abbas. Oil rose on the news. Iran has offered to reopen the strait and resume nuclear talks if the US lifts its blockade, and Vance said he and Trump feel "pretty good" about a negotiated settlement.
**Why it matters:** The strikes, the first since early September, came hours before voters weighed in on a war that drove fuel prices higher.
**Sources:** [AP via OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/03/us-strikes-in-the-strait-of-hormuz-create-more-questions-about-whats-ahead/), [PBS NewsHour](https://pbs.org/newshour/world/u-s-strikes-strait-of-hormuz-hours-before-tuesday-midterms), [Democracy Now headlines](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/3/headlines)

## 5. Record Southwest flooding forces evacuations and alters Election Day voting [Weather / Disaster]

**What happened:** Powerful downpours sent water over normally dry terrain in Arizona, Utah, New Mexico and Colorado, forcing hundreds to evacuate and keeping swift-water teams busy. Arizona's Workman Creek recorded 7.3 inches of rain over two days. Colorado and Utah arranged replacement ballots and alternative voting for stranded residents, including in Pagosa Springs.
**Why it matters:** Flood risk stayed high even after the rain cleared, complicating cleanup and voting.
**Sources:** [AP via OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/02/rain-clears-but-flood-risk-remains-high-after-destructive-storms-swamp-normally-arid-southwest/), [NY1 (AP)](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2026/11/02/floodwaters-swamp-the-southwest-forcing-evacuations-and-altering-some-election-day-voting), [Good Morning America](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/floodwaters-swamp-southwest-forcing-evacuations-altering-election-day-137772563)

## 6. Stocks edge up on Election Day after oil's Monday plunge [Markets]

**What happened:** On Tuesday the S&P 500 rose 0.4% to 7,568.92 and the Nasdaq 0.8% to 25,372.01, while the Dow fell 0.4% to 51,786.72 with 21 components lower. Apple fell about 2% after disappointing quarterly results. On Monday Brent crude had dropped about 7.6% on hopes of Iran peace, with tech pulling the S&P 500 down 0.5% to about 7,541.
**Why it matters:** Investors positioned for the election with AI and chip stocks already showing volatility.

## 7. NTSB: Learjet that killed nine in Florida was breaking apart before impact [Disaster / Transport]

**What happened:** NTSB member Tom Chapman said most of the left elevator was found in three pieces between 1.3 and 3.8 miles from the crash site, indicating the jet was coming apart before it hit a residential area of Greenacres near West Palm Beach on Sunday. All nine aboard died, and about 600 residents were displaced. The NTSB has not named a cause.
**Why it matters:** Investigators are examining why the tail section separated, with icing reported but unconfirmed.
**Sources:** [AP News in Brief, 3 Nov](https://www.ottumwacourier.com/news/national_news/ap-news-in-brief-at-6-04-p-m-est/article_bb127fe1-c1d9-53d6-9988-bcffeb3f3645.html), [ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/ntsb-to-brief-on-deadly-greenacres-jet-crash-as-600-residents-remain-displacedgreenacres-plane-crash-greenacres-jet-crash-learjet-60-crash-military-trail-crash-10th-avenue-north-crash-palm-beach-county-plane-crash-nine-killed-in-plane-crash-600-re), [Aerocorner](https://aerocorner.com/news/n722bd-learjet-60-greenacres-florida-crash/)

## 8. Oklahoma man charged with mailing hundreds of white-powder hoax letters to officials [Crime / Security]

**What happened:** Robin Martin of Oklahoma City was charged with hoax and threat offenses over letters that the FBI says contained yeast or flour, with more than 700 mailings tied to the same sender this fall. The mailings forced evacuations of election offices in Louisiana and Oklahoma, and the FBI says his fingerprints were on a letter addressed to FBI Director Kash Patel.
**Why it matters:** The campaign targeted election officials just before the vote, adding to threats against public servants.
**Sources:** [ADN (AP)](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/11/03/oklahoma-man-charged-with-mailing-hundreds-of-letters-containing-powder-to-officials-ahead-of-the-election/), [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/man-charged-with-mailing-hundreds-of-letters-containing-powder-to-officials-ahead-of-the-election/), [News 9](https://www.news9.com/crime/oklahoma-man-charged-mailing-hundreds-powder-hoax-letters)

## 9. Judge dismisses Justice Department suit aimed at blocking Alaska Senate certification [Courts / Politics]

**What happened:** US District Judge Sharon Gleason dismissed the DOJ's suit seeking to block certification of Alaska's Senate race if Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan lost to Democrat Mary Peltola, saying the department lacked standing and offered no workable way to separate votes for a second candidate named Daniel Sullivan without compromising ballot secrecy. DOJ filed notice it will appeal.
**Why it matters:** The ruling rejected a Trump-ordered attempt to shape a close race's outcome.
**Sources:** [AP via WOWT](https://www.wowt.com/2026/11/02/judge-dismisses-lawsuit-seeking-block-certification-alaska-senate-race-if-gop-incumbent-loses/?outputType=amp), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/01/trump-doj-delay-alaska-midterm-results-rejected-judge), [ADN](https://www.adn.com/politics/2026/11/01/judge-throws-out-trump-ordered-lawsuit-seeking-to-block-certification-of-alaskas-us-senate-race/)

## 10. Pelicans end Grizzlies' perfect start; NBA pauses for Election Day [Sports]

**What happened:** New Orleans beat Memphis 135-134 in overtime on Monday, handing the NBA's last undefeated team its first loss. Zion Williamson scored 25, Jeremiah Fears hit the clutch free throws, and Ty Jerome scored 40 for Memphis but missed a last-second jumper. The league then paused play on Tuesday to encourage civic participation.
**Why it matters:** The upset resets the early-season race, and the pause was a rare Election Day break.
