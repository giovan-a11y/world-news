# US Top 10 - Sunday, 8 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Trump ordered a Justice Department probe of Democrats days after their midterm sweep, Stephen Miller's rise as chief of staff drew vows of oversight, and Oregon stunned Ohio State.

## 1. Trump orders DOJ to investigate Democrats over alleged threats to officials and donors [Politics / Justice]

**What happened:** In a Truth Social post on Saturday, Trump said he was ordering the Justice Department to look into what he called a "criminal act", accusing Democrats of threatening Attorney General Todd Blanche and other officials with impeachment and of threatening donors. He gave no evidence, named only Sen. Elissa Slotkin and denied that any impeachable acts had occurred. The Justice Department had not said whether it will open an investigation.
**Why it matters:** The order previews a clash over oversight once Democrats take Congress in January.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/trump-orders-doj-to-investigate-democrats-for-threatening-people), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-orders-doj-probe-democrats-over-alleged-threats-against-admin-officials.amp), [Political.org](https://political.org/2026/11/07/trump-orders-justice-department-to-look-into-democrats-over-alleged-threats-against-attorney-general-todd-blanche-donors/)

## 2. Democrats vow oversight and legal fights as Miller replaces Wiles [Politics]

**What happened:** Trump named Stephen Miller, the architect of his deportation campaign, White House chief of staff to start on 9 November, moving Susie Wiles to his political operation. Gov. Gavin Newsom accused Miller of driving a "sadistic mass deportation regime", House Oversight's Robert Garcia said he should "lawyer up", and Sen. Ruben Gallego said he "better get ready to testify". Vance and Rubio praised the pick.
**Why it matters:** The personnel shake-up three days after the midterm rout sets up an investigation-heavy next Congress.
**Sources:** [Political.org](https://political.org/2026/11/06/democrats-vow-oversight-and-legal-fights-against-stephen-miller-after-trump-names-him-white-house-chief-of-staff/), [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/06/nation/susie-wiles-replaced-stephen-miller-white-house-chief-of-staff/), [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-names-stephen-miller-as-33rd-white-house-chief-of-staff/?query-11-page=2)

## 3. Midterms shake Wall Street: AI stocks fall, alcohol and homebuilders gain [Markets]

**What happened:** AP reports data-center and AI stocks fell as data-center skeptics won seats, while beer, wine and liquor makers rose on hopes a Democratic Congress eases tariffs. D.R. Horton rose 4.9%, PulteGroup 4.4% and Home Depot 2.9%, while energy shares slipped on hopes the war ends. The S&P 500 finished the week down 2.7% at 7,376.86 and the Nasdaq down 3.37% at 24,579.42.
**Why it matters:** It was the S&P 500's worst week since April 2025 and left it about 5.7% below its 6 October record.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/from-high-flying-ai-stocks-to-tariff-hit-alcohol-companies-the-midterms-shook-up-wall-street/), [LPL Research](https://www.lpl.com/research/blog/weekly-market-performance-november-6-2026.html), [Star-Advertiser](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/11/07/breaking-news/dizzying-week-for-investors-as-politics-ai-and-the-economy-collide/)

## 4. Oregon routs No. 11 Ohio State; Utah beats BYU in Holy War [Sports]

**What happened:** Oregon came back from a two-touchdown deficit to beat No. 11 Ohio State 41-28 in Columbus, probably ending the Buckeyes' hopes of a third straight playoff berth. No. 3 Utah stayed unbeaten with a 28-20 win over No. 7 BYU, with Devon Dampier producing 264 yards of offense.
**Why it matters:** The results reshape the College Football Playoff race a few weeks before selection.
**Sources:** [OPB (AP)](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/07/oregon-storms-back-from-2-touchdown-deficit-to-hammer-no-11-ohio-state-41-28/), [AJC (AP)](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/utah-and-ucla-stay-unbeaten-texas-pulls-out-win-at-missouri-as-the-playoff-race-begins-in-earnest/), [Your Valley (AP)](https://www.yourvalley.net/stories/no-3-utah-stays-perfect-ends-holy-war-skid-against-no-7-byu,738861)

## 5. Record Georgia-Tennessee flood moves up the Atlantic coast [Weather / Disaster]

**What happened:** Storms that caused a catastrophic 1-in-1,000-year flood on the Tennessee-Georgia line moved up the East Coast on Friday. At least one person died, in Murray County, Georgia, Tennessee coordinated 68 swift-water rescues, the Toccoa River hit a record 18 feet, and Gov. Brian Kemp declared an emergency for four counties. It was the second major flood in three months for some of the towns.
**Why it matters:** Communities still recovering from an August flood face a second round of losses.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/storms-that-caused-1-in-1000-year-flood-event-in-tennessee-and-georgia-move-up-atlantic-coast/), [OPB (AP)](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/06/heavy-rains-flood-towns-dozens-rescued-on-georgia-tennessee-border/)

## 6. Man with three guns arrested near White House after saying voices told him to kill Trump [Crime / Security]

**What happened:** Secret Service officers approached a man in a parked car on Constitution Avenue and found several firearms, including an AR-15-style weapon, a Mac-10 and a Glock. ## 8. Justice Department appeals dismissal of Alaska Senate certification suit [Courts / Politics]

**What happened:** US District Judge Sharon Gleason dismissed a Justice Department suit seeking to block certification of Alaska's Senate race if Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan lost to Democrat Mary Peltola, citing lack of standing and the states' constitutional role in elections. DOJ said it will appeal. The race remained uncalled with a large share of ballots outstanding.
**Why it matters:** The case is a test of federal efforts to shape election outcomes after Democrats won both chambers.
**Sources:** [AP via WOWT](https://www.wowt.com/2026/11/02/judge-dismisses-lawsuit-seeking-block-certification-alaska-senate-race-if-gop-incumbent-loses/?outputType=amp), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/01/trump-doj-delay-alaska-midterm-results-rejected-judge), [Alaska's News Source](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/06/peltola-sullivan-campaigns-project-wins-while-quarter-ballots-are-outstanding/)

## 10. Hiring rebounds in October as unemployment dips to 4.1% [Economy]

**What happened:** Employers added 165,000 jobs in October, far above forecasts of about 65,000, and the unemployment rate fell to 4.1%. August and September were revised up by 84,000, though hourly wages rose just 3.1% from a year earlier and the broader U-6 measure rose to 7.9%.
**Why it matters:** A stronger labor market adds to Fed rate-hike arguments just as inflation data approaches.
**Sources:** [ABC7 (AP)](https://abc7news.com/story/us-employers-added-surprising-165000-jobs-october-unemployment-rate-dipped-41/20020018/), [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/us-jobs-report-october-2026.amp), [Industry Week](https://www.industryweek.com/talent/news/55417255/october-jobs-report-us-adds-165000-jobs-as-unemployment-falls-slightly-to-41)
