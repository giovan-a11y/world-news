# US Top 10 - Saturday, 07 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A strong jobs report, a new White House chief of staff and a congressional summons for Nvidia's CEO follow a punishing week for stocks.

## 1. Employers add 165,000 jobs in October [Economy]

**What happened:** The Labor Department reported payrolls rose 165,000 in October versus about 65,000 expected, with 84,000 in upward revisions. The unemployment rate fell to 4.1% and participation rose to 61.9%. Manufacturing added 19,000 jobs.
**Why it matters:** Strong hiring keeps pressure on the Fed to tighten as inflation stays elevated.
**Sources:** [Fox Business](https://foxbusiness.com/economy/us-jobs-report-october-2026), [TheStreet](https://pro.thestreet.com/market-commentary/october-payrolls-top-every-forecast-as-unemployment-falls-back-to-4-1)

## 2. Trump names Stephen Miller White House chief of staff [Politics]

**What happened:** Trump said Miller will replace Susie Wiles, effective Monday 9 November. Wiles will move to Trump's political operation. Schumer called Miller the architect of the administration's "most barbaric, inhumane policies."
**Why it matters:** It follows Republican losses in the midterms and signals a harder line heading into divided government.
**Sources:** [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-names-stephen-miller-as-33rd-white-house-chief-of-staff/), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/us/politics/article/3373617/trump-names-stephen-miller-chief-staff-replacing-susie-wiles-after-midterm-losses)

## 3. Stocks end a volatile week [Economy / Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 rose 0.3% to 7,376.86, the Nasdaq gained 1.1% to 24,579.42 and the Dow fell 0.5% to 51,285.73. For the week, the Nasdaq fell 3.4%, the S&P 500 2.7% and the Dow 0.9%. Treasury yields held firmer after the jobs data.
**Why it matters:** The week included the worst day for the S&P 500 since April 2025.
**Sources:** [AP via La Nacion](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/usa/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-1162026-nid06112026/), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3026040/stock-market-news-for-nov-9-2026)

## 4. House Republicans summon Nvidia's CEO over alleged spy link [Business / Politics]

**What happened:** Three House Republican chairs asked Jensen Huang for a closed-door briefing on 18 November about his contacts with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive whom US spy agencies reportedly concluded passed information to Beijing. Nvidia had not confirmed his attendance.
**Why it matters:** It adds political and export-control risk for the leading AI chipmaker.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/05/jensen-huang-nvidia-congress-china), [GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/lawmakers-press-nvidia-ceo-on-defenses-against-foreign-spies-a-33341)

## 5. Democrats outline post-election agenda [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats plan a 100-day agenda on gas, grocery and health costs, and House Democrats plan to quickly move to reverse Republican cuts to the Affordable Care Act and Medicaid. Trump struck a conciliatory tone but threatened vetoes.
**Why it matters:** It sets the terms of divided government from January (earlier reporting; timing of latest remarks unconfirmed).
**Sources:** [GV Wire](https://gvwire.com/2026/11/05/democrats-vow-to-rein-in-trump-as-he-shows-off-white-house-ballroom/), [WBTV](https://www.wbtv.com/2026/11/04/voters-deliver-rebuke-trump-by-flipping-several-gop-held-senate-seats/)
