# US Top 10 - Saturday, 7 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Trump replaced his chief of staff with Stephen Miller days after the midterm rout, a stronger-than-expected jobs report raised Fed-hike bets, and a judge froze work on Trump's arch.

## 1. Trump names Stephen Miller chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles [Politics]

**What happened:** Trump announced on Friday that Miller, the deputy chief of staff and architect of his mass-deportation campaign, will become White House chief of staff on 9 November. Susie Wiles, who said Friday was her last day, moves to the Trump political operation, and Trump told reporters "I wanted to make a change." Newsom called Miller's regime "sadistic", and Democratic House members vowed investigations.
**Why it matters:** The shake-up three days after Democrats won Congress points to a harder line on immigration and a confrontational posture toward oversight.
**Sources:** [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/article/trump-replaces-susie-wiles-with-stephen-miller-as-chief-of-staff-after-gops-midterm-defeat/), [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/06/nation/susie-wiles-replaced-stephen-miller-white-house-chief-of-staff/), [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-names-stephen-miller-as-33rd-white-house-chief-of-staff/?query-11-page=2)

## 2. Employers add 165,000 jobs in October; unemployment falls to 4.1% [Economy]

**What happened:** Payrolls rose 165,000, far above forecasts of about 65,000, and the unemployment rate dipped to 4.1% from 4.2%. Professional and business services added 70,000 jobs and health care 42,000, and August and September were revised up by 84,000. Hourly wages rose just 3.1% over the year, and the broader U-6 rate rose to 7.9%.
**Why it matters:** The strong report solidifies expectations that the Federal Reserve will raise rates in December.
**Sources:** [ABC7 (AP)](https://abc7news.com/story/us-employers-added-surprising-165000-jobs-october-unemployment-rate-dipped-41/20020018/), [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/us-jobs-report-october-2026.amp), [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm?tc=ix)

## 3. Stocks rebound Friday but cap a bruising week for AI shares [Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 rose about 0.3% and the Nasdaq about 1.1% on Friday, a day after an AI-led selloff took the S&P down 4.4% and the Nasdaq 5.5%, wiping out roughly $2.5 trillion. For the week the S&P 500 fell 2.7% to 7,376.86, its worst since April 2025, and the Nasdaq fell 3.37% to 24,579.42.
**Why it matters:** Investors tied the rout to Democrats' midterm sweep and fears of slowed AI infrastructure spending.
**Sources:** [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-november-6-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-jobs-report-earnings-135953863.html), [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/05/markets/asian-markets-us-rout-ai-investment-intl-hnk), [LPL Research](https://www.lpl.com/research/blog/weekly-market-performance-november-6-2026.html)

## 4. Judge temporarily blocks construction of Trump's Washington arch [Courts]

**What happened:** US District Judge Tanya Chutkan paused work on Trump's 250-foot triumphal arch for 14 days, ruling that challengers are likely to show the government skipped required legal processes. The order came shortly after a planning commission approved the project. The plaintiffs include Vietnam veterans, a preservationist, Washington, D.C. and Virginia.
**Why it matters:** The case tests whether the administration can build a major monument without a clear congressional approval.
**Sources:** [Honolulu Star-Advertiser (AP)](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/11/06/breaking-news/judge-blocks-construction-of-trumps-proposed-washington-arch/), [KPTV (AP)](https://www.kptv.com/2026/11/06/federal-judge-orders-14-day-delay-any-work-beginning-trumps-monumental-arch/?outputType=apps), [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/judge-says-she-fears-waking-up-to-bulldozers-in-trump-arch-case)

## 5. Catastrophic 1-in-1,000-year flood hits Georgia-Tennessee border [Weather / Disaster]

**What happened:** Heavy rain caused flash flooding along the Georgia-Tennessee line, with the National Weather Service calling it a 1-in-1,000-year event and up to 12 inches of rain in under 24 hours. Julie Barrs, 56, died after apparently driving through floodwaters in Murray County, Georgia, Tennessee coordinated 68 swift-water rescues, and the Toccoa River set a record at 18 feet. Gov. Brian Kemp declared an emergency for four counties, and the storms moved up the East Coast.
**Why it matters:** It is the second disastrous flood in three months for some of these communities.
**Sources:** [AP via AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/storms-that-caused-1-in-1000-year-flood-event-in-tennessee-and-georgia-move-up-atlantic-coast/), [OPB (AP)](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/06/heavy-rains-flood-towns-dozens-rescued-on-georgia-tennessee-border/)

## 6. Nithya Raman ousts Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass [Politics]

**What happened:** Councilmember Nithya Raman, a progressive, won the LA mayoral runoff, with NBC projecting her victory on Thursday after she held a 52%-48% lead that widened as ballots were counted. Bass conceded and promised a smooth transition. She is the first incumbent LA mayor to lose re-election since Jim Hahn in 2005.
**Why it matters:** The result is another sign of anti-incumbent energy in Democratic cities after the party's midterm wave.
**Sources:** [NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/elections/los-angeles-mayor-election-results-bass-raman/3965846/?amp=1), [Ballotpedia](https://news.ballotpedia.org/?p=52526), [CBS Los Angeles](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/los-angeles-mayor-election-bass-raman-pratt-2026/)

## 7. Senate Democrats face a revolt against Schumer's leadership [Politics]

**What happened:** Several incoming Democratic senators, including Illinois's Juliana Stratton, Kansas's Adam Hamilton, Maine's Troy Jackson and South Carolina's Annie Andrews, have said they will not back Chuck Schumer for majority leader. Michigan's Abdul El-Sayed said he will back Chris Van Hollen. Schumer has said he does not expect a challenge, and the vote is a secret ballot after the election.
**Why it matters:** Democrats will hold a larger Senate majority, but who leads it is now in question.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/11/5/division-among-us-democrats-over-schumers-leadership-role), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/schumer-faces-mutiny-from-newly-elected-senate-dems-party-prepares-seize-power), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/full-list-democrats-rallying-against-chuck-schumer-senate-12315442)

## 8. Consumer sentiment jumps to a three-month high [Economy]

**What happened:** The University of Michigan's preliminary November index rose to 51.0 from 48.0, beating the 48.0 forecast and the best reading since August. Households expect inflation of 4.3% over the next year, down from 4.7% in October.
**Why it matters:** Easing price expectations are a rare bright spot as inflation and tariffs dominate voters' concerns.
**Sources:** [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/consumer-sentiment-rises-november-2026/), [ABA Banking Journal](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2026/11/preliminary-consumer-sentiment-rises-in-november/), [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/news/november-us-umich-prelim-consumer-sentiment-vs-48-0-expected/)
