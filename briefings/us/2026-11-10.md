## 1. Stephen Miller becomes White House chief of staff [Politics]

**What happened:** Trump announced on Friday 6 November that Miller, a deputy chief of staff and architect of the immigration crackdown, would replace Susie Wiles, effective Monday 9 November. Wiles moves to Trump's political operation. Senate Democratic Leader Chuck Schumer called Miller the architect of "the most barbaric, inhumane policies of this administration."
**Why it matters:** The change comes days after Republicans lost both chambers of Congress and signals a harder-edged posture heading into divided government.
**Sources:** [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-names-stephen-miller-as-33rd-white-house-chief-of-staff/), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/us/politics/article/3373617/trump-names-stephen-miller-chief-staff-replacing-susie-wiles-after-midterm-losses)

## 2. Nvidia investigation hits shares [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia shares fell more than 5% on Monday after the company opened an investigation tied to reports about CEO Jensen Huang's contacts with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. House Republican chairs asked Huang for a closed-door briefing on 18 November. The stock is reported down 23% over 30 days.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the biggest AI bellwether and reports earnings on 17 November.
**Sources:** [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/10/why-nvidia-stock-just-fell-again/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/07/jensen-huang-nvidia-congress-china)
