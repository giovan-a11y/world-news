# US Top 10 - Tuesday, 10 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Stocks surged as oil fell, Nvidia's board probe of its CEO deepened, and Stephen Miller took over as White House chief of staff as Massie moved to impeach Hegseth.

## 1. Wall Street rebounds sharply as oil slides [Markets]

**What happened:** On Monday the Dow rose about 1,104 points (2.15%) to 52,389.75, the S&P 500 gained about 2.3% to 7,549.94 and the Nasdaq jumped 2.74% to 25,253.14, pulling it out of correction territory. WTI crude fell about 4.2% to roughly $87. All 11 S&P sectors rose, though Nvidia fell about 4.2% to its lowest since 7 April.
**Why it matters:** The rally recovered most of the prior week's post-election AI selloff, but analysts called it fragile ahead of Tuesday's inflation data.
**Sources:** [GV Wire (AP)](https://gvwire.com/2026/11/09/wall-street-rallies-after-last-weeks-ai-rout-nvidia-falls/), [NST / Reuters](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/corporate/2026/11/1574335/wall-street-jumps-oil-slide-cools-inflation-worries-nvidia), [Trading News](https://www.tradingnews.com/news/sp500-hirts-7517-nasdaq-jumps-578-points-dow-climbs-to-52139)

## 2. Nvidia investigates CEO Jensen Huang over alleged foreign intelligence targeting [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia's board opened an investigation into whether Huang was the target of a "foreign intelligence effort" after he acknowledged an extramarital affair. Shares fell sharply, with estimates of lost market value near $192 billion in one session. The company says he remains committed to the job, and Trump called him an "honorable man".
**Why it matters:** The world's most valuable AI chip company faces a governance crisis ahead of its 17 November earnings.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/9/nvidia-shares-slump-amid-investigation-into-ceo-jensen-huang), [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-affair-board-to-launch-investigation), [Fox Carolina (AP)](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/11/09/nvidia-investigates-whether-ceo-jensen-huang-was-targeted-by-chinese-spy-with-whom-he-had-an-affair/)

## 3. Massie moves to impeach Hegseth over the Iran war [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** Rep. Thomas Massie filed a privileged resolution with eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth as the House returned after the midterms. The first articles accuse him of waging the Iran war without authorization, and others cite Yemen and Venezuela. Hegseth, in his first Pentagon briefing since May, called it a "political spectacle" and said US forces remain ready to strike Iran.
**Why it matters:** House rules force a vote within days, putting Republicans on record on war powers.
**Sources:** [WJLA](https://wjla.com/news/nation-world/defense-secretary-pete-hegseth-holds-first-pentagon-briefing-since-may-as-rep-thomas-massie-republican-kentucky-house-impeachment-vote-nears-iran-war-gop-midterm-elections-losses-president-trump-war-powers-speaker-mike-johnson), [Times of Israel liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-9-2026/), [AP News in Brief](https://www.dailyrecordnews.com/ap_news/us/ap-news-in-brief-at-9-04-p-m-est/article_a4e77958-c3a5-5a7e-9fda-9720ea041a0c.html)

## 4. Trump says Iran war is "militarily effectively over" but US is "locked and loaded" [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** After talks with Qatar's prime minister, Trump said the war is effectively over militarily while keeping the situation at the "status quo" at the behest of Iran's neighbors. Reuters, citing US officials, reports he has received conflicting advice, with Vance and Witkoff backing diplomacy and Hegseth and Adm. Brad Cooper favoring a return to major combat. Cooper ordered a new Task Force Iron Gate to disrupt Iranian arms and illicit revenue flows.
**Why it matters:** A decision between renewed strikes and talks will drive oil prices and troop risk.
**Sources:** [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/666130/world/iran-war-effectively-over-trump-says-after-talks-with-qatar-pm), [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/world/trump-says-iran-war-is-militarily-effectively-over-but-warns-us-remains-locked-and-loaded), [American Legion summary](https://www.legion.org/information-center/news/security/2026/november/five-things-to-know-nov-9-2026)

## 5. Stephen Miller takes over as White House chief of staff [Politics]

**What happened:** Miller, the architect of Trump's mass-deportation push, replaced Susie Wiles as chief of staff on Monday after Trump announced the move on 6 November. Wiles will move to Trump's political operation. Democratic leaders including Rep. Robert Garcia and Sen. Angela Alsobrooks sharply criticized the choice.
**Why it matters:** The shake-up comes days after Republicans lost Congress and signals a harder line on immigration and the coming oversight fights.
**Sources:** [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/article/trump-replaces-susie-wiles-with-stephen-miller-as-chief-of-staff-after-gops-midterm-defeat/), [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/who-is-stephen-miller-trumps-new-white-house-chief-of-staff), [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/9/headlines)

## 6. Democrats' House majority grows as Trump renews fraud claims [Politics] (unconfirmed)

**What happened:** Democracy Now, relaying vote tallies, says Democrats can now claim 254 House seats to Republicans' 162 with 19 races yet to be decided. The Times of Israel liveblog says Trump has renewed baseless allegations of voter fraud, questioning the legitimacy of the elections.

## 7. FDA orders vision-loss warning on GLP-1 weight-loss and diabetes drugs [Health]

**What happened:** The FDA said all GLP-1 drugs, including Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro and Zepbound, must carry a warning about a rare eye condition called NAION that can cause sudden vision loss. One report put the rate at about 6.4 per 100,000 users a year versus 3.8 for comparison groups, and a causal link has not been established.
**Why it matters:** Millions of Americans use these drugs, so the label change affects prescribing and patient counseling.
**Sources:** [Drugwatch](https://drugwatch.com/news/2026/11/09/fda-adds-vision-loss-warning-to-all-glp-1-drugs/), [Powers Health](https://www.powershealth.org/about-us/newsroom/health-library/2026/11/10/rare-eye-condition-prompts-new-fda-warning-on-popular-glp-1-drugs), [Fox 5 NY](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/what-is-naion-ozempic-wegovy-other-glp-1s-linked-rare-eye-condition)

## 8. Ohio State suspends fraternity over video of men on fire [Crime / Campus]

**What happened:** Ohio State placed its Sigma Alpha Epsilon chapter on interim suspension after a video from Saturday showed a crowd laughing as one man's sweatpants caught fire and then a second man's clothing ignited. Both men had moderate injuries, and police reportedly are looking at hazing charges. How the fraternity is linked to the incident is unclear.
**Why it matters:** The case revives scrutiny of fraternity hazing as the university and Columbus police investigate.
**Sources:** [KOLD (AP)](https://www.kold.com/2026/11/09/ohio-state-suspends-fraternity-while-investigating-video-showing-men-engulfed-flames/?outputType=amp), [Philadelphia Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/fraternity-suspend-ohio-state-video-men-fire-sigma-alpha-epsilon-20261110.html)

## 9. Senate advances sweeping permitting reform vehicle [Congress]

**What happened:** The Senate invoked cloture 73-25 on the motion to proceed to H.R. 2347, the legislative vehicle for permitting reform, after Majority Leader Thune filed cloture the prior week.
**Why it matters:** It is one of the few bipartisan items moving before the lame-duck session and the 11 December funding deadline.
**Sources:** [Senate Daily Press, 9 Nov](https://www.dailypress.senate.gov/monday-november-9-2026/), [Senate roll call vote 257](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1192/vote_119_2_00257.htm)

## 10. Cleanup after record flooding in the South and Southwest [Weather]

**What happened:** The National Weather Service described a catastrophic 1-in-1,000-year flood along the Tennessee-Georgia line on 5-6 November, with up to 12 inches of rain in under 24 hours. Earlier flooding hit Arizona, Utah, New Mexico and Colorado, where Pagosa Springs began cleaning up. The Storm Prediction Center forecast no severe storm areas on Monday.
**Why it matters:** Recovery is beginning for communities hit by a cross-country storm that affected more than 120 million people.
**Sources:** [Fox Weather](https://www.foxweather.com/extreme-weather/video-photo-flood-cross-country-storm-southwest-florida-southeast-northeast), [Claims Pages](https://www.claimspages.com/news/catastrophic-flash-flooding-hits-georgia-and-tennessee-as-storm-moves-east-20261106/), [ADN (AP)](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/11/03/receding-floodwaters-leave-sea-of-mud-and-shattered-homes-in-the-southwest/)
