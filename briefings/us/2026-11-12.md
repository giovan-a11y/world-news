# US Top 10 - Thursday, 12 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street rebounded from a hot-inflation selloff, House Republicans blocked an effort to impeach Hegseth, and a building collapse in Brooklyn trapped people in rubble.

## 1. Stocks rebound after hot inflation data revived rate-hike fears [Markets / Economy]

**What happened:** After Tuesday's selloff, when October consumer prices rose 0.4% (core 0.3% against 0.2% expected) and traders priced roughly 86-88% odds of a December Fed hike, stocks bounced on Wednesday as an oil slide eased inflation worries. The S&P 500 gained about 1.3% to roughly 7,619, while Nvidia slumped. Tuesday's close was 7,520.93 on the S&P 500 and 25,065.87 on the Nasdaq.
**Why it matters:** Persistent inflation under Fed Chair Kevin Warsh is pushing markets toward a tightening cycle that would hit mortgages and borrowing costs.
**Sources:** [Honolulu Star-Advertiser (AP)](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/11/10/breaking-news/wall-street-falls-as-hot-inflation-fuels-fed-rate-hike-fears/), [NST / Reuters](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/corporate/2026/11/1574335/wall-street-jumps-oil-slide-cools-inflation-worries-nvidia), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3027683/stock-market-news-for-nov-11-2026)

## 2. House Republicans block Massie's push to impeach Hegseth [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** The House voted 211-204 to refer Rep. Thomas Massie's eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, effectively ending them in this Congress. Massie was the only Republican to oppose the motion, and the articles accused Hegseth of waging an unauthorized war with Iran, among other charges.
**Why it matters:** Democrats will hold a large House majority from January and could revive the effort.
**Sources:** [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/house-gop-leaders-block-massie-hegseth-impeachment-push.amp), [Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/house-tables-hegseth-impeachment-resolution-601151), [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/10/headlines)

## 3. Democrat Scott Colom wins Mississippi Senate seat [Politics]

**What happened:** The AP called the closest Senate race in the country for Colom over Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith after more than a week of counting absentee and provisional ballots, by a few thousand votes. He is the first Black senator from Mississippi since Reconstruction.
**Why it matters:** A flipped seat in the Deep South pushes the Democratic Senate majority beyond a bare margin.
**Sources:** [NPR via KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/npr-news/2026-11-11/democrat-scott-colom-wins-mississippi-senate-race), [Fox 4](https://www.fox4news.com/news/scott-colom-wins-senate-mississippi)

## 4. Building collapse in Brooklyn traps people in rubble [Disaster]

**What happened:** A three-story building at the edge of a construction site at 2625 Bedford Avenue in Flatbush collapsed around 4 p.m. Wednesday. At least one person died and others were trapped, with early updates reporting one dead and three to ten injured as firefighters dug through the wreckage.
**Why it matters:** Excavation beside the building is a likely focus for investigators and for scrutiny of construction-site safety.
**Sources:** [AP via Cleveland19](https://www.cleveland19.com/2026/11/11/new-york-city-building-collapse-traps-people-rubble/), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/americas/firefighters-dig-through-rubble-after-new-york-city-building-collapse-traps-people-in-wreckage-1.500744353)

## 5. Trump says US has won the Iran war but wants "total victory" [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** Trump claimed Washington has already won the war against Iran but does not want to stop until there is a knockout. Speaking at Arlington National Cemetery on Veterans Day, he honored veterans at the 73rd National Veterans Day Observance, with the Iran war, which began on 28 February, a backdrop. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Trump and Xi Jinping will meet next week in Shenzhen.
**Why it matters:** The rhetoric clashes with reports of diplomacy and a hold on new strikes, leaving the path of the war unclear.

## 6. Bessent moves to a military base after Iranian threats [Security]

**What happened:** The New York Times reported that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent recently moved from his home onto a military base after Iranian threats against his life. Bessent has said he will not be cowed.
**Why it matters:** It shows the personal security risks facing US officials as the Iran conflict drags on.
**Sources:** [Just Security Early Edition](https://www.justsecurity.org/164111/early-edition-november-11-2026/), [Democracy Now headlines](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/13/headlines)

## 7. Labor Department proposes five weekly job searches for jobless benefits [Economy / Policy]

**What happened:** The Labor Department proposed a national minimum of five qualifying work-search activities a week for people claiming unemployment insurance, using a point system. Only four states require five today, and many require two or three. Comments are due 60 days after Federal Register publication.
**Why it matters:** Critics at the Economic Policy Institute say the rule would be devastating to claimants and cost states money.
**Sources:** [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-work-search-rules-unemployment-benefits/), [Department of Labor](https://www.dol.gov/index.php/newsroom/releases/eta/eta20261109), [WFSB](https://www.wfsb.com/2026/11/10/trump-administration-considers-5-job-searches-weekly-jobless-benefits/)

## 8. Judge lets Trump's $10 billion BBC defamation suit proceed [Courts / Media]

**What happened:** US District Judge Jeffrey Kuntz in Florida refused to dismiss Trump's defamation claim against the BBC, finding it plausible that the broadcaster harmed his reputation, while throwing out a Florida consumer-protection claim. The BBC said it was surprised by the decision, and Trump called it a "huge victory".
**Why it matters:** The case, with a provisional trial date of February 2027 and a 11 December deposition deadline for Trump, will test US defamation law against a foreign broadcaster.
**Sources:** [GV Wire](https://gvwire.com/2026/11/11/judge-allows-trumps-defamation-suit-against-bbc-to-proceed/), [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/11/11/trumps-10bn-bbc-defamation-lawsuit-can-proceed-judge-rules), [Anadolu](https://aa.com.tr/en/americas/us-judge-allows-10b-defamation-lawsuit-against-bbc-to-proceed/4119887)
