# US Top 10 - Thursday, 12 November 2026

**Today in one line:** The White House warns Democrats that planned oversight probes could be criminal, Wall Street bounces back, and Nvidia faces a congressional deadline over its CEO's reported ties to an alleged Chinese intelligence asset.

## 1. White House warns House Democrats that planned probes could be "criminal" [Politics / Courts]

**What happened:** AP reports White House counsel Will Scharf sent a letter, dated Monday, to Rep. Robert Garcia, the top Democrat on the House Oversight Committee, saying planned investigations could amount to "blatant criminality." Democrats are looking at the Trump family's wealth, the White House ballroom and other construction, mineral deals and Trump's crypto dealings with envoy Steve Witkoff. Garcia said "It appears Donald Trump has something to hide." Oversight Democrats said the committee had not yet received the letter. Attorney General Todd Blanche said on Sunday the Justice Department would defend officials facing probes.
**Why it matters:** It previews the oversight and executive-power fights expected once Democrats take control of the House in January.
**Sources:** [AP via WCAX](https://www.wcax.com/2026/11/11/white-house-confronts-democrats-over-planned-probes-claiming-possible-criminality/), [AP via KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51645181/the-latest-white-house-claims-criminality-in-democratic-oversight)

## 3. Nvidia CEO faces a deadline over alleged spy link [Business / Tech / Politics]

**What happened:** Three House Republican committee chairs asked Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang for a closed-door briefing on 18 November about his contacts with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive that US intelligence reportedly concluded was passing information to Beijing. Huang had until 11 November to respond. Nvidia's board has opened its own independent investigation. The original report did not say he knowingly aided espionage.
**Why it matters:** The world's most valuable AI chipmaker reports earnings on 17 November while under political and export-control pressure.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/07/jensen-huang-nvidia-congress-china), [GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/lawmakers-press-nvidia-ceo-on-defenses-against-foreign-spies-a-33341)
