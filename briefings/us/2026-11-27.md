# US Top 10 - Friday, 27 November 2026

**Today in one line:** On Thanksgiving, with markets closed, the US is weighing a post-midterm political reset, hotter inflation and a shaky AI-led stock market.

## 1. Democrats win both chambers of Congress [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats won the House and the Senate in the 3 November midterms, flipping more Senate seats than expected. They take office in January with plans for oversight of the Iran war and immigration enforcement.
**Why it matters:** Trump loses unified control; legislation will need his signature and judicial confirmations face a Democratic Senate.
**Sources:** [OPB/NPR](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/04/2026-midterms-democrats-win-the-senate/), [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/4791815/takeaways-2026-midterm-elections/)
