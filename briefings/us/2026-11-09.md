# US Top 10 - Monday, 9 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Nvidia's board opened a probe of its CEO, Democrats' midterm sweep set up early fights in Washington, and Notre Dame upset top-ranked Miami.

## 1. Nvidia board opens probe after CEO Jensen Huang admits affair [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Axios reported that Huang admitted to a relationship with a suspected Chinese spy in a message to staff, apologizing to his family and colleagues. Nvidia's board said an independent committee is investigating reports that he was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort, that it has "no reason to believe" national security or company secrets were compromised, and that Huang will remain CEO for now.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the market's heaviest-weighted stock, so a leadership crisis could move AI valuations and US-China chip policy.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-affair-board-to-launch-investigation), [Fox Carolina (AP)](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/11/09/nvidia-investigates-whether-ceo-jensen-huang-was-targeted-by-chinese-spy-with-whom-he-had-an-affair/), [AP News Summary](https://www.dailyrecordnews.com/ap_news/us/ap-news-summary-at-6-59-a-m-est/article_532f4874-e829-5bf5-a1a0-6c29e02bf6ec.html)

**What happened:** Meet the Press described a political earthquake not seen since the Watergate era, with Democrats seizing both the House and Senate.
