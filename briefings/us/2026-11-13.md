# US Top 10 - Friday, 13 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Nvidia's CEO resigned and shook Wall Street, Democrats flipped Mississippi to reach 55 Senate seats, and a deadly Brooklyn building collapse dominated the domestic news.

## 1. Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia said Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member effective immediately on Thursday. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent chief, and the Wall Street Journal had reported the board was preparing to oust him. Reuters tied the exit to reports of an affair with a Chinese businesswoman whom US authorities suspect of spying for Beijing.
**Why it matters:** The departure of the face of the AI boom raises questions about the chipmaker's direction, US chip sales to China and AI valuations.
**Sources:** [CNBC live updates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/why-did-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resign-and-who-is-colette-kress), [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm)

## 2. Wall Street slips as Nvidia tumbles and yields rise [Markets]

**What happened:** On Thursday the S&P 500 fell 0.35% to 7,592.40, the Nasdaq 0.39% to 25,366.09 and the Dow 0.36% to 52,297.09, per Reuters-based reports. Nvidia closed about 4.3% lower after sliding as much as 14.3% earlier, while AMD gained 5.9% and Broadcom 5.5%. Treasury yields rose.
**Why it matters:** The market now faces Nvidia's earnings and hawkish Fed signals after a hot October inflation reading.
**Sources:** [Euronext / Reuters](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/wall-street-ends-lower-nvidia-ceo-resigns-us-yields), [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262303336-us-stock-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-nasdaq-falls-tradingkey), [Yahoo Finance](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/live/stock-market-today-thursday-november-12-sp-500-nasdaq-dow-nvidia-slides-jensen-huang-resigns-invest-165632931.html)

## 3. Democrat Scott Colom wins Mississippi Senate seat [Politics]

**What happened:** The AP called the closest Senate race in the country for Colom over incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith after more than a week of counting absentee and provisional ballots, a margin of a few thousand votes. He becomes the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction and lifts Democrats to 55 seats.
**Why it matters:** The win widens the Democratic Senate majority well beyond the bare control secured on 3 November.
**Sources:** [NPR via KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/npr-news/2026-11-11/democrat-scott-colom-wins-mississippi-senate-race), [Fox 4](https://www.fox4news.com/news/scott-colom-wins-senate-mississippi), [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/12/scott_colom_victory_speech)

## 4. Brooklyn building collapse kills several, many injured [Disaster]

**What happened:** A three-story building at the edge of a construction site at 2625 Bedford Avenue in Flatbush collapsed on Wednesday afternoon, trapping people in the rubble. Early reports counted one dead; later roundups said at least two died with a dozen or more injured, including children, and an AP digest reported the toll had risen to eight (counts differ by source).
**Why it matters:** Investigators are looking at excavation beside the building, putting construction oversight under scrutiny.
**Sources:** [AP via Cleveland19](https://www.cleveland19.com/2026/11/11/new-york-city-building-collapse-traps-people-rubble/), [AP News digest (Sitka Sentinel)](https://sitkasentinel.com/stories/ap-news-digest-12-pm,185804), [Morning News Update, 12 Nov](https://ru4people.substack.com/p/morning-news-update-november-12-2026)

## 5. Vance weighs direct talks with Iran's security chief as threats mount [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** The Wall Street Journal reported Vice President JD Vance is considering face-to-face talks with Iranian security chief Mohsen Rezaei, though US officials called the idea preliminary and Rezaei dismissed the reports. A roundup said Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has moved to a military base after Iranian threats against his life, and that Trump would not hesitate to renew strikes.

**What happened:** Five people were shot in downtown Washington, DC, one fatally, at a gym, according to the AP digest. The suspect died by suicide. A separate roundup describes one death and four wounded.
**Why it matters:** It is another high-casualty shooting in the capital, where federal policing has been a political flashpoint.

## 7. Oklahoma executes James Pavatt for 2001 killing [Crime / Justice]

**What happened:** Oklahoma put to death a man convicted of a 2001 killing, the first of two US executions that day per AP. James Pavatt, convicted of the 2001 murder of advertising executive Rob Andrew, had been denied clemency 3-2 by the state's parole board in October.
**Why it matters:** It continues a high pace of executions in 2026, the state's fourth this year.
**Sources:** [AP News digest (Sitka Sentinel)](https://sitkasentinel.com/stories/ap-news-digest-12-pm,185804), [News 9](https://www.news9.com/crime/oklahoma-death-row-james-pavatt)

## 8. Alaska Senate race: Peltola widens lead with 15% of ballots left [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrat Mary Peltola expanded her narrow lead over Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan, with about 15% of ballots still to be counted. The race is uncalled, and the ranked-choice tabulation is scheduled for 18 November.
**Why it matters:** The outcome decides the final size of the Democratic Senate majority.
**Sources:** [Alaska Beacon / Washington State Standard](https://washingtonstatestandard.com/2026/11/11/repub/with-25000-more-votes-counted-peltola-jkt-and-begich-near-victory-in-alaska-election/), [Morning News Update, 12 Nov](https://ru4people.substack.com/p/morning-news-update-november-12-2026)

## 9. US envoys meet Putin and Zelenskyy after Abu Dhabi talks [Diplomacy]

**What happened:** US envoys travelled to Moscow to brief Vladimir Putin after US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi that produced a large prisoner exchange but no visible breakthrough on ending the war. They then met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Dublin.
**Why it matters:** The talks are Washington's main channel on Ukraine as Trump prepares for a possible three-way meeting with Xi and Putin.

## 10. International panel weighs US measles elimination status [Health]

**What happened:** International health officials met to decide whether the United States and Mexico have lost measles-elimination status, with a decision expected the following week. Elimination is lost when one strain spreads continuously for 12 months.
**Why it matters:** A loss would formalise the setback after record US cases and weaker vaccination coverage.
**Sources:** [AP News in Brief](https://www.ottumwacourier.com/news/national_news/ap-news-in-brief-at-12-04-a-m-est/article_b4d0c169-c0da-5066-b56b-cfef7164b364.html), [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/public-health/measles-free-status-us-cdc-ralph-abraham-paho-who-outbreaks-vaccines/)
