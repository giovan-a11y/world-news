# US Top 10 - Sunday, 15 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A nationwide air-traffic ground stop left thousands of flights cancelled, Trump signalled restraint on Iran, and Wall Street ended a winning week under a cloud over Nvidia's CEO.

## 1. Thousands of flights cancelled after longest nationwide ground stop in 25 years [Transport / Business]

**What happened:** The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop on Friday afternoon with about 3,500 flights in the air, the longest in 25 years. On Saturday FlightAware counted more than 4,100 cancellations, while other outlets reported higher totals. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed a router at an FAA site in El Dorado, Arkansas, being replaced by contractor L3Harris; air traffic controllers dispute that explanation.
**Why it matters:** FAA chief Bryan Bedford said there was "zero evidence" of anything nefarious, but a fragile system and days of recovery land just before Thanksgiving travel.
**Sources:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/faa-ground-stop-flights-resume-delays.html), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/faa-ground-stop-canceled-flights-travelers-what-we-know/), [Common Dreams](https://www.commondreams.org/news/duffy-ground-stop-explanation)

## 2. Trump tells advisers he may hold off on attacking Iran [Politics / Security]

**What happened:** Trump has told senior advisers he may refrain from new strikes on Iran if there are no fresh provocations. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said earlier in the week that the naval blockade and economic pressure make major combat operations unnecessary for now. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said kinetic strikes around the Strait of Hormuz are not ruled out.
**Why it matters:** The posture shapes oil prices, US troop risk and the prospects for a negotiated settlement after the midterms.
**Sources:** [Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/11/marco-rubio-iran-military-operations-unnecessary-negotiations/), [i24NEWS](https://i24news.tv/en/news/international/americas/artc-us-has-no-immediate-plans-for-new-strikes-on-iran-report)

## 3. Wall Street ends a winning week despite Friday slide [Markets]

**What happened:** On Friday 13 November the S&P 500 fell 0.4% to 7,561.67 and the Nasdaq fell 0.9% to 25,136.27, while the Dow edged up 0.1% to 52,347.55. For the week the S&P gained 2.5%, its first winning week since October and best since August. Technology led the decline, with Nvidia sliding again a day after Jensen Huang's resignation.
**Why it matters:** The rebound sets up a heavy week of Nvidia earnings, retail results and Fed commentary.
**Sources:** [How major US stock indexes fared (AP via WTOP)](https://wtop.com/national/2026/11/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-11-13-2026/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-futures-tick-133734502.html)

## 4. Nvidia hunts for a CEO after Jensen Huang resigns amid scandal [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member effective immediately on 12 November, after a board probe into reported ties to an alleged Chinese intelligence operative and his admission of an affair. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and the Financial Times reports the board has approached Arm CEO Rene Haas. Some staff are reportedly calling for his return, and Trump has suggested Apple's Tim Cook as a replacement.
**Why it matters:** The world's most valuable AI chip company is leaderless days before its 17 November earnings report, and shares have been under heavy pressure.
**Sources:** [CNBC live updates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/why-did-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resign-and-who-is-colette-kress), [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm)

**Why it matters:** One seat can decide how large Democrats' new Senate majority is.
