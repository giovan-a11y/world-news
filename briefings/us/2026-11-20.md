# US Top 10 - Friday, 20 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street's AI-led sell-off deepened to a sixth straight loss while markets price in more Fed rate hikes and Trump returns from the Shenzhen summit.

## 1. Stocks tumble more than 2%, longest losing streak in 2.5 years [Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 fell 2.02% to 7,175.83 on Thursday, the Dow lost 1,101 points to 49,097 and the Nasdaq dropped 2.25%. Nvidia fell 3.3% for a sixth losing session.
**Why it matters:** Retirement accounts and corporate sentiment are exposed to the AI trade, which now looks fragile.
**Sources:** [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-nov-19-dow-plunges-1-100-points-as-the-s-and-p-500-suffers-its-6th-straight-day-of-losses/), [Reuters via Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-street-ends-sharply-lower-asanthropics-debut-fails-to-revive-ai-fervor-5040108)

## 2. Anthropic debuts on Nasdaq in one of the largest IPOs ever [Business]

**What happened:** The AI company listed Thursday, seeking up to about $64.4 billion at $70-78 a share. An early jump faded and shares did not lift the sector, per Reuters.
**Why it matters:** It tests how much capital public markets will give AI developers while valuations are under pressure.
**Sources:** [Reuters via Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-street-ends-sharply-lower-asanthropics-debut-fails-to-revive-ai-fervor-5040108), [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/anthropic-ipo-frenzy-spcx-cbrs-arm-mega-listings-show-what-happens-after-the-first-day-pop/cNbaHhER9Wl)

## 3. Fed hike expectations build for December [Economy]

**What happened:** After the Fed's first hike in September, markets price a high chance (reports range from about 69% to 82%) of another quarter-point rise at the 8-9 December meeting. Cleveland Fed's Beth Hammack has said inflation will not return to 2% on its own, and a hot CPI report this month pushed yields higher.
**Why it matters:** Higher rates raise borrowing costs and pressure equities; the 10-year yield is near 5%.
**Sources:** [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/fed-hammack-inflation-action-rate-hike/), [TheStreet](https://pro.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/2026-11-13), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3027683/stock-market-news-for-nov-11-2026)

## 4. Trump leaves China after summit with Xi and Putin [Politics]

**What happened:** Trump headed home after the trilateral meeting in Shenzhen, which produced a joint statement on nuclear risk, and is due to brief the press on Friday.
**Why it matters:** Any follow-on arms-control talks would be a major shift in US-Russia-China relations.
**Sources:** [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/live-blog/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-congress-2026-live-updates-rcna609888), [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/trump-xi-putin-apec-meeting-9.7427718)
