# US Top 10 - Thursday, 26 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street clawed back a violent AI-led sell-off before the Thanksgiving break, while hot inflation data and tariff plans keep the policy outlook uncertain.

*Editor's note: web access was limited today. Fewer than ten stories could be sourced responsibly; single-source items are marked "(unconfirmed)".*

## 1. Stocks recover most of a steep AI sell-off [Markets]

**What happened:** On 25 November the S&P 500 fell as much as 3.2% and the Nasdaq as much as 4.6% before recovering. The S&P closed down about 0.25% near 7,183, the Nasdaq down 0.62%, and the Dow up 0.33% at 50,182.95. Nvidia, Oracle and Micron led the declines, while most S&P 500 stocks rose.
**Why it matters:** The breadth of the rebound suggests the pressure is concentrated in AI leaders, but the intraday swings are among the largest this year.
**Sources:** [Closing Look](https://cappnotes.substack.com/p/closing-look-wednesday-november-25), [Stock Titan](https://stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-25-2026)

## 2. Growth revised up, inflation hotter than expected [Economy]

**What happened:** Q3 GDP growth was reportedly revised to 4.3% from 3.4%, and core PCE inflation rose to 3.3%, above forecasts (single-source figures; unconfirmed). The 10-year Treasury yield fell to roughly 4.8%, an eighth straight decline.
**Why it matters:** Late-October coverage put December rate-hike odds above 80%, so the data keeps tightening, not cuts, on the table.
**Sources:** [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/september-2026-pce-inflation.amp), [BabyPips](https://www.babypips.com/news/headline-us-q3-gdp-core-pce-december-fed-rate-hike-focus-2026-10-30)

## 5. Democrats' House majority is the largest in decades [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrats flipped the House in the midterms and, with remaining races counted, could hold about 259-260 seats, the most since the early 1990s. OPB reported a popular-vote margin of about 14 points.
**Why it matters:** The majority gives Democrats strong oversight and spending leverage when the new Congress convenes.
**Sources:** [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/19/democrats-house-win-was-the-largest-in-50-years/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/democratic-party-gains-us-house-majority-in-2026-midterm-elections)

## 7. Iowa Army plant lead-azide disposal [Safety]

**What happened:** The Army planned to destroy about 9,700 pounds of degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown in controlled blasts, after the EPA issued an emergency permit. Headlines on 25 November mentioned a disaster emergency in three counties and an appeals court clearing the way; I could not confirm the outcome.
**Why it matters:** The plume raised lead-exposure concerns for nearby residents, and the episode highlights aging munitions stockpiles.
**Sources:** [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/iowa-army-plant-lead-azide-detonation/story?id=138261613), [Task & Purpose](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/army-iowa-munitions-lead-detonation/)
