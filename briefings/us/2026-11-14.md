# US Top 10 - Saturday, 14 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A nationwide FAA ground stop grounded flights across the country, Nvidia's CEO quit amid scandal, and Senate Democrats opened a fight over their own leadership.

## 1. FAA halts all US flights in longest nationwide ground stop in 25 years [Transport / Business]

**What happened:** The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop at 3:36 p.m. ET Friday with about 3,500 flights airborne, blaming a communications outage. Flights resumed that evening after hours, but more than 2,000 Saturday flights were already cancelled per FlightAware. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was no sign of a national security threat but would not rule out any cause, including a cyberattack.
**Why it matters:** A near-total halt to US aviation just before Thanksgiving travel raises fresh questions about the resilience of air traffic control systems.
**Sources:** [CNN live](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/faa-ground-stop-canceled-flights-travelers-what-we-know/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/faa-ground-stop-flights-resume-delays.html)

## 2. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns, CFO Colette Kress named interim chief [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia said Huang resigned as president, CEO and director effective immediately, days after a board investigation opened into reported ties to an alleged Chinese intelligence operative and his admission of an affair. Kress will serve as interim CEO while remaining CFO, and Harvey C. Jones becomes the first board chair. Trump suggested Apple's Tim Cook as a successor on social media.
**Why it matters:** The exit of the AI boom's most prominent executive unsettled markets and raises questions over US chip policy.
**Sources:** [CNBC live updates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/why-did-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resign-and-who-is-colette-kress), [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/13/headlines)

## 3. Stocks dip on Friday but major indexes post weekly gains [Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 fell 0.4% to 7,561.67 and the Nasdaq fell 0.9% to 25,136.27, with Nvidia and other tech names sinking, while the Dow edged up to 52,347.55. For the week the S&P rose 2.5%, its first weekly gain since October, the Dow gained 2.1% and the Nasdaq 2.3%.
**Why it matters:** A shaky finish to a rebound week leaves investors waiting on Nvidia earnings and inflation-driven Fed signals.

## 5. Senate Democrats grow their majority, then face a leadership fight [Politics]

**What happened:** Democrat Scott Colom's Mississippi win, as reported by Democracy Now, lifts Democrats' Senate majority to 55 and makes him the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction. Sen. Chris Van Hollen said he will challenge Chuck Schumer for Senate leader, and activists staged a sit-in in Schumer's office in which more than 50 were reportedly arrested.
**Why it matters:** Democrats enter the new Congress with a larger Senate majority but divided over who leads it and whether to end the filibuster.
**Sources:** [Democracy Now headlines](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/13/headlines), [Democracy Now: Colom](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/12/scott_colom_victory_speech)
