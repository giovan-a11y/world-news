# US Top 10 - Wednesday, 25 November 2026

**Today in one line:** New tariffs, a violent Nvidia-driven market swing and a busy lame-duck Congress headline the pre-Thanksgiving news cycle.

## 1. Administration set to unveil Section 301 tariffs [Trade]

**What happened:** The administration is preparing tariffs after an excess-capacity probe covering 16 trading partners, with China the main target. Rates were not finalised and a US-China truce runs to 10 January.
**Why it matters:** The duties replace tariffs the Supreme Court struck down and could raise prices as Democrats campaign on affordability.
**Sources:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/24/trump-tariffs-china-affordability), [US News/Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-11-24/trump-administration-set-to-unveil-new-tariffs-after-probe-on-excess-factory-capacity-sources-say)
