# US Top 10 - Wednesday, 18 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Wall Street is sliding on AI-stock jitters and 5%-plus Treasury yields, with Nvidia's first post-Huang earnings and Anthropic's IPO the next tests.

## 1. Stocks fall again as AI shares swing lower [Economy / Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 fell about 1% and the Dow about 1.6% (848 points) on Tuesday, led by Nvidia and other AI stocks. The S&P was on track for its first back-to-back losses of 1.5% or more since March.
**Why it matters:** AI names have driven the market's gains, so weakness there hits retirement accounts and the broader economy's mood.
**Sources:** [AP via BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/dow-jones/2026/11/17/world-markets-mostly-drop-as-ai-stocks-remain-under-pressure/), [WISH-TV](https://www.wishtv.com/news/business/wall-street-falls-as-nvidia-and-other-ai-stocks-swing-sharply/)

## 2. Treasury yields above 5% [Economy]

**What happened:** The 10-year yield was about 5.1% and the 30-year near 5.48% (reports differ slightly). A report showed stronger-than-expected retail spending in an underlying measure.
**Why it matters:** Higher yields raise borrowing costs for mortgages and businesses and weigh on growth stocks. A Fed rate hike is widely expected in December per earlier reporting.
**Sources:** [AP via BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/dow-jones/2026/11/17/world-markets-mostly-drop-as-ai-stocks-remain-under-pressure/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nvidia-rebounds-141544786.html)

## 3. Nvidia earnings, first since CEO Huang's exit [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** Nvidia reported fiscal Q3 results after the close on Tuesday; expectations were for $2.47 adjusted EPS on about $108.9 billion revenue. Results were not available when this was written.
**Why it matters:** The stock is down about 26% in a month and the report will steer the whole AI trade.
**Sources:** [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nvidia-26-142802182.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/anthropic-ipo-jensen-huang-resignation-nvidia/)

## 4. Anthropic IPO set to price [Business / Tech]

**What happened:** The San Francisco AI company's Nasdaq IPO (ticker ANT) is expected to price on 18 November, with a reported range of about $70-78 a share and roughly $26 billion of anchor commitments.
**Why it matters:** It is a landmark listing and a read on whether investors still want AI exposure.
**Sources:** [Investing Live](https://investinglive.com/stocks/anthropic-lines-up-26-billion-from-nvidia-google-others-as-ipo-priced-at-70-to-80/), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/13/can-the-anthropic-ipo-save-the-ai-rally/)

**Why it matters:** American forces remain in harm's way in a ninth-month war that is also feeding oil and market anxiety.
**Sources:** [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/17/nx-s1-6044255/us-iran-war-latest-bahrain), [Reuters via Investing.com](https://za.investing.com/news/commodities-news/iran-attacks-us-military-radar-system-in-bahrain-despite-talks-with-mediators-4580662)
