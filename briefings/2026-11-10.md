# World Top 10 - Tuesday, 10 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Trump called the Iran war "effectively over" while keeping the Hormuz blockade, Wall Street bounced from its AI sell-off even as Nvidia sank on its CEO probe, and the COP31 climate summit opened in Turkey.

## 1. Trump calls the Iran war "effectively over" after meeting Qatar's prime minister [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** After talks in Washington with Qatar's prime minister on 9 November, Trump said he would hold off new strikes for now, after appeals from regional states, and called the war militarily effectively over, while keeping the Strait of Hormuz blockade. Iranian officials dismissed the claim as "fake news"; Rubio said a breakthrough was not imminent and rejected Iran's truce terms as unrealistic. US strikes on Iran's Qeshm island were confirmed earlier in the week.
**Why it matters:** Whether pauses turn into a deal decides if one of the world's key oil routes reopens or fighting resumes.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/trump-calls-iran-war-effectively-over-after-talks-with-qatar-pm), [Al-Monitor](https://al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-says-he-will-hold-fresh-iran-strikes-now), [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/trump-meet-qatar-pm-iran-war-peace-deal-doubts)

## 2. Wall Street rebounds from AI rout as oil falls [Markets]

**What happened:** US stocks rose on 9 November, with all 11 S&P 500 sectors higher; the S&P 500 bounced from its biggest weekly drop since April 2025 and the chip index jumped 6.9%, its best day since late July. A drop in oil prices eased inflation fears, and Morgan Stanley and UBS called the previous week's AI sell-off overdone.
**Why it matters:** The rebound followed a sell-off triggered by fears that Democratic midterm gains could bring tougher regulation of AI and data centres.
**Sources:** [Honolulu Star-Advertiser / Reuters](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/11/09/breaking-news/wall-street-rallies-after-last-weeks-ai-rout-nvidia-falls/), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/347996/Wall-St-jumps-back-as-oil-slide-eases-inflation-worries-Nvidia-slumps)

## 3. Nvidia drops about 4-5% as board probes CEO Jensen Huang [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia fell roughly 4.2% to 5.6% (sources differ) to its lowest since April, after its board opened an independent investigation on 8 November into reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. Huang admitted an extramarital affair; the board hired outside counsel, says it has confidence in him and has "no reason to believe" secrets were compromised. Claims of a link to an alleged Chinese-intelligence-linked executive are unconfirmed.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the AI trade's anchor, and the probe lands nine days before its earnings.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/9/nvidia-shares-slump-amid-investigation-into-ceo-jensen-huang), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-board-jensen-huang-inquiry-directors-succession), [Motley Fool](https://fool.com/investing/2026/11/09/why-nvidia-stock-was-down-today)

## 4. COP31 climate summit opens in Antalya [Climate]

**What happened:** The UN climate conference opened on 9 November in Antalya, Turkey, and runs to 20 November, with a Turkey-Australia co-presidency and a focus on turning pledges into action. Climate finance sparked an early fight, UN chief Guterres told leaders to "get serious", and scientists warn 2026 may be among the hottest years on record. The US sent no official delegation.
**Why it matters:** Finance for poorer countries is the central test, with the US absent.
**Sources:** [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/cop31-opens-leaders-pressure-tackle-climate-crisis), [Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/cop31-opens-amid-warnings-that-2026-will-be-among-hottest-years-on-record/), [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c363e0p-cop31-opens-antalya-with-hopes-turn-climate)
