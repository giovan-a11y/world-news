# World Top 10 - Friday, 13 November 2026

**Today in one line:** Nvidia's CEO resigned and US stocks slipped as Treasury yields hit 5.1%, while Trump's rejection of an Iran deal pushed oil up and Anthropic's record IPO roadshow ran on.

## 1. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns, leadership reset hits tech [Technology]

**What happened:** Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang resigned as CEO after a board investigation into his conduct and alleged ties to a suspected Chinese spy; CFO Colette Kress is reported as interim chief. Shares plunged at the open, recovered much of the loss and closed down 4.34%.
**Why it matters:** Nvidia is the AI-chip bellwether and a huge weight in US indexes, so a founder-CEO exit raises questions about AI spending and the company's direction ahead of earnings.
**Sources:** [Eurasia Business News](https://eurasiabusinessnews.com/2026/11/13/stock-market-today-november-12-2026-dow-sp-500-and-nasdaq-fall-after-jensen-huang-resigns/), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/tech/article/3373724/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-affair-board-orders-investigation), [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/nvidia-jensen-huang-succession-plan-investigation-3809602)

## 2. Wall Street slips as 10-year Treasury yield hits 5.10% [Markets]

**What happened:** The S&P 500 closed at about 7,592 (-0.35%), the Dow at 52,297 (-0.36%) and the Nasdaq at 25,366 (-0.39%), while small caps fell 1.66%. Treasury yields climbed to 5.10% and the S&P 500 is still up about 2.9% on the week after a rebound.
**Why it matters:** Rising long-term yields raise borrowing costs worldwide and weigh on valuations, especially in tech and small companies.

## 3. Trump says he is no longer interested in a deal with Iran; oil rises [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Reports say President Trump said he is no longer interested in a deal with Iran, a sharp turn after talk earlier in the week of "momentum" following his meeting with Qatar's prime minister. Oil rose on the news.
**Why it matters:** It lowers hopes for a negotiated end to the US-Iran confrontation and keeps risk of renewed fighting and energy disruption in the Gulf alive.
**Sources:** [Vista PGlobal](https://vistapglobal.com/stock-market-today-november-12-2026-stocks-fall-as-treasury-yields-hit-5-10-oil-rises-and-nvidia-faces-leadership-reset-amd-crwv-eprx-googl-modd-msft-nvda-ser-spcx-ttek-ual/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/9/iran-war-live-trump-says-war-effectively-over-iran-demands-surrender) (single-source on the reversal, unconfirmed)

## 6. Hot US inflation keeps a December Fed hike in view [Economy]

**What happened:** October consumer prices came in above expectations, and a Treasury 10-year auction drew a high yield on weak demand. Traders are pricing a likely December rate hike by the Federal Reserve.
**Why it matters:** A hiking Fed tightens financial conditions globally and strengthens the dollar, which pressures emerging markets and dollar-pegged Hong Kong.
**Sources:** [Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/news/live-news/article-2-2246094-20261110), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3027683/stock-market-news-for-nov-11-2026)

## 7. COP31 leaders' segment in Antalya focuses on finance [Climate]

**What happened:** Day 4 of the UN climate summit (12 November) featured the second day of the Leaders' Summit, with a roundtable on accelerating Paris Agreement implementation and access to finance, plus a business forum. The presidency is split between Turkey and Australia, who have disagreed over who runs negotiations.
**Why it matters:** Climate finance for vulnerable countries is the make-or-break issue, in a year forecast to rank among the hottest on record.
**Sources:** [UNFCCC schedule](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/COP_31_Overview_Schedule.pdf), [Earth Negotiations Bulletin](https://enb.iisd.org/antalya-un-climate-change-conference-cop31)

## 8. Record Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak continues in DRC [Health]

**Why it matters:** The scale and high fatality rate strain health systems and raise regional spread concerns.
**Sources:** [UK Health Security Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/outbreaks-under-monitoring-in-2026/outbreaks-under-monitoring-week-44-week-ending-1-november-2026) (single source, unconfirmed)
