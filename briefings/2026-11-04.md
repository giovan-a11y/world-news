# World Top 10 - Wednesday, 04 November 2026

**Today in one line:** US voters head to the midterm polls with the Iran war and fuel prices at the centre of the vote, as oil swings on Qeshm strikes and Asian tech stocks tumble.

## 1. US midterm elections held amid Iran war and cost-of-living anger [Politics]

**What happened:** Americans voted on 3 November in midterms widely seen as a verdict on Trump, the Iran war and inflation. Prediction markets put a Democratic sweep at about 51% beforehand, and the Supreme Court had rejected administration efforts to curb mail ballots.
**Why it matters:** The result will decide whether Trump can keep funding the war and pushing his agenda.
**Sources:** [Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/us-midterm-update-latest-polymarket-pricing-puts-democratic-sweep-at-51), [Newsquawk (Supreme Court)](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/us-supreme-court-rejects-trump-administration-mail-ballot-curbs-for-midterms), [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/52982/business/stocks-and-dollar-drop-as-democrats-enjoy-big-early-wins/)

## 2. US strikes Iran's Qeshm Island on election eve; Trump plays down war risk [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** US forces carried out limited, defensive strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island late on 2 November, officials told CNN, NBC and AP. Trump confirmed them but said he was "not looking to kill lots of people" after the election, adding that a deal with Tehran was still possible. Vice President Vance said Washington had made "progress" in talks.
**Why it matters:** The strikes show the blockade standoff can flare at any moment even as diplomacy continues.
**Sources:** [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/middle_east/2026-11-03/us-strikes-iran-qeshm-island-hormuz-23348317.html), [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/us-forces-strike-iran-qeshm-island-midterm-elections-1829754), [Bernama](https://bernama.com/en/world/news.php?id=2629268)

## 3. Oil drops about 7.6% on diplomacy hopes, then whipsaws [Energy]

**What happened:** On Monday Brent settled at $104.77, down 7.6%, and WTI at $96.46, down 7.4%, as renewed hopes for negotiations outweighed strike threats. Prices swung more than $6 intraday, peaking at $107.24 after the Qeshm news before falling back to about $100.80, and on Tuesday they steadied near $104-105.
**Why it matters:** Oil near $100 keeps inflation high and was a central issue in the election.
**Sources:** [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil/news/599585), [Oil Monster](https://www.oilmonster.com/article/oil-rises-after-us-strike-on-irans-qeshm-island/8750), [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2026/11/03/asian-stocks-retreat-ahead-of-us-midterms-oil-steadies)

## 4. Tech sell-off deepens, with Seoul down 4% [Technology]

**What happened:** Asian shares retreated before the US vote, with MSCI's Asia-Pacific index ex-Japan down 0.9% and South Korea's Kospi falling 4% to 5,626.08 as tech losses mounted. Reports of the Nikkei's decline vary by source, from about 1.4% to more.
**Why it matters:** AI-heavy markets such as Korea and Taiwan are highly sensitive to chip stock sentiment.
**Sources:** [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2026/11/03/asian-stocks-retreat-ahead-of-us-midterms-oil-steadies), [WSB-TV/AP](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/business/asian-shares-are/TZN4ESJELQ3YBBHIWXGSEDVSVQ/), [AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/asian-trading-is-mixed-as-tech-sell-off-deepens-and-oil-prices-rebound/)

## 5. Russia-Ukraine-US talks set for Abu Dhabi [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** The US will host a second trilateral round between Ukraine and Russia in Abu Dhabi on 10-11 November, per the Kremlin and Reuters-based reports. Trump also said Ukraine had agreed not to hit Russian energy targets (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** It is the first formal negotiating round in months, though territory and security guarantees remain stumbling blocks.
**Sources:** [FDD](https://www.fdd.org/overnight-brief/november-4-2026/), [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/us-attempts-new-peace-push-first-russia-ukraine-talks-nine-months)

## 6. Congo's Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak passes 10,500 cases [Health]

**What happened:** UK monitoring data to 1 November show 10,532 confirmed cases and 5,126 deaths, making it the largest outbreak of this virus species and the second-largest Ebola outbreak ever. It remains a WHO emergency, with cases reported in neighbouring countries including Kenya.
**Why it matters:** High fatality and continued spread strain health systems and raise regional containment concerns.
**Sources:** [UK Health Security Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/outbreaks-under-monitoring-in-2026/outbreaks-under-monitoring-week-44-week-ending-1-november-2026) (single source, unconfirmed), [WHO](https://who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON618)

## 7. Fed holds with two members favouring a hike [Economy]

**What happened:** A report ahead of the vote said the Federal Reserve held rates at its latest meeting with two members voting for an increase, signalling policymakers are still weighing more tightening. Sources conflict on the Fed's exact policy rate and recent moves (unconfirmed).
**Why it matters:** Higher-for-longer US rates affect borrowing costs, the dollar and Hong Kong's currency peg.
**Sources:** [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/fed-holds-rates-bank-england-decision-1828777)
