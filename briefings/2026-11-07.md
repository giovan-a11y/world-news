# World Top 10 - Saturday, 07 November 2026

**Today in one line:** A blowout US jobs report, a Bank of England rate rise and fresh US strikes near Iran headlined a Friday that followed the week's AI-stock rout and Democratic midterm sweep.

## 1. US strikes Iran's Qeshm Island as Trump says talks are "progressing very well" [Geopolitics]

**What happened:** Trump confirmed limited US strikes that destroyed Iranian air-defence artillery on Qeshm Island, in the Strait of Hormuz, calling them minor. Earlier on 6 November he said talks with Iranian representatives were going very well, while the IRGC Navy said Hormuz would stay closed until Iran's conditions are met. Qatar is pushing an interim deal to halt escalation, and dates for some Qeshm strikes differ across outlets.
**Why it matters:** The simultaneous strikes and diplomacy show how fragile the Gulf standoff is for global energy supply.
**Sources:** [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/trump-confirms-new-us-strikes-on-irans-qeshm-island), [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3373633/trump-confirms-fresh-iran-strikes-days-after-voters-punish-war-polls), [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-live-blog-trump-middle-east/33803414.html)

## 2. US adds 165,000 jobs, well above forecasts [Economy]

**What happened:** October payrolls rose 165,000 versus roughly 65,000 expected, and unemployment dipped from 4.2% to 4.1%. The dollar climbed and traders cut bets on near-term rate relief. The S&P 500 closed at 7,376.86 (+0.31%), the Nasdaq at 24,579.42 (+1.09%) and the Dow at 51,285.73 (-0.46%).
**Why it matters:** A hot labour market, with war-driven inflation, makes Fed easing unlikely and keeps borrowing costs high.
**Sources:** [Hargreaves Lansdown](https://www.hl.co.uk/shares/stock-market-news/market-reports/us-close-tech-stocks-rebound-after-sell-off,-dow-slips-after-jobs-report), [EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/U-S-Adds-165-000-Jobs-in-October-as-Wall-Street-Ends-Week-Lower-1772683)

## 3. Bank of England raises rates to 4% [Economy]

**What happened:** The BoE lifted Bank Rate by 25 basis points to 4.00%, its first hike since 2023, citing energy-driven inflation. Staff project inflation near 5% by mid-2027; reports differ on the vote (most say 8-1, one says 9-0). Sterling barely reacted.
**Why it matters:** It shows major central banks tightening again because of the Middle East energy shock.
**Sources:** [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-kingdom/interest-rate/news/598618), [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/53075/business/boe-lifts-rate-for-first-time-since-2023-to-curb-prices/), [BabyPips](https://www.babypips.com/analysis/headline-boe-hiked-rates-why-gbp-just-shrugged-2026-11-06)

## 4. Tech rebounds after Thursday's AI sell-off [Technology]

**What happened:** Chip and AI stocks recovered on Friday after a Thursday rout that reports put at over 5% for the Nasdaq, driven by fears a Democratic Congress will regulate AI and by valuation worries. Sources disagree on the size of Thursday's fall. For the week the Nasdaq lost 3.4%.
**Why it matters:** AI shares carry huge weight in US indexes, so regulatory risk now moves broad markets.
**Sources:** [Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824763), [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3025392/stock-market-news-for-nov-6-2026)

## 5. Democrats' Congress sweep reshapes Washington [Politics]

**What happened:** With both chambers flipped, Hakeem Jeffries is set to be Speaker and House Democrats are on track for their largest majority in nearly two decades. Trump says he will veto Democratic bills; Jeffries urged him to end the Iran war before the new Congress starts. A White House request for $67 billion in Iran war funding is pending.
**Why it matters:** The result limits Trump's ability to fund the war or pass his agenda.
**Sources:** [NBC San Diego](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/elections/election-day-after-2026-midterms-congress-results-live-updates/4096585/), [Argus Media](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2901877-trump-faces-new-limits-after-midterms-blowout)

## 6. Judge halts Trump's planned triumphal arch for 14 days [Politics]

**What happened:** Judge Tanya Chutkan issued a temporary order after a federal planning commission approved the arch near Arlington Memorial Circle. She found construction likely to harm Washington and Virginia and said the arch is "a tribute to Donald Trump."
**Why it matters:** It tests the limits of executive power over federal land and building projects.
**Sources:** [WLOX](https://www.wlox.com/2026/11/06/federal-judge-orders-14-day-delay-any-work-beginning-trumps-monumental-arch/), [NPR via WWNO](https://www.wwno.org/npr-news/2026-11-05/a-judge-blocked-construction-of-trumps-arch-at-the-11th-hour-whats-next)

## 7. WHO: Ebola in DR Congo passes 10,600 cases [Health]

**What happened:** WHO's 6 November bulletin reported 10,668 confirmed cases and 5,198 deaths of the Bundibugyo strain, with 1,463 new cases in 21 days, mostly in Ituri and North Kivu. Cases have reached Uganda, Kenya and Nigeria; Kenya has begun a 42-day countdown toward being declared Ebola-free.
**Why it matters:** It is the second-largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded and is still growing in the east.
**Sources:** [WHO](https://who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON618)

**Why it matters:** Hong Kong stocks are swinging sharply with US tech and rate news.
**Sources:** [Xinhua (6 Nov)](https://www.xinhuanet.com/20261106/e84e9fa5dde846c9b3eacfaa9ca7e983/c.html), [Xinhua (5 Nov)](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20261105/30dca13060c845e8aeb2926ad0009301/c.html)
